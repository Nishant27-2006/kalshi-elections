# Race: 2026 Montana Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open Republican-held seat (Steve Daines not running): US Attorney Kurt Alme (R) vs independent Seth Bodnar vs Democrat Alani Bankhead, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Daines had announced he would seek reelection but withdrew minutes before the filing deadline and endorsed Kurt Alme, who filed the same day. (as_of: 2026-03-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Montana)
- Seth Bodnar, former University of Montana president, launched an independent campaign with support from former Democratic Sen. Jon Tester. (as_of: 2026-03-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Montana)
- In the June 2 primaries, Alme won about 76% as the only major Republican. Air Force officer Alani Bankhead won the Democratic nomination with about 43.6% over Reilly Neill and Michael Black Wolf. Kyle Austin won the Libertarian primary. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Montana)
- Fundraising per FEC data compiled by Wikipedia, as of June 30, 2026: Bodnar raised $3.24M with $0.98M on hand. Alme raised $1.98M with $1.45M on hand. Bankhead raised $0.06M. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Montana)
- Inside Elections moved Montana from Likely Republican to Solid Republican. (as_of: 2026-06-25; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- Peak Insights, Aug 23–25, 600 LV: Alme 44, Bodnar 22, Bankhead 19, Austin 2. (as_of: 2026-09-04; source: https://cdn.sanity.io/files/ifn0l6bs/production/72ac898bdf17c5121988f619a81d80fde4e18325.pdf)
- Rutgers-Eagleton poll, Aug 27–Sep 7, 845 RV: Alme 38, Bodnar 30, Bankhead 16, Libertarian Austin 5. Democrats in the poll favored the independent over their own nominee. (as_of: 2026-09-11; source: https://montanafreepress.org/2026/09/11/alme-holds-an-8-point-lead-over-bodnar-in-senate-race/)
- Quantus Insights (R), Sep 14–15, 625 LV: Alme 44, Bodnar 24, Bankhead 23. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: https://quantusinsights.org/f/latest-survey-alme-leads-as-montana%E2%80%99s-senate-opposition-splits)
- Cook Political Report lists Montana (open) as Solid Republican. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rated Montana Safe R as of Oct 6. DDHQ and FiftyPlusOne had Likely R as of Oct 7. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Montana)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Montana 58.39% to 38.46% (R+19.93). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Trump won Montana 56.92% to 40.55% (R+16.37). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Daines (R) 55.01%, Steve Bullock (D) 44.99%. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_Montana)
- This is Montana's first open Senate election since 1976. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Montana)
- Kalshi SENATEMT-26 on Oct 9: R (Alme) bid/ask 0.926/0.942; IND (Bodnar) 0.056/0.072; D (Bankhead) 0.008/0.009. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEMT-26?with_nested_markets=true)

## Resolution notes

- A Bodnar win settles the IND market, not D: "If Seth Bodnar (as an Independent) is sworn in as a Senator of Montana for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEMT-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- R and D markets: "If a representative of the Republican [Democratic] party is sworn in as a Senator of Montana for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." All markets can resolve early on a media consensus projection. Expected expiration is 2027-01-04. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEMT-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- A Libertarian (Austin) win would not settle any of the three listed markets Yes. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEMT-26?with_nested_markets=true)
