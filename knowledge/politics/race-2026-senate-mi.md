# Race: 2026 Michigan Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open Democratic-held seat (Gary Peters retiring): Democrat Abdul El-Sayed vs Republican Mike Rogers, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Incumbent Gary Peters (D) is not running for a third term. This is the only open Democratic-held seat in a state Trump won in 2024. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Michigan)
- Abdul El-Sayed won the Aug 4 Democratic primary with 48.5% after a tight race with Rep. Haley Stevens. Mike Rogers was the only Republican to qualify for the primary ballot. (as_of: 2026-08-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Michigan)
- Bridge Michigan put El-Sayed's margin over Stevens at about 1 point. Mallory McMorrow had suspended but stayed on the ballot, and her roughly 4% was more than that margin. (as_of: 2026-08-05; source: https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/how-abdul-el-sayed-won-michigan-big-turnout-liberal-towns-walkup-votes/)
- The general-election ballot also lists Libertarian, Constitution, Green and Natural Law candidates. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Michigan)
- Rogers was the 2024 GOP nominee and lost to Elissa Slotkin by 0.3%. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- FEC totals through July 15, 2026 (pre-primary): El-Sayed raised $14.51M with $2.55M on hand. Rogers raised $10.90M with $4.47M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-23; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=MI&election_full=true)
- Fox News (Beacon/Shaw), Sep 24–28, 1,028 LV: El-Sayed 50, Rogers 49. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/fox-news-poll-michigan-voters-split-between-el-sayed-rogers)
- Trafalgar Group (R), Sep 28–30, 1,085 LV: El-Sayed 47, Rogers 45. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://www.thetrafalgargroup.org/news/mi-senate-general-1001/)
- CNN says a Marist poll of registered voters had El-Sayed up 51 to 44, while every September likely-voter poll showed no clear leader. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)

## Resolution notes

- SENATEMI-26 rules: "If a representative of the Republican [Democratic] party is sworn in as a Senator of Michigan for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEMI-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXMISENATE-26 is a person market: "If Abdul El-Sayed wins the 2026 Michigan Senate race, then the market resolves to Yes." The listed candidate must win as the nominee of the listed party; a candidate who fails to get the nomination resolves No. McMorrow and Stevens markets are still listed at about 0.01. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMISENATE-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Both events can resolve early once a consensus of media organizations projects the winner. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMISENATE-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXMIDTERMMOV-MISEND is a margin ladder: "If the Democratic Party wins the 2026 U.S. Senate election in Michigan by N percentage points or more, then the market resolves to Yes," with strikes from 1 to 15 in steps of 2. Margin is the Democrat's share minus the runner-up's, with no rounding and the lower bound inclusive. A Democratic loss gives a negative margin, so every strike resolves No. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMIDTERMMOV-MISEND?with_nested_markets=true)
