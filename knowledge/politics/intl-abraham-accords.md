# International: Abraham Accords Expansion

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: which countries normalize relations with Israel before 2027 (KXABRAHAMSA-27). Covers Colombia's restoration of ties, Kazakhstan and Somaliland, Saudi Arabia's stated conditions, the Syria and Lebanon tracks, and current pricing.

## Claims

- Colombian President Gustavo Petro announced on 2 May 2024 that Colombia would break diplomatic ties with Israel over the Gaza war. (as_of: 2024-05-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia%E2%80%93Israel_relations)
- Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman said the kingdom "will not establish diplomatic relations with Israel" without an independent Palestinian state with East Jerusalem as its capital. (as_of: 2024-09-18; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93Saudi_Arabia_relations)
- In July 2025 the Trump administration was reported to be seeking to expand the Accords to Syria, Lebanon and Saudi Arabia. A month later Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa said Syria would not join. (as_of: 2025-08-31; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Accords)
- Petro expelled all remaining Israeli diplomats on 1 October 2025 after Israel intercepted the Global Sumud Flotilla. (as_of: 2025-10-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia%E2%80%93Israel_relations)
- US and Israeli officials said on 6 November 2025 that Kazakhstan would join the Abraham Accords. Kazakhstan has had diplomatic relations with Israel since 1992, and the move was described as "largely symbolic." (as_of: 2025-11-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Accords)
- Israel recognized Somaliland on 26 December 2025, and Somaliland pledged to join the Accords. (as_of: 2025-12-26; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Accords)
- On 14 April 2026, Secretary of State Rubio hosted the first direct Israel-Lebanon peace talks in Washington, the first such talks since the failed 1983 May 17 Agreement. (as_of: 2026-04-14; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israel%E2%80%93Lebanon_peace_talks)
- In May 2026, Trump suggested that some of Israel's neighbors should be required to sign the Abraham Accords and recognize Israel as part of a peace deal in the Iran war. (as_of: 2026-05-31; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Accords)
- Under a US-brokered framework agreed on 26 June 2026, the IDF would withdraw from southern Lebanon and be replaced by Lebanese Armed Forces units that would oversee Hezbollah's disarmament. (as_of: 2026-06-26; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israel%E2%80%93Lebanon_peace_talks)
- Israeli FM Gideon Sa'ar attended the 7 August 2026 inauguration of Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella. (as_of: 2026-08-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia%E2%80%93Israel_relations)
- On 10 August 2026 Colombia's new government recognized Israeli sovereignty over the Golan Heights, the second country to do so. Petro called the restoration of ties an applause for "the Gaza genocide." (as_of: 2026-08-10; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia%E2%80%93Israel_relations)
- Kalshi's Colombia market (KXABRAHAMSA-27-JAN01-COLO) closed early on 17 August 2026 and settled Yes. (as_of: 2026-08-17; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXABRAHAMSA-27?with_nested_markets=true)
- Colombia and Israel began visa-free travel on 1 September 2026. On 22 September the ICJ confirmed that Colombia had withdrawn its support for South Africa's case against Israel. (as_of: 2026-09-22; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia%E2%80%93Israel_relations)

## Resolution notes

- Each KXABRAHAMSA-27 market resolves Yes if Israel and the named country normalize diplomatic relations before 1 Jan 2027. The agreement must be ratified before that date. Settlement sources are The New York Times and gov.il. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXABRAHAMSA-27?with_nested_markets=true)
- Markets close and expire early if the event occurs. The Colombia Yes shows that restoring previously severed ties was treated as normalization. (as_of: 2026-08-17; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXABRAHAMSA-27?with_nested_markets=true)
