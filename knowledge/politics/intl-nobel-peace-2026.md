# International: 2026 Nobel Peace Prize

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize (announcement timing, publicly confirmed nominees, the laureate) and how the Kalshi KXNOBELPEACE-26 event priced and settled.

## Claims

- Maria Corina Machado was awarded the 2025 Nobel Peace Prize "for her tireless work promoting democratic rights for the people of Venezuela." (as_of: 2025-10-10; source: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2025/summary/)
- The Norwegian Nobel Committee announced the 2026 Peace Prize in Oslo on 9 October 2026. The award ceremony is set for 10 December 2026. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nobel_Peace_Prize)
- NobelPrize.org listed the 2026 Nobel announcements as running 5-12 October 2026. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2026/summary/)
- 208 individuals and 79 organisations were nominated for the 2026 Peace Prize. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nobel_Peace_Prize)
- Publicly confirmed 2026 nominations included Donald Trump, nominated by Rep. Anna Paulina Luna for brokering the Israel-Hamas ceasefire and by Rep. Claudia Tenney for the Abraham Accords. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nobel_Peace_Prize)
- The Peace Research Institute Oslo nominated Mykola Kuleba together with Save the Children "for rescuing Ukrainian children." (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nobel_Peace_Prize)
- Other publicly confirmed 2026 nominees included Francesca Albanese, Volodymyr Zelenskyy, Yulia Navalnaya, Pope Leo XIV, Maia Sandu, Greta Thunberg, the ICC and ICJ, and Sudan's Emergency Response Rooms. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nobel_Peace_Prize)
- The 2026 Nobel Peace Prize went to Navanethem "Navi" Pillay, unshared, "for her efforts to promote peace and international law." (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2026/summary/)
- According to the committee's press release, Pillay has served as a judge on the High Court in South Africa, the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda and the International Criminal Court. She was also UN High Commissioner for Human Rights and until recently chaired the UN Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2026/press-release/)
- The press release said Pillay now sits as an ICJ judge in the genocide case against Myanmar. It framed the award as a defence of international law at a time when judges are being sanctioned and their institutions attacked. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2026/press-release/)
- KXNOBELPEACE-26 listed 50 nominee markets and Pillay was not one of them. All 50 settled No, and trading closed at 09:08 UTC on 9 October 2026. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXNOBELPEACE-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- In the Kalshi previous_price field just before settlement, the highest-priced names were Sudan's Emergency Response Rooms (0.15), Mykola Kuleba / Save Ukraine (0.10), Save the Children (0.09), Yulia Navalnaya (0.06) and the ICJ (0.04). (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXNOBELPEACE-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- The Donald Trump market had the most volume in the event, about 3.06 million contracts, and last traded at 0.01. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXNOBELPEACE-26?with_nested_markets=true)

## Resolution notes

- Each KXNOBELPEACE-26 market resolves Yes only if its named person or organization wins the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize. The Nobel Foundation is the settlement source. A laureate who is not listed leaves every market at No. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXNOBELPEACE-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- The event is not mutually exclusive, so a shared prize can settle more than one market Yes. Some markets in the event carry KXNOBELPEACE-27-* tickers (e.g. ICC, Navalnaya, Pope Leo XIV) but belong to the 2026 event. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXNOBELPEACE-26?with_nested_markets=true)
