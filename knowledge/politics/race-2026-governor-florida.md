# Race: 2026 Florida Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open-seat Florida governor race (DeSantis term-limited): Republican Byron Donalds vs Democrat David Jolly, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Incumbent Ron DeSantis (R) is term-limited; the general election is November 3, 2026 and party primaries were held August 18, 2026. (as_of: 2026-08-18; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Florida_gubernatorial_election)
- Nominees are U.S. Rep. Byron Donalds (R) and former Rep. David Jolly (D), a former Republican; it is the second straight Florida governor race with an ex-Republican Democratic nominee. (as_of: 2026-08-18; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Florida_gubernatorial_election)
- Republicans have won every Florida governor election since 1998. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Florida_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: DeSantis (R) 59.4%, Charlie Crist (D) 40.0%, about R+19.4. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Florida by 13.10 points (Harris 42.99%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook PVI (2025): R+5. (as_of: 2025-03-06; source: https://www.cookpolitical.com/cook-pvi/2025-partisan-voting-index/state-map-and-list)
- Cook Political Report and Inside Elections both rate the race Lean R as of their Oct 1, 2026 updates. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rates the race Lean R (Oct 6, 2026 table); RealClearPolitics lists it as a Tossup (Oct 5, 2026). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Change Research (D), Sep 7–9, 1,107 LV: Jolly 47, Donalds 44. (as_of: 2026-09-10; source: https://changeresearch.com/research/2026/2026-09-10-florida-aapor-methodology-statement)
- St. Pete Polls, Sep 15–17, 913 LV: Donalds 44, Jolly 43. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: http://stpetepolls.org/files/StPetePolls_2026_State_Gov_September17_G7V29XU6.pdf)
- Stetson University CPOR, Sep 14–21, 830 Floridians (leaners included): Donalds 51, Jolly 39, 5% other, 5% undecided; Donalds was 47 in Stetson's spring survey. (as_of: 2026-09-23; source: https://www.stetson.edu/artsci/cpor/media/cpor-sept.-2026-poll-final.pdf)
- Change Research (D), Sep 25–27, 1,063 LV: Jolly 48, Donalds 46. (as_of: 2026-09-28; source: https://changeresearch.com/research/2026/2026-09-28-florida-aapor-methodology-statement)

- YouGov, Sep 28–Oct 5, 3,813 LV: Donalds 47, Jolly 45. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://d3nkl3psvxxpe9.cloudfront.net/documents/ttw_fl_20260921_lv.pdf)

- RealClearPolitics average (Sep 14–Oct 7 polls): Donalds 47.8, Jolly 42.5 (Donalds +5.3). (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/polls/governor/general/2026/florida/donalds-vs-jolly)

## Resolution notes

- GOVPARTYFL-26-D: "If a representative of the Democratic party is inaugurated as the governor of Florida pursuant to the 2026 election, then the market resolves to Yes." The -R contract is the same with Republican. It settles on the party of the person inaugurated, not on the named candidate. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/GOVPARTYFL-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Rules allow accelerated determination once a consensus of media organizations projects the winner; expected expiration is 2027-01-08 and the market can close early. Settlement source is the Florida Secretary of State. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/GOVPARTYFL-26?with_nested_markets=true)
