# Race: 2026 Alaska Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open-seat Alaska governor race (Dunleavy term-limited), decided by a four-candidate ranked-choice general on November 3, 2026: Democrat Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins and Republicans Bernadette Wilson, Dave Bronson and Treg Taylor.

## Claims

- Incumbent Mike Dunleavy (R) is term-limited. A nonpartisan top-four primary was held August 18, 2026, and the top four advance to a ranked-choice general election. (as_of: 2026-08-18; source: https://www.elections.alaska.gov/Core/primaryelectioninformation.php)
- Top-four primary results: Kreiss-Tomkins (D) 22.5%, Tom Begich (D) 20.8%, Bernadette Wilson (R) 10.0%, Dave Bronson (R) 8.1%, Treg Taylor (R) 7.3%, Click Bishop (R) 7.0%. (as_of: 2026-08-18; source: https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/08/18/us/elections/results-alaska-governor-primary.html)
- Begich (D) withdrew after the primary and Bishop (R) also withdrew. Rep. Nick Begich endorsed Wilson as the first-place Republican finisher. (as_of: 2026-08-31; source: https://www.adn.com/politics/2026/08/31/pressure-grows-for-a-consolidated-gop-field-in-alaska-governors-race-as-deadline-nears/)
- Lt. Gov. Nancy Dahlstrom initially ruled on Aug 31 that Taylor's financial disclosure did not substantially comply with the law, keeping him off the ballot. (as_of: 2026-08-31; source: https://www.adn.com/politics/2026/08/31/pressure-grows-for-a-consolidated-gop-field-in-alaska-governors-race-as-deadline-nears/)
- Dahlstrom reversed herself late on Sep 4 and reinstated Taylor, citing a Department of Law review that found potential discrepancies in financial statements across the field. (as_of: 2026-09-05; source: https://www.adn.com/politics/2026/09/04/treg-taylor-is-back-in-alaskas-race-for-governor-after-election-officials-reverse-decision/)
- The general-election ballot is Wilson (R), Taylor (R), Bronson (R) and Kreiss-Tomkins (D). Wilson asked Taylor and Bronson to suspend their campaigns; they urged Republicans to rank all three Republicans. (as_of: 2026-09-18; source: https://www.adn.com/politics/2026/09/18/wilson-again-calls-on-her-republican-opponents-to-drop-out-of-alaska-governors-race/)
- 2022 result: Dunleavy (R) 50.3%, Les Gara (D) 24.2%, Bill Walker (I) 20.7%; Dunleavy won outright on first choices. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Alaska by 13.13 points (Harris 41.41%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook and Inside Elections both rate the race a Tossup (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Lean D (flip) (Oct 6, 2026 table); Silver Bulletin has Likely D (flip). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- AARP poll (Fabrizio Ward/Impact Research), September, first choices: Kreiss-Tomkins 40, Wilson 20, Bronson 14, Taylor 7; Kreiss-Tomkins beat Wilson after ranked-choice tabulation. (as_of: 2026-09-18; source: https://www.adn.com/politics/2026/09/18/wilson-again-calls-on-her-republican-opponents-to-drop-out-of-alaska-governors-race/)
- co/efficient (R), Sep 14–17, 799 LV, first choices: Kreiss-Tomkins 43, Wilson 20, Bronson 17, Taylor 6, undecided 14. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: https://battlegrounds.coefficient.org/ak/)
- Cygnal (R), Sep 24–27, 502 LV, ±4.37, first choices: Kreiss-Tomkins 44, Wilson 24, Taylor 9, Bronson 8, undecided 15. The three Republicans combined for 41%, and Cygnal said undecideds lean right. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://www.cygn.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/AK_GOV_Memo.pdf)
- NYT/Siena, Sep 24–Oct 1, 504 LV: Kreiss-Tomkins 40, Wilson 20, Bronson 15, Taylor 11 before allocation; the final RCV round was Kreiss-Tomkins 52, Wilson 48. (as_of: 2026-10-03; source: https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/10/03/polls/times-siena-alaska-poll-toplines.html)
- Alaska Survey Research, Oct 1–3, 1,512 LV: first round Kreiss-Tomkins 48, Wilson 27, Bronson 15, Taylor 10; final round Kreiss-Tomkins 54, Wilson 46. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://x.com/The_Real_ASR/status/2107585990869348810)

## Resolution notes

- KXGOVAK-26 is a person market. Each contract resolves Yes "If [candidate] is elected the governor of Alaska pursuant to the election in 2026" (e.g. KXGOVAK-26-JKRE for Kreiss-Tomkins and -BWIL for Wilson). It keys on being elected after RCV tabulation, not on party. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXGOVAK-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Settlement sources are a list of media outlets (NYT, AP, Reuters, Axios, Politico, WaPo, WSJ, CNN, Fox News and others); accelerated determination is allowed after a media-consensus projection. Expected expiration is 2026-12-03, earlier than the GOVPARTY markets. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXGOVAK-26?with_nested_markets=true)
