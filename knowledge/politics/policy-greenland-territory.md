# Policy: Greenland and Territorial Expansion

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: dated events in the Trump administration's push to acquire Greenland, the September 2026 U.S.-Denmark-Greenland security agreement, other "51st state" statements, and current pricing.

## Claims

- Vice President JD Vance visited Pituffik Space Base without an invitation in March 2025 and criticized Denmark's treatment of Greenlanders. (as_of: 2025-03-31; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)
- Trump appointed Louisiana Gov. Jeff Landry special envoy to Greenland in December 2025; Landry said he would work to make Greenland part of the United States. (as_of: 2025-12-31; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)
- After the U.S. intervention in Venezuela, Stephen Miller said on Jan. 3, 2026 that the U.S. had the right to take Greenland. (as_of: 2026-01-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)
- On Jan. 13, 2026 Trump threatened military action and said there was "no going back" on annexation, calling Greenland "imperative for national and world security." (as_of: 2026-01-13; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)
- On Jan. 18, 2026 Trump threatened 10% tariffs from Feb. 1 on Denmark, Norway, Sweden, France, Germany, the UK, the Netherlands and Finland, rising to 25% on June 1 unless Denmark ceded Greenland. (as_of: 2026-01-18; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)
- At Davos on Jan. 21, 2026 Trump announced the "framework of a future deal" on Greenland with NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte and withdrew the tariff threats. (as_of: 2026-01-21; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)
- Trump twice called for Venezuela to eventually become the 51st state in March 2026, after Venezuela's World Baseball Classic wins. (as_of: 2026-03-31; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_expansionism_under_Donald_Trump)
- At the July 7, 2026 NATO summit in Ankara, Trump repeated that the U.S. should control Greenland. (as_of: 2026-07-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)
- Trump announced on Sep. 18, 2026 that the U.S., Denmark and Greenland had reached a security agreement expanding the U.S. military footprint. (as_of: 2026-09-18; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)
- The security agreement was signed Sep. 22, 2026 at the UN General Assembly. It gives the U.S. permanent rights to install military bases and use Greenland's airspace and bars non-NATO states from a military presence there. (as_of: 2026-09-22; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)
- Danish and Greenlandic officials said the Sep. 22, 2026 deal "does not transfer sovereignty of Greenland to the US" and affirms Danish sovereignty and Greenland's right to self-determination; Trump said it gives the U.S. "permanent control" over security. (as_of: 2026-09-22; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis)

## Resolution notes

- KXGREENLAND-29 needs the United States to purchase at least part of Greenland from Denmark before Jan. 20, 2029. A security or basing agreement is not a purchase. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXGREENLAND-29?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXGREENTERRITORY-29 needs part of Greenland to come under formal U.S. governance or jurisdiction (state, territory or other U.S. classification). A joint U.S.-controlling-entity announcement that it will happen also counts. Leasing territory, such as for a military base, does not. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXGREENTERRITORY-29?with_nested_markets=true)
