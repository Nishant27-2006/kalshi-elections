# Race: 2026 Ohio Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open-seat Ohio governor race (DeWine term-limited): Republican Vivek Ramaswamy vs Democrat Amy Acton, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Incumbent Mike DeWine (R) is term-limited; primaries were held May 5, 2026, with Ramaswamy facing minimal opposition and Acton unopposed. (as_of: 2026-05-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Ohio_gubernatorial_election)
- Democrats have not won an Ohio governor race since 2006; the winner is inaugurated January 11, 2027. (as_of: 2026-05-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Ohio_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: DeWine (R) 62.4%, Nan Whaley (D) 37.4%, about R+25. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Ohio by 11.21 points (Harris 43.93%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Campaign finance as of June 5, 2026: Ramaswamy $30.7M cash on hand vs Acton $8.1M (Ohio SOS). (as_of: 2026-06-05; source: https://data.ohiosos.gov/portal/campaign-finance)
- On Sep 2, Ramaswamy accepted CNN and WLWT debate invitations; Acton's campaign declined, citing his ads, so no nominee debates were scheduled. (as_of: 2026-09-02; source: https://www.dispatch.com/story/news/state/2026/09/02/ohio-governor-candidates-may-not-debate-find-out-why/91585349007/)
- Acton was attacked by an armed individual at a Canfield Fair campaign event on Sep 6; the suspect was apprehended. (as_of: 2026-09-06; source: https://www.wlwt.com/article/amy-acton-attacked-at-campaign-event-near-youngstown/73625988)
- Cook and Inside Elections both rate the race a Tossup (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it a Tossup (Oct 6, 2026 table); Decision Desk HQ rated it Lean D flip (Oct 8). (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
