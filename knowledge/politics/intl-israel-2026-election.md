# International: Israel 2026 Knesset Election

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Covers the 27 October 2026 Knesset election: the date, seat polls by date, bloc arithmetic toward 61 seats, PM head-to-heads, and Kalshi pricing for who becomes PM.

## Claims

- Coalition head Ofir Katz said the election would stay on its legally set date of Oct 27, 2026, rather than move earlier. (as_of: 2026-07-12; source: https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/israels-election-held-october-27-coalition-head/)
- The Knesset formally dissolved on 17 July 2026. The electoral threshold is 3.25%. (as_of: 2026-07-17; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)
- The main lists are Likud (Netanyahu), Yashar (Gadi Eisenkot) and Together/B'Yachad (Naftali Bennett, running jointly with Yair Lapid's Yesh Atid), along with Shas, Blue and White, Religious Zionist Party–Zehut, the Joint List and Ra'am. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)
- Globes' poll of polls had Likud 24, Yashar 23, Beyachad 12, Democrats 10, Yisrael Beitenu 8, UTJ 8, Shas 8, Joint Arab List 8, Otzma Yehudit 7, Ra'am 5, Religious Zionist 5 and Ofer Winter's People of Israel 4. On this count the coalition led the opposition for the first time in weeks. (as_of: 2026-09-06; source: https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001554485)
- Maariv/Lazar (with Panel4All) had Yashar 22, Likud 18, B'Yachad 13, Democrats 11, Yisrael Beytenu 9, Otzma Yehudit 9, Joint Arab List 7, UTJ 6, Shas 6, Religious Zionism-Zehut 6, Ra'am 5, People of Israel 4 and Reservists 4. That gives the coalition bloc 49 and the Zionist opposition 55. (as_of: 2026-09-25; source: https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-909659)
- In a late-September roundup, Channel 14 had Likud 32 and Yashar 22, with the right-wing bloc at 63, the left-wing bloc at 45 and Arab parties at 12. (as_of: 2026-09-27; source: https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-909804)
- The same roundup had N12 at Yashar 23, Likud 20, B'Yachad 13, with the opposition at 66 (54 Zionist plus 12 Arab) and the coalition at 50. Channel 13 had Yashar 22 and Likud 18, with the opposition at 52, the coalition at 51 and Arab parties at 13. (as_of: 2026-09-27; source: https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-909804)
- Jerusalem Post described the five late-September seat polls as giving "very different pictures" whose average is "an exceptionally close contest at the top." A majority takes 61 of 120 seats. (as_of: 2026-09-27; source: https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-909804)
- In Maariv's Sep 30-Oct 1 PM head-to-head (LRI+P4A), Eisenkot led Netanyahu 47% to 41%. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leadership_opinion_polling_for_the_2026_Israeli_legislative_election)
- In Kan 11's Oct 4 head-to-heads (Kantar), Netanyahu led Eisenkot 41% to 38% and led Bennett 40% to 35%. (as_of: 2026-10-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leadership_opinion_polling_for_the_2026_Israeli_legislative_election)
- In N12's Oct 5 head-to-heads (Midgam), Eisenkot led Netanyahu 41% to 38%, and Netanyahu led Bennett 41% to 37%. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leadership_opinion_polling_for_the_2026_Israeli_legislative_election)
- In Channel 14's Oct 7 head-to-head, Netanyahu led Eisenkot 52% to 48%. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leadership_opinion_polling_for_the_2026_Israeli_legislative_election)
- i24NEWS/Direct Polls (n=518, ±4.3%) had Likud 26 (-2), Yashar 20 (-2), B'Yachad 11 (+2), Democrats 10, Otzma Yehudit 9, UTJ 8, Shas 7, Yisrael Beiteinu 7, Joint List 7, Religious Zionism 6, Zehut 6, Ra'am 5 and Amcha Yisrael 4. Blocs: coalition 60, opposition 48, Arab parties 12. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel-election-s-2026/artc-i24news-poll-netanyahu-and-eisenkot-fall-while-bennett-rises-full-poll)

## Resolution notes

- KXISRAELPM-26OCT27 pays the first person who, through government formation after the 2026 election, is formally sworn in, wins the Knesset's confidence and holds full (not caretaker) powers before Oct 27, 2027. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXISRAELPM-26OCT27?with_nested_markets=true)
- If no government forms by Oct 27, 2027, or new elections are called before one forms, all listed strikes resolve No. The event lists no "No one" strike. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXISRAELPM-26OCT27?with_nested_markets=true)
- A Netanyahu caretaker period does not count as being PM "as a result of government formation." Separately, under KXLEADERSOUT his staying on as caretaker means he has not left office. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXLEADERSOUT-27JAN01?with_nested_markets=true)
