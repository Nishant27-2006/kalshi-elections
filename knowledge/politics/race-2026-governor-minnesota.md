# Race: 2026 Minnesota Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Minnesota governor race: DFL Sen. Amy Klobuchar vs Republican House Speaker Lisa Demuth, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Gov. Tim Walz (DFL) had been running for a third term but withdrew in January 2026 amid a scandal over fraud in state-funded social services; Klobuchar then entered. (as_of: 2026-08-11; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Minnesota_gubernatorial_election)
- At the August 11 primaries, Klobuchar won the DFL nomination with 89.7%; Demuth won the GOP primary with 43.4% over Trump-endorsed Mike Lindell and party-endorsed Kendall Qualls. (as_of: 2026-08-11; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Minnesota_gubernatorial_election)
- With both nominees women, the winner will be Minnesota's first female governor. (as_of: 2026-08-17; source: https://minneapolimedia.town.news/g/coon-rapids-mn/n/384781/minneapolimedia-editorial-minnesota-will-elect-its-first-woman-governor)
- Republicans have not won a Minnesota governor race since 2006. (as_of: 2026-08-11; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Minnesota_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: Walz (DFL) 52.3%, Scott Jensen (R) 44.6%, about D+7.7. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Harris won Minnesota by 4.24 points (50.92% to 46.68%). (as_of: 2024-11-21; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook rates the race Solid D and Inside Elections Likely D (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Safe D (Oct 6, 2026 table); RealClearPolitics and Fox News list it as Likely D. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
