# International: Venezuela Leadership

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Covers who officially leads Venezuela after the US capture of Maduro: Maduro's legal status versus acting president Delcy Rodríguez, the election timetable, and how Kalshi settles "officially holds."

## Claims

- On January 3, 2026, US military strikes in Caracas and elsewhere in Venezuela ended with Nicolás Maduro captured and flown to New York City. (as_of: 2026-01-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delcy_Rodr%C3%ADguez)
- That same day, January 3, the Supreme Tribunal's Constitutional Chamber ordered Delcy Rodríguez to "assume and exercise, as acting president" the powers of the office. It treated Maduro's situation as a "temporary inability," not an absolute absence, and set no fixed term. (as_of: 2026-01-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delcy_Rodr%C3%ADguez)
- Rodríguez was sworn in as acting president on January 5, 2026, by her brother Jorge Rodríguez, president of the National Assembly. (as_of: 2026-01-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delcy_Rodr%C3%ADguez)
- Kalshi's 04/15/26 clarification said that if the market expired then, it would resolve Yes for Maduro. Its reasons: state institutions still recognized Maduro as constitutional president and Rodríguez as acting president, and the TSJ, National Assembly, Defense Ministry, Rodríguez herself and the presidency website all named Maduro. (as_of: 2026-04-15; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXVENEZUELALEADER-26DEC31?with_nested_markets=true)
- Chavista official sources gradually dropped the "(e)"/"encargada" (acting) label and began calling Rodríguez simply "presidenta." The US government still called her government "interim." (as_of: 2026-08-03; source: https://www.infobae.com/venezuela/2026/08/03/el-chavismo-dejo-de-identificar-a-delcy-rodriguez-como-encargada-en-medio-del-debate-sobre-la-legitimidad-del-ejercicio-de-la-presidencia-en-venezuela/)
- The constitution caps a temporary presidential absence at 90 days, extendable by 90 more with National Assembly approval. By early August Rodríguez had served more than 200 days without any declaration of Maduro's absolute absence; the TSJ instead used a "forced absence" doctrine. (as_of: 2026-08-03; source: https://www.infobae.com/venezuela/2026/08/03/el-chavismo-dejo-de-identificar-a-delcy-rodriguez-como-encargada-en-medio-del-debate-sobre-la-legitimidad-del-ejercicio-de-la-presidencia-en-venezuela/)
- Trump met Rodríguez on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly on Sep 22, their first meeting since Maduro's capture. (as_of: 2026-09-22; source: https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-meet-venezuelas-rodriguez-first-time-since-maduros-capture-2026-09-22/)
- In her UN General Assembly address, Rodríguez said "Elections will be held in Venezuela" but gave no date, saying the people would determine the best time. (as_of: 2026-09-24; source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/24/venezuelas-delcy-rodriguez-promises-elections-at-un-gives-no-date)
- Rodríguez came home from the US visit with no major deals and no election date. (as_of: 2026-09-26; source: https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-09-26/analysis-venezuela-president-returns-from-us-visit-with-no-deals-no-firm-election-date)
- As of early October, no election date had been set. Opposition leader María Corina Machado had been blocked from returning at least seven times, including three attempts from Panama in late September. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://www.latinpost.com/articles/167589/20261006/nine-months-after-maduros-capture-venezuela-still-has-no-elections-machado-pressing-army-act.htm)
- Secretary of State Rubio described the US sequence as stabilization, then economic recovery, with a democratic handover only after that. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://www.latinpost.com/articles/167589/20261006/nine-months-after-maduros-capture-venezuela-still-has-no-elections-machado-pressing-army-act.htm)
- A superseding US indictment added conspiracy-to-torture charges against Maduro and Cilia Flores. A judge denied Flores house arrest, and Maduro's trial is scheduled for June 2027. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://venezuelanalysis.com/news/venezuela-flores-denied-bail-as-justice-department-levies-new-maduro-charges/)

## Resolution notes

- KXVENEZUELALEADER-26DEC31 resolves to whoever "officially holds" the post of head of state at 10:00 AM ET on Dec 31, 2026. That means meeting any one of: (i) formally appointed and sworn in; (ii) listed as head of state in official directories or equivalent authoritative sources; (iii) actively serving with full authority, not in an acting or interim capacity. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXVENEZUELALEADER-26DEC31?with_nested_markets=true)
- Under the 04/15/26 clarification, Maduro's US detention is not disqualifying "absent a formal termination, resignation, removal, or expiration of his term." Rodríguez does not qualify while acting. A formal designation of a successor as constitutional president would change the outcome. (as_of: 2026-04-15; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXVENEZUELALEADER-26DEC31?with_nested_markets=true)
