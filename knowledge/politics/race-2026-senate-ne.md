# Race: 2026 Nebraska Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Pete Ricketts (R) seeks his first full term against independent Dan Osborn, with no Democrat on the ballot, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- The Nebraska Democratic Party endorsed independent Dan Osborn for the 2026 Senate race. (as_of: 2025-08-01; source: https://nebraska.tv/news/local/dan-osborn-endorsed-by-ne-democratic-party-for-2026-senate-race)
- Ricketts won the May 12 GOP primary with 81.5%. Party-backed Cindy Burbank won the Democratic primary with 89.2% over William Forbes. (as_of: 2026-05-12; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Nebraska)
- Burbank filed on July 17 to be removed from the general-election ballot so she could back Osborn. (as_of: 2026-07-17; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Nebraska)
- FEC totals: Ricketts raised $11.23M with $6.91M on hand through June 30. Osborn raised $5.75M with $2.42M on hand through Sept 30. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=NE&election_full=true)
- Impact Research (D), Aug 9–13, 600 LV: Ricketts 47, Osborn 47. (as_of: 2026-08-26; source: https://omaha.com/news/state-regional/government-politics/article_666083f7-4e07-45ae-a534-adbb42b64734.html)
- SurveyUSA, Sep 8–13, 503 LV: Osborn 46, Ricketts 42. (as_of: 2026-09-14; source: https://results.surveyusa.com/client/PollReport.aspx?g=d94236a7-5861-4b7f-9d81-d5a81ab786cc)
- GBAO (D) for Working Class Majority PAC, Sep 3–8, 800 LV: Osborn 47, Ricketts 46. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: https://static.washingtonsun.com/38/fa/94e79558460286cbf3847a27e4b5/working-class-majority-pac-september-survey-memo-091826.pdf)
- Inside Elections moved Nebraska from Likely Republican to Lean Republican. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- Per CNN, Osborn faces an uphill climb against Ricketts, and Nebraska is feeling the economic strain of the Iran war. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- YouGov, Sep 24–Oct 3, 1,809 LV, MoE ±3.3: Osborn 48, Ricketts 47. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.newsweek.com/democrats-flip-senate-seats-midterms-poll-12535052)
- Cook Political Report lists Nebraska (Ricketts) as Likely Republican. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rated Nebraska Likely R as of Oct 6. The Economist, Silver Bulletin and Split Ticket had Lean R as of Oct 7. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Nebraska)
- Last election for this seat (2024 special): Ricketts (R) 62.58%, Preston Love Jr. (D) 37.42%. (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Nebraska)
- In the 2024 regular Senate race, Deb Fischer (R) beat Osborn (I) 53.19% to 46.52%. (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Senate_election_in_Nebraska)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Nebraska statewide 59.32% to 38.86% (R+20.46). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Trump won Nebraska statewide 58.22% to 39.17% (R+19.06). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Kalshi SENATENE-26 on Oct 9: R (Ricketts) bid/ask 0.73/0.74; DOSB (Osborn, independent) 0.27/0.28; D (Burbank) 0.000/0.002. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATENE-26?with_nested_markets=true)
