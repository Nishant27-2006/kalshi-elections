# International: Iran Markets

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Covers the 2026 US-Israel war with Iran and the Kalshi markets tied to it: a US-Iran nuclear deal, Reza Pahlavi (visit, leadership, US recognition), and Strait of Hormuz traffic returning to normal.

## Claims

- The US and Israel struck Iran on Feb 28, 2026. Iranian state media confirmed that Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei had been killed. (as_of: 2026-02-28; source: https://apnews.com/live/live-updates-israel-iran-february-28-2026)
- The IRGC announced the Strait of Hormuz closed on March 2, 2026. Traffic fell from about 100 vessels a day before the war to about five a day. (as_of: 2026-08-27; source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/27/how-a-95-percent-drop-in-hormuz-traffic-changed-global-shipping)
- The US imposed a naval blockade on Iranian ports in April. A June 17 interim memorandum of understanding raised Hormuz traffic to about 20 vessels a day. After the US resumed the blockade on July 14, traffic fell back to about five a day. (as_of: 2026-08-27; source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/27/how-a-95-percent-drop-in-hormuz-traffic-changed-global-shipping)
- Kalshi's 06/17/26 clarification said the US-Iran MOU then reportedly in effect does not qualify as a "new Iran-US nuclear deal." It freezes the program at an unspecified status quo and defers disposal of the enriched-uranium stockpile to a later agreement. (as_of: 2026-06-17; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXUSAIRANAGREEMENT-27?with_nested_markets=true)
- Six months into the war, Mojtaba Khamenei, badly wounded in the opening strikes, held the supreme leader post but had not been seen or heard by Iranians. (as_of: 2026-08-28; source: https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-08-28/irans-disfigured-supreme-leader-remains-invisible-six-months-into-an-existential-war)
- A Reuters investigation described Reza Pahlavi's bid to lead Iran as "faltering." (as_of: 2026-07-28; source: https://www.reuters.com/investigations/regime-change-inside-reza-pahlavis-faltering-bid-lead-iran-2026-07-27/)
- Trump said Pahlavi seemed "very nice" but questioned whether he could rally enough support inside Iran to take power. Contacts with Pahlavi's side went through envoy Steve Witkoff, and no formal US recognition is recorded. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reza_Pahlavi,_Crown_Prince_of_Iran)
- Daily Hormuz transits stayed in single digits in mid-September (four on one Tuesday), against a pre-war 10-day average of 18 in that dataset. (as_of: 2026-09-16; source: https://www.marinelink.com/news/strait-hormuz-traffic-falls-four-ship-542975)
- VP Vance said Iran must make a "meaningful" cut in enrichment ("why do you need 60 percent enriched fuel?"), a softer line than full abandonment. The same day, Iranian President Pezeshkian called talks "futile." (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/7/us-sets-new-demands-for-iran-deal-what-are-they)
- Trump said the US would not attack Iran before the Nov 3 midterms, that "productive" talks were under way, and that the blockade of Iranian ports would stay. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610086381)
- Israel's Channel 12 reported that US forces were told to prepare for a possible large-scale attack on Iran within three weeks. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610086381)
- About 7-8 vessels transited Hormuz on Oct 6, down from 14 on Oct 5 and more than 20 a day earlier in October (Kpler/LSEG). There were 10 incidents in the strait from Oct 1 to 8. ## Resolution notes

- A KXUSAIRANAGREEMENT "new Iran-US nuclear deal" is a formal written agreement that (1) imposes verifiable limits on Iran's nuclear program and (2) lifts, suspends or modifies at least one US sanction. Both governments publicly agreeing to the terms is enough even before signing (04/19/26 clarification). (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXUSAIRANAGREEMENT-27?with_nested_markets=true)
- Per the 06/02/26 clarification, a bare pledge not to pursue nuclear weapons is not "verifiable." A commitment to stop or suspend enrichment, or to remove or cap the stockpile in a confirmable way, does count. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXUSAIRANAGREEMENT-27?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXHORMUZNORM strikes resolve Yes if the 7-day moving average of Hormuz transit calls on IMF PortWatch is above 60 before the strike date. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXHORMUZNORM-26MAR17?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXPAHLAVIVISITA requires Pahlavi to be physically present inside Iran's borders. Overflights, airside layovers, remote appearances and announced plans do not count. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXPAHLAVIVISITA?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXRECOGPERSONIRAN-26 requires official US action recognizing Reza Pahlavi by name as Iran's rightful or legitimate leader. KXPAHLAVIHEAD-27JAN requires him to be appointed, elected, named, designated or to succeed as head of state or government before Jan 1, 2027. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXRECOGPERSONIRAN-26?with_nested_markets=true)
