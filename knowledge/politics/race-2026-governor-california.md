# Race: 2026 California Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open-seat California governor race (Newsom term-limited): top-two general between Democrat Xavier Becerra and Republican Steve Hilton, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Gavin Newsom (D) is term-limited; the top-two primary was held June 2, 2026 with 61 candidates on the ballot. (as_of: 2026-03-26; source: https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-primary/cert-list-candidates.pdf)
- Top-two system: the two highest finishers advance regardless of party, so the November ballot is Becerra (D) vs Hilton (R). (as_of: 2026-07-10; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_California_gubernatorial_election)
- Certified primary result: Becerra 27.98% (2,591,857), Hilton 24.59%, Tom Steyer (D) 22.79%, Chad Bianco (R) 10.17%, Katie Porter (D) 4.36%, Matt Mahan (D) 3.54%. (as_of: 2026-07-10; source: https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/sov/2026-primary/sov/complete-sov.pdf)
- Final pre-primary polling had Becerra, Hilton and Steyer close for the two slots; e.g. Emerson May 27–28, 1,000 LV: Becerra 28, Hilton 23, Steyer 23. (as_of: 2026-05-30; source: https://emersoncollegepolling.com/california-2026-poll-becerra-leads-steyer-and-hilton-toss-up-for-second-spot/)
- Republicans have not won a California governor race since 2006. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_California_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: Newsom (D) 59.2%, Brian Dahle (R) 40.8%, about D+18.4. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Harris won California by 20.14 points (58.47% to 38.33%). (as_of: 2024-12-13; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook and Inside Elections both rate the race Solid D (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Safe D (Oct 6, 2026 table). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Berkeley IGS, first post-primary poll: Becerra 52, Hilton 31, 17% undecided (registered voters). (as_of: 2026-06-11; source: https://www.nbcsandiego.com/news/local/poll-california-governor-steve-hilton-xavier-becerra/4035718/)
- UC Berkeley IGS/L.A. Times: Becerra 55, Hilton 37, 7% undecided; Trump has endorsed Hilton. (as_of: 2026-08-14; source: https://abc7.com/post/xavier-becerra-maintains-commanding-lead-over-steve-hilton-california-governor-race-poll-shows/19674942/)
- Kalshi GOVPARTYCA-26 (party market): Democratic (Becerra) contract 96.3¢ bid / 96.7¢ ask; Republican (Hilton) 3.2¢ / 4.1¢. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/GOVPARTYCA-26?with_nested_markets=true)
