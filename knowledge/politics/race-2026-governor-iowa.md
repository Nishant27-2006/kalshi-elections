# Race: 2026 Iowa Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open-seat Iowa governor race (Reynolds not running): Democrat Rob Sand vs Republican Zach Lahn, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Gov. Kim Reynolds (R) is not seeking a third term; it is Iowa's first open governor race since 2006, which was also the last one Democrats won. (as_of: 2026-06-03; source: https://smartpolitics.lib.umn.edu/2026/06/03/could-iowa-democrats-win-2026s-gubernatorial-and-us-senate-races/)
- In the June 2 GOP primary, Zach Lahn won in an upset with 37.65% over Trump-endorsed Rep. Randy Feenstra; State Auditor Rob Sand was the only Democrat on the ballot. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iowa_gubernatorial_election)
- Lahn has faced residency questions: the Des Moines Register reported he lived in Kansas until 2023, and Sand called him a "part-time Iowan". (as_of: 2026-06-19; source: https://who13.com/news/politics/iowa-politics/lahn-defends-iowa-residency-amid-campaign-criticism/)
- The Des Moines Register reported a Black Hawk County Sheriff's investigation of Lahn's running mate, Derek Wulf, over theft and fraud allegations. (as_of: 2026-09-04; source: https://www.desmoinesregister.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/09/04/republican-derek-wulf-finances-fraud-iowa-election-zach-lahn/91251482007/)
- 2022 result: Reynolds (R) 58.0%, Deidre DeJear (D) 39.5%, about R+18.5. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Iowa by 13.21 points (Harris 42.52%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook rates the race Lean D (flip) and Inside Elections Tilt D (flip) as of Oct 1, 2026. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Lean D (flip) (Oct 6, 2026 table); Silver Bulletin had it at Solid D (flip) as of Oct 7. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iowa_gubernatorial_election)
- Fox News (Beacon/Shaw), Sep 24–28: Sand 53, Lahn 44 among 1,008 LV (±3); the Senate race in the same poll was Turek (D) 49, Hinson (R) 47. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/fox-news-poll-turek-hinson-close-iowa-senate-race)
- NYT/Siena, Sep 22–Oct 1, 606 LV: Sand 52, Lahn 41. (as_of: 2026-10-03; source: https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/10/03/polls/times-siena-iowa-poll-toplines.html)
- Suffolk, Oct 1–4, 500 LV: Sand 55, Lahn 43. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.suffolk.edu/-/media/suffolk/documents/academics/research-at-suffolk/suprc/polls/other-states/2026/10_7_2026_iowa_statewide_marginals.pdf)

- The first general-election debate, hosted by KWQC and St. Ambrose University, was held Oct 6. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.kcrg.com/2026/10/07/watch-full-iowa-governors-debate/)
- RealClearPolitics average (Aug 31–Oct 5 polls): Sand 51.0, Lahn 42.7 (Sand +8.3). (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/polls/governor/general/2026/iowa/lahn-vs-sand)
