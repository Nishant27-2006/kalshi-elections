# Race: 2026 South Dakota Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Mike Rounds (R) seeks a third term against independent Brian Bengs, November 3, 2026. The Democratic nominee withdrew.

## Claims

- Brian Bengs, the 2022 Democratic Senate nominee, launched an independent bid for the seat. (as_of: 2025-04-30; source: https://www.argusleader.com/story/news/politics/2025/04/30/brian-bengs-independent-bid-for-mike-rounds-senate-seat-2026-election/83367276007/)
- Rounds won the June 2 Republican primary with 75.8% (101,284 votes) over Justin McNeal. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_South_Dakota)
- Former state trooper Julian Beaudion won the Democratic nomination unopposed. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_South_Dakota)
- Beaudion withdrew on Aug 4, clearing the way for Bengs. (as_of: 2026-08-04; source: https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/08/04/democrat-exits-senate-race-south-dakota-clearing-way-independent/)
- With no Democrat on the ballot, this is the second South Dakota Senate race without a Democratic candidate; the first was 2010. (as_of: 2026-08-05; source: https://smartpolitics.lib.umn.edu/2026/08/05/a-brief-history-of-us-senate-elections-without-democrats-on-the-ballot/)
- FEC: Rounds had raised $4.51M with $2.74M cash on hand as of June 2, 2026. Bengs had raised $0.78M with $51K on hand as of June 18. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_South_Dakota)

## Resolution notes

- SENATESD-26-R: "If a representative of the Republican party is sworn in as a Senator of South Dakota for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." The D contract uses the same wording for the Democratic party. With no Democrat on the ballot, it is close to worthless. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATESD-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Bengs has his own contract, SENATESD-26-BBEN: "If Brian Bengs is sworn in as a Senator of South Dakota for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." All contracts can be settled early once a consensus of media organizations projects the winner.
