# Race: 2026 Pennsylvania Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Democratic incumbent Josh Shapiro seeks a second term against Republican State Treasurer Stacy Garrity, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Shapiro (D) is seeking a second term; Shapiro and Garrity were both unopposed in the May 19, 2026 primaries. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pennsylvania_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 nominee Doug Mastriano endorsed Garrity. (as_of: 2026-07-01; source: https://www.abc27.com/pennsylvania-politics/mastriano-backs-garrity-says-he-didnt-make-deal-for-ambassadorship/)
- Data centers are a live issue: 64% of likely voters said AI data centers in Pennsylvania will do more harm than good, and 24% said more good than harm (Quinnipiac, Oct 1–5). (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://poll.qu.edu/images/polling/pa/pa10072026_pgrx47.pdf)
- 2022 result: Shapiro (D) 56.5%, Mastriano (R) 41.7%, about D+14.8. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Pennsylvania by 1.71 points (Harris 48.66%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook and Inside Elections both rate the race Solid D (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Safe D (Oct 6, 2026 table). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Franklin & Marshall, Aug 17–23, 501 RV: Shapiro 50, Garrity 25. (as_of: 2026-08-27; source: https://www.fandmpoll.org/franklin-marshall-college-poll-release-august-2026/)
- NYT/Inquirer/Siena, Sep 15–22, 615 LV: Shapiro 58, Garrity 38. (as_of: 2026-09-24; source: https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/09/24/polls/times-inquirer-siena-pa-poll-lv-crosstabs.html)
- Susquehanna (R), Sep 22–30, 700 LV: Shapiro 52, Garrity 31. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://x.com/SusquehannaPR/status/2107848814992449714)
- Quinnipiac, Oct 1–5, 1,159 LV, ±3.9: Shapiro 59, Garrity 34, Libertarian Ken Krawchuk 2, 5% no choice; independents favor Shapiro 62–23. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://poll.qu.edu/images/polling/pa/pa10072026_pgrx47.pdf)
- Quinnipiac: Shapiro job approval 63/30 and favorability 55/29; Garrity favorability 22/32, with 44% saying they haven't heard enough about her. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://poll.qu.edu/images/polling/pa/pa10072026_pgrx47.pdf)
- RealClearPolitics average (Sep 15–Oct 5 polls): Shapiro 56.0, Garrity 32.8 (Shapiro +23.2). (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/polls/governor/general/2026/pennsylvania/shapiro-vs-garrity)
