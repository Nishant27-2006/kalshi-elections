# Chamber: 2026 House Control

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: who controls the House after the 2026 midterms and the final party seat counts. Covers composition, ratings tallies, forecast models, retirements and Kalshi prices.

## Claims

- Republicans held a 218-214 House majority with just under a month to go before Election Day. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.npr.org/2026/10/08/nx-s1-5993809/2026-house-map-democrats-flip-republicans-tossup-triage)
- Pre-election composition is 218 Republicans, 214 Democrats and 1 independent, Kevin Kiley, who was re-elected as a Republican in 2024 and caucuses with Republicans. A majority is 218, and Democrats need a net gain of 4. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections)
- In the 2024 House elections Republicans won 220 seats with 49.8% of the national House vote and Democrats won 215 seats with 47.2%. (as_of: 2024-12-31; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections)
- 60 representatives and 2 non-voting delegates (26 Democrats, 36 Republicans) are retiring, 29 of them (9 D, 20 R) to run for other offices. That is the second-most retirements in one cycle after 1992, which had 65. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections)
- Axios reported that House retirements were tied for the most this century. (as_of: 2026-03-02; source: https://www.axios.com/2026/03/02/members-of-congress-retirements-record-high)
- House primaries ran from March 3 to September 15, 2026. (as_of: 2026-09-15; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections)
- Cook Political Report's first 2026 House ratings, from February 2025, listed 39 competitive races with 18 toss-ups. Cook now lists 43 competitive districts, including 22 toss-ups. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.npr.org/2026/10/08/nx-s1-5993809/2026-house-map-democrats-flip-republicans-tossup-triage)
- Cook ratings as of Sep 25, 2026: 22 Toss-up (16 R-held, 5 D-held, plus the new FL-22), 11 Lean D, 10 Lean R, 12 Likely D, 19 Likely R. (as_of: 2026-09-25; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)
- Cook's 22 toss-ups as of Sep 25, 2026 are AZ-6, CA-22, CO-8, FL-14, FL-22, FL-25, IA-1, IA-3, MI-7, MI-10, NY-17, NC-11, OH-7, OH-9, PA-7, PA-8, PA-10, TX-15, TX-34, VA-2, WA-3 and WI-3. (as_of: 2026-09-25; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)
- Inside Elections ratings as of Oct 1, 2026: 16 Toss-up (12 R-held, 4 D-held), 10 Tilt D, 8 Tilt R, 5 Lean D, 8 Lean R. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball ratings as of Oct 8, 2026: 20 Toss-up (16 R-held, 3 D-held, plus the new FL-22), 17 Lean D, 11 Lean R, 15 Likely D, 20 Likely R. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_election_ratings)
- The Decision Desk HQ House forecast, updated Oct 8, 2026, gives Democrats a 76% chance of winning the House, with a projected 235 D and 200 R seats. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://votes.decisiondeskhq.com/forecast/2026/house)

- AdImpact data showed close to half a billion dollars in ad reservations placed between Oct 8 and Election Day. Both parties have begun pulling some reservations, including in NE-2. ## Resolution notes

- CONTROLH-2026 resolves Yes for the Democratic market if Democrats win control of the House in 2026. It can resolve early on a consensus of media calls. Otherwise it settles on the party of the Speaker of the House on Feb 1, 2027. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/CONTROLH-2026?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXDHOUSESEATS-27 and KXRHOUSESEATS-27 count seats held on Feb 1, 2027, including individuals who caucus with the party. Kiley-type independents count with the party they caucus with. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXDHOUSESEATS-27?with_nested_markets=true)
- Louisiana moved its U.S. House primary to Nov 3, 2026 and its House general election to Dec 12, 2026. Those seats will be decided after Election Day but before the Feb 1, 2027 seat-count date. (as_of: 2026-05-29; source: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/29/us/politics/louisiana-redistricting-map-majority-black-district.html)
