# Policy: Trump Cabinet and Staff Departures

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: who has left Trump's second administration since Jan 2025, with dates; first-term turnover base rates; and current pricing for the departure and successor markets. (KXLEADERSOUT returned not_found on the API on 2026-10-09; it has no open events.)

## Claims

- Trump removed Mike Waltz as national security adviser on May 1, 2025 and nominated him as UN ambassador, with Marco Rubio as interim NSA. Waltz was the first major official to leave in the second term. (as_of: 2025-05-01; source: https://www.deseret.com/politics/2025/05/01/mike-waltz-out-as-national-security-adviser)
- Trump fired Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem on Mar. 5, 2026, named her special envoy for "The Shield of the Americas," and said he would nominate Sen. Markwayne Mullin. (as_of: 2026-03-05; source: https://www.boston.com/news/politics/2026/03/05/trump-fires-homeland-security-secretary-kristi-noem/)
- Markwayne Mullin took office as Homeland Security Secretary on Mar. 24, 2026. (as_of: 2026-03-24; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_cabinet_of_Donald_Trump)
- Trump announced on Apr. 2, 2026 that Attorney General Pam Bondi was leaving and that Deputy AG Todd Blanche would serve as acting attorney general. (as_of: 2026-04-02; source: https://www.axios.com/2026/04/02/trump-todd-blanche-acting-attorney-general)
- Labor Secretary Lori Chavez-DeRemer resigned on Apr. 20, 2026 during an internal misconduct investigation; Deputy Secretary Keith Sonderling became acting secretary. (as_of: 2026-04-21; source: https://www.wweek.com/news/2026/04/21/us-department-of-labor-secretary-chavez-deremer-resigns-amid-investigation/)
- DNI Tulsi Gabbard announced her resignation on May 22, 2026, effective June 30, citing her husband's cancer diagnosis. (as_of: 2026-05-22; source: https://www.wafb.com/2026/05/22/tulsi-gabbard-resigns-director-national-intelligence/)
- 27 Senate-confirmed officials in Cabinet departments had left in Trump's second term, versus 11 in his entire first term and an average of 4.3 for the Bush, Obama and Biden administrations (Partnership for Public Service). (as_of: 2026-07-22; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/departures-senate-confirmed-trump-administration-officials-cabinet-rcna588694)
- Jay Clayton took office as Director of National Intelligence on Aug. 3, 2026. (as_of: 2026-08-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_cabinet_of_Donald_Trump)
- The Senate confirmed Todd Blanche as attorney general 50-49 on Aug. 8, 2026. Republican Sens. Susan Collins and Lisa Murkowski voted no. (as_of: 2026-08-08; source: https://www.wunc.org/2026-08-08/gop-controlled-senate-delivers-win-for-trump-with-blanche-confirmation)

(as_of: 2026-09-10; source: https://idahonews.com/news/nation-world/democrats-plan-to-go-after-defense-secretary-hegseth-if-they-win-2026-midterms-robert-garcia-pentagon-jason-crow)

## Resolution notes

- KXTRUMPADMINLEAVE requires an actual departure date within the window. Temporary leave, suspension or recusal does not count. Death may settle at last fair price. Re-occupying the role can still settle on the first vacation. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXTRUMPADMINLEAVE-26DEC31?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXHEGSETHOUT resolves on Hegseth leaving as Secretary of Defense, with the same actual-departure and death rules. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXHEGSETHOUT-26APR?with_nested_markets=true)

If the person serving at issuance stays through expiry, every named market resolves No and "No new person" resolves Yes.
