# Policy: California Billionaire Tax (Prop 40)

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: California Proposition 40, the one-time 5% billionaire wealth tax on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot. Covers its path to qualification, the competing measures, polling, money, and the Kalshi market.

## Claims

- On 22 October 2025, Jim Mangia and Suzanne Jimenez filed the initiative (#25-0024) with the California Attorney General. It was cleared for signature gathering on 26 December 2025. (as_of: 2025-12-26; source: https://ballotpedia.org/California_Proposition_40,_One-Time_Wealth_Tax_for_State-Funded_Healthcare,_Education,_and_Food_Assistance_Programs_Initiative_(2026))
- Berkeley IGS polled the billionaire tax at 52% yes, 33% no, 15% undecided among 5,109 registered voters (March 9-15, 2026). (as_of: 2026-03-20; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_California_Proposition_40)
- Proponents submitted signatures on 27 April 2026. (as_of: 2026-04-27; source: https://ballotpedia.org/California_Proposition_40,_One-Time_Wealth_Tax_for_State-Funded_Healthcare,_Education,_and_Food_Assistance_Programs_Initiative_(2026))
- On 17 June 2026 the Secretary of State reported that the initiative had qualified. Of 1,617,675 signatures, 980,438 were projected valid, against 874,641 required. (as_of: 2026-06-17; source: https://ballotpedia.org/California_Proposition_40,_One-Time_Wealth_Tax_for_State-Funded_Healthcare,_Education,_and_Food_Assistance_Programs_Initiative_(2026))
- The next day, sponsor SEIU-UHW offered to withdraw the measure if Gov. Newsom backed a smaller 2% levy passed by the legislature. Newsom rejected the offer, and the measure stayed on the ballot past the June 25 withdrawal deadline. (as_of: 2026-06-25; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_California_Proposition_40)
- Berkeley IGS (Aug 3-9, 2026; 2,310 LV) found 48% yes, 41% no, 11% undecided. (as_of: 2026-08-24; source: https://ballotpedia.org/California_Proposition_40,_One-Time_Wealth_Tax_for_State-Funded_Healthcare,_Education,_and_Food_Assistance_Programs_Initiative_(2026))
- In August 2026 the California Democratic Party endorsed Prop 40 on a second vote with 61.7%, just over its 60% threshold. Gov. Newsom opposes the tax. (as_of: 2026-08-31; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_California_Proposition_40)
- PPIC's September 2026 survey (Sept 4-10; 1,103 LV) found 52% yes, 46% no, 2% undecided. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://ballotpedia.org/California_Proposition_40,_One-Time_Wealth_Tax_for_State-Funded_Healthcare,_Education,_and_Food_Assistance_Programs_Initiative_(2026))
- Berkeley IGS (Sept 15-20, 2026; 4,512 LV) found 45% yes, 42% no, 13% undecided. (as_of: 2026-09-25; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_California_Proposition_40)
- Prop 40 appears on the Secretary of State's list of qualified measures for the November 3, 2026 ballot as "Imposes One-Time Tax on Certain Taxpayers. Initiative Constitutional Amendment and Statute." (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/ballot-measures/qualified-ballot-measures)
- Per the LAO, Prop 40 charges billionaires who were California residents on 1 January 2026 a one-time tax of 5% of net worth. The tax is due in 2027 and can be paid over five years at extra cost. Real estate, pensions and retirement accounts are generally excluded, and 90% of the money must go to health care. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://lao.ca.gov/BallotAnalysis/Proposition?number=40&year=2026)
- The official fiscal estimate is a temporary revenue increase of tens of billions of dollars over several years, plus a possible ongoing loss of less than $1 billion a year in income tax from billionaires. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://ballotpedia.org/California_Proposition_40,_One-Time_Wealth_Tax_for_State-Funded_Healthcare,_Education,_and_Food_Assistance_Programs_Initiative_(2026))
- Propositions 41 and 42 on the same ballot contain competing-measure clauses. If all three pass and 41 and 42 get more votes than Prop 40, they would nullify it. Prop 40 has a reciprocal clause. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://ballotpedia.org/California_Proposition_40,_One-Time_Wealth_Tax_for_State-Funded_Healthcare,_Education,_and_Food_Assistance_Programs_Initiative_(2026))
- In reports through 19 September 2026, support committees had raised about $32.2 million and opposition committees about $101.7 million net of PAC-to-PAC transfers. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://ballotpedia.org/California_Proposition_40,_One-Time_Wealth_Tax_for_State-Funded_Healthcare,_Education,_and_Food_Assistance_Programs_Initiative_(2026))
- Building a Better California, co-founded by Sergey Brin and Eric Schmidt, had raised $118 million by August 2026, including $102 million from Brin. Peter Thiel separately gave about $100 million to an anti-Prop 40 committee. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_California_Proposition_40)
- Late polls: SurveyUSA (Sept 15-20; 742 LV) had 48-40 yes, Mason-Dixon (Sept 21-24; 625 LV) 48-38 yes, and IVC Media (Sept 23-Oct 2; 2,906 LV) 38-42, with no ahead. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_California_Proposition_40)
- KXCAWEALTHTAX-26 was 0.33 bid / 0.34 ask (last 0.34) on about 1.39 million contracts traded. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXCAWEALTHTAX-26?with_nested_markets=true)

## Resolution notes

- KXCAWEALTHTAX-26 resolves Yes if a one-time tax on billionaires ballot initiative in California passes. It may resolve early once a consensus of designated media sources declares a winner. The California Secretary of State is the listed settlement source. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXCAWEALTHTAX-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- The market's expected expiration is 3 Nov 2026, but its close and latest expiration run to 3 Nov 2027. The rule says "passes" and does not address the Prop 41/42 nullification clauses, so a Prop 40 majority that is later nullified is an edge case. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXCAWEALTHTAX-26?with_nested_markets=true)
