# Base Rate: State Ballot Measure Passage

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: how often statewide ballot measures pass, split by citizen-initiated versus legislatively referred, from Ballotpedia year-end analyses and its initiative history table.

## Claims

- In 2018, Ballotpedia counted 167 certified statewide measures; 116 were approved and 50 defeated, with one (Kentucky Marsy's Law) pending a court ruling. (as_of: 2019-01-09; source: http://web.archive.org/web/20190109032942/https://ballotpedia.org/2018_ballot_measures)
- In 2019, 36 statewide measures were on ballots in eight states; 22 were approved and 14 defeated. (as_of: 2019-12-17; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2019/12/17/ballotpedia-releases-its-year-end-state-ballot-measure-analysis-with-statistical-summaries-campaign-finance-analysis-readability-analysis-historical-context-and-more-2/)
- In 2019, state legislatures referred 22 measures; voters approved 18 and rejected 4. (as_of: 2019-12-17; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2019/12/17/ballotpedia-releases-its-year-end-state-ballot-measure-analysis-with-statistical-summaries-campaign-finance-analysis-readability-analysis-historical-context-and-more-2/)
- In 2020, voters in 34 states decided 129 statewide measures; 93 were approved and 36 defeated. 43 were citizen-initiated. (as_of: 2020-12-17; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2020/12/17/ballotpedia-releases-year-end-analysis-of-statewide-ballot-measures/)
- In 2022, of 30 citizen-initiated measures with campaign finance activity, the side that raised more money won 24 (80%). (as_of: 2022-12-16; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2022/12/16/ballotpedias-year-end-analysis-of-statewide-ballot-measures-3/)
- In 2022, voters in 28 states decided 99 constitutional amendments, approving 70 (70.71%); 88 were legislatively referred and 11 citizen-initiated. (as_of: 2022-12-16; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2022/12/16/ballotpedias-year-end-analysis-of-statewide-ballot-measures-3/)
- In 2024, voters in 35 states decided 96 constitutional amendments and approved 60 (63%), the lowest amendment approval rate since 2005 (14 of 26, 54%). (as_of: 2024-12-20; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2024/12/20/approval-rate-for-constitutional-amendments-in-2024-hits-lowest-level-since-2005/)
- From 2003 through 2024, odd-year cycles had a higher average approval rate for constitutional amendments than even years; 2007 approved 28 of 31 (90%) and 2017 approved all 17. (as_of: 2024-12-20; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2024/12/20/approval-rate-for-constitutional-amendments-in-2024-hits-lowest-level-since-2005/)
