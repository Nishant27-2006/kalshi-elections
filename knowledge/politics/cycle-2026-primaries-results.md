# Cycle: 2026 Primaries Results

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: notable 2026 Senate and governor primary and runoff results through early October 2026, each dated to when it became known.

## Claims

### Texas (primary Mar 3, runoff May 26)
- TX Dem Senate primary (Mar 3): James Talarico beat Jasmine Crockett 53.1% to 45.6% with most votes counted and avoided a runoff. Crockett conceded the morning of Mar 4. (as_of: 2026-03-04; source: https://www.marfapublicradio.org/texas/2026-03-04/james-talarico-beats-jasmine-crockett-in-democratic-primary-for-u-s-senate)
- TX GOP Senate primary (Mar 3): Sen. John Cornyn and AG Ken Paxton finished top two of eight. No one passed 50%, which forced a May 26 runoff. (as_of: 2026-03-04; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/03/04/incumbent-john-cornyn-r-and-ken-paxton-r-were-the-top-two-finishers-among-the-eight-candidates-running-in-the-republican-primary-for-u-s-senate-in-texas/)
- TX governor primaries (Mar 3): Greg Abbott (R) took about 82.6%. Gina Hinojosa (D) won outright with about 61.13% in a crowded field. (as_of: 2026-03-04; source: https://www.kbtx.com/2026/03/04/texas-2026-midterm-primaries-abbott-patrick-cruise-wins-attorney-general-comptroller-races-head-runoffs)
- TX GOP attorney general primary (Mar 3): state Sen. Mayes Middleton led with 41.12% to Rep. Chip Roy's about 30.35%, sending the race to a runoff. (as_of: 2026-03-04; source: https://www.kbtx.com/2026/03/04/texas-2026-midterm-primaries-abbott-patrick-cruise-wins-attorney-general-comptroller-races-head-runoffs)
- Trump endorsed Paxton on Truth Social on May 19, 2026, a week before the runoff. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/05/27/ken-paxton-r-defeated-incumbent-john-cornyn-r-in-the-republican-primary-runoff-for-u-s-senate-in-texas-on-may-26-2026/)
- TX GOP Senate runoff (May 26): Paxton beat incumbent Cornyn with roughly 64% to about 35%. (as_of: 2026-05-26; source: https://www.kut.org/politics/2026-05-26/texas-runoff-results-paxton-wins-gop-u-s-senate-nomination-plus-other-key-statewide-race-results)
- Ballotpedia's final first-round figures had Cornyn ahead of Paxton 42.0% to 40.5% on Mar 3. Paxton went from second in the first round to a runoff win of about 29 points. (as_of: 2026-05-27; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/05/27/ken-paxton-r-defeated-incumbent-john-cornyn-r-in-the-republican-primary-runoff-for-u-s-senate-in-texas-on-may-26-2026/)
- TX runoffs (May 26): Mayes Middleton beat Chip Roy for the GOP AG nomination with about 55%. Vikki Goodwin won the Dem LG runoff with about 68%. (as_of: 2026-05-26; source: https://www.kut.org/politics/2026-05-26/texas-runoff-results-paxton-wins-gop-u-s-senate-nomination-plus-other-key-statewide-race-results)
- Runoff turnout in Texas was over 1.3 million Republicans and about 470,000 Democrats. (as_of: 2026-05-26; source: https://www.kut.org/politics/2026-05-26/texas-runoff-results-paxton-wins-gop-u-s-senate-nomination-plus-other-key-statewide-race-results)

### North Carolina (Mar 3)
- North Carolina's GOP Senate primary (Mar 3, 2026): AP called it for Michael Whatley, former RNC chair, at about 8:50 p.m. ET. He beat six other Republicans for the open Tillis seat. (as_of: 2026-03-03; source: https://www.wbtv.com/2026/03/04/ap-michael-whatley-wins-2026-republican-primary-us-senate-seat-north-carolina)
- Former Gov. Roy Cooper (D) and Whatley (R) both won their North Carolina Senate primaries easily over crowded fields. (as_of: 2026-03-03; source: https://cfjctoday.com/2026/03/03/roy-cooper-and-michael-whatley-clinch-north-carolina-senate-nods-setting-up-high-stakes-fall-fight/)

### Illinois (Mar 17)
- IL Dem Senate primary (Mar 17, 2026): Lt. Gov. Juliana Stratton, backed by Gov. Pritzker, won with 40.4% over Reps. Raja Krishnamoorthi and Robin Kelly. (as_of: 2026-03-17; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Illinois)

### Georgia (primary May 19, runoff Jun 16)
- GA GOP Senate primary (May 19): no majority. Rep. Mike Collins and Derek Dooley (Kemp-backed) advanced to a June 16 runoff, and Rep. Buddy Carter finished third. (as_of: 2026-05-20; source: https://news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/05/20/georgia-primary-2026-here-are-the-results-in-key-races/)
- GA GOP governor primary (May 19): Lt. Gov. Burt Jones (Trump-endorsed) finished first and Rick Jackson second with 32.5%, setting up a runoff. Keisha Lance Bottoms won the Democratic nomination outright. (as_of: 2026-05-20; source: https://news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/05/20/georgia-primary-2026-here-are-the-results-in-key-races/)
- GA GOP Senate runoff (Jun 16): Collins 390,089 (56%) to Dooley 312,278 (44%). Collins faces Sen. Jon Ossoff. (as_of: 2026-06-17; source: https://www.news4jax.com/vote-2026/2026/06/16/georgia-primary-runoff-2026-here-are-the-results/)
- GA GOP governor runoff (Jun 16): Rick Jackson 373,486 (53%) beat first-round leader Burt Jones 335,921 (47%). (as_of: 2026-06-17; source: https://www.news4jax.com/vote-2026/2026/06/16/georgia-primary-runoff-2026-here-are-the-results/)

### Maine (Jun 9)
- ME Dem Senate primary (Jun 9, 2026): Graham Platner won with over 150,000 votes, a record for a Democratic primary candidate in Maine. Gov. Janet Mills had suspended her campaign in April. (as_of: 2026-06-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Maine)

### Michigan (Aug 4)
- MI Dem Senate primary (Aug 4, 2026): Abdul El-Sayed won with 48.5%. Mike Rogers was unopposed for the GOP nomination. (as_of: 2026-08-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Michigan)

### Florida (Aug 18)
- FL governor primaries (Aug 18, 2026): Rep. Byron Donalds won the GOP nomination, with Lt. Gov. Jay Collins second. David Jolly won the Democratic nomination. (as_of: 2026-08-18; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Florida_gubernatorial_election)
