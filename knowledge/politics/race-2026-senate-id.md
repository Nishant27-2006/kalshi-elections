# Race: 2026 Idaho Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Jim Risch (R) seeks a fourth term against independent Todd Achilles, November 3, 2026. The Democratic nominee withdrew.

## Claims

- Risch won the May 19 Republican primary with 67.3% (156,140 votes); Josh Roy took 14.3% and Joe Evans 14.1%. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Idaho)
- Realtor David Roth, the 2022 nominee, won the Democratic primary with 61.9% over Brad Moore (31.1%). (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Idaho)
- Todd Achilles, a former Democratic state representative, resigned from the legislature to run for the seat as an independent. (as_of: 2025-07-01; source: https://idahocapitalsun.com/2025/07/01/idaho-lawmaker-steps-down-from-legislature-to-run-for-u-s-senate-seat-as-independent-candidate/)
- On July 8 the Ada County Democratic Central Committee gave Roth until Aug 12 to raise $50,000 and produce a campaign plan, or it would consider endorsing Achilles. Roth called the demands "completely ridiculous." (as_of: 2026-07-18; source: https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/jul/18/idaho-democrats-think-jim-risch-could-be-beaten-bu/)
- Roth ended his Senate campaign on July 28. (as_of: 2026-07-28; source: https://idahocapitalsun.com/2026/07/28/idaho-democrat-david-roth-ends-campaign-for-us-senate-seat-held-by-risch/)
- Other general-election candidates: Libertarian Matt Loesby and independent Natalie Fleming. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Idaho)
- Trump endorsed Risch in March 2025. (as_of: 2025-03-20; source: https://localnews8.com/news/top-stories/2025/03/20/president-trump-endorses-senator-risch-well-ahead-of-mid-term-elections/)
- The U.S. Chamber of Commerce endorsed Risch. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: https://www.uschamber.com/improving-government/u-s-chamber-endorses-senator-jim-risch-for-u-s-senate-in-idaho)
- Achilles is endorsed by the Forward Party and the American Federation of Government Employees. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Idaho)
- FEC through June 30, 2026: Risch raised $4.43M with $3.54M cash on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Idaho)

- SENATEID-26-R: "If a representative of the Republican party is sworn in as a Senator of Idaho for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." The D market uses the same wording for the Democratic party. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEID-26?with_nested_markets=true)

An Achilles win settles the D contract No, because he is not a Democrat. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEID-26?with_nested_markets=true)
