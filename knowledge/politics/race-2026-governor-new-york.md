# Race: 2026 New York Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Democratic incumbent Kathy Hochul seeks a second full term against Republican/Conservative Bruce Blakeman, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Hochul (D) is running for re-election; primaries were held June 23, 2026, and nominees chose their own running mates instead of separate LG primaries. (as_of: 2026-06-23; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_York_gubernatorial_election)
- Elise Stefanik withdrew from the GOP primary in December 2025, and Trump then endorsed Nassau County Executive Bruce Blakeman. (as_of: 2025-12-20; source: https://www.politico.com/news/2025/12/20/trump-new-york-governor-endorsement-bruce-blakeman-00702013)
- Blakeman is the Republican and Conservative nominee; Republicans have not won a statewide race in New York since Pataki in 2002. (as_of: 2026-06-23; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_York_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: Hochul (D) 53.2%, Lee Zeldin (R) 46.8%, about D+6.4. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Harris won New York by 12.60 points (55.91% to 43.31%). (as_of: 2024-12-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook rates the race Solid D and Inside Elections Likely D as of their Oct 1, 2026 updates. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rates it Safe D (Oct 6, 2026 table). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- McLaughlin & Associates (R, Blakeman-aligned), Aug 27–31, 800 LV: Hochul 50, Blakeman 46. (as_of: 2026-09-08; source: https://nypost.com/2026/09/08/us-news/blakeman-poll-claims-hes-within-4-points-of-toppling-hochul/)
- Siena, Sep 11–17, 1,144 LV, ±3.9: Hochul 50, Blakeman 41, little changed from August's 49–39; Hochul job approval 48–47; independents favor Blakeman 44–39. (as_of: 2026-09-23; source: https://sri.siena.edu/2026/09/23/siena-poll-of-likely-new-york-state-voters-hochul-50-blakeman-41/)
- Siena: voters view Trump's Blakeman endorsement negatively (51–30) and NYC Mayor Mamdani's Hochul endorsement positively (50–30). (as_of: 2026-09-23; source: https://sri.siena.edu/2026/09/23/siena-poll-of-likely-new-york-state-voters-hochul-50-blakeman-41/)
- Quinnipiac, Sep 17–20, 1,026 LV: Hochul 58, Blakeman 39. (as_of: 2026-09-23; source: https://poll.qu.edu/poll-release?releaseid=3967)
- co/efficient (R), Sep 23–25, 1,046 LV: Hochul 48, Blakeman 41. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://nypost.com/2026/10/05/us-news/more-than-half-of-new-york-voters-concerned-about-rise-of-socialism-poll/)
- Marist, Oct 1–4, 1,361 RV: Hochul 53, Blakeman 42. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://maristpoll.marist.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Marist-Poll_NYS_NOS-and-Tables_202610051034565bVw9_q.pdf)
