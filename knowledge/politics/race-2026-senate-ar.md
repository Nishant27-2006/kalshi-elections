# Race: 2026 Arkansas Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Tom Cotton (R) seeks a third term against Democrat Hallie Shoffner, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Cotton announced he would seek re-election in 2026. (as_of: 2025-02-27; source: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/top-intel-republican-tom-cotton-seeking-re-election-senate-2026)
- Cotton won the March 3 Republican primary with 81.6% (230,209 of 281,973 votes). (as_of: 2026-03-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Arkansas)
- Farmer Hallie Shoffner won the Democratic primary with 78.3% (102,289 votes) over Lewisville mayor Ethan Dunbar. (as_of: 2026-03-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Arkansas)
- Libertarian Jeff Wadlin is also on the ballot. (as_of: 2026-02-23; source: https://www.myarklamiss.com/news/arkansas-news/arkansas-libertarians-nominate-candidates-for-2026-general-election/)
- FEC through June 30, 2026: Cotton raised $13.60M with $9.93M cash on hand; Shoffner raised $2.27M with $0.86M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Arkansas)
- GrayHouse (R, for America One Policies), Feb 7–9, 550 LV: Cotton 58, Shoffner 36. (as_of: 2026-03-05; source: https://brooklandnews.com/2026/03/05/poll-finds-strong-support-for-trump-cotton-in-arkansas/)
- 2040 Strategy Group (D, Shoffner campaign), Jul 17–25, 586 LV: Cotton 46, Shoffner 43. (as_of: 2026-07-30; source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xl5CVWv-W49Lo1iPOweW9Mm78u0xUsvU/view)
- Hendrix College (for Talk Business & Politics), Aug 11–12, 1,217 LV: Shoffner 47, Cotton 44, Wadlin 3. (as_of: 2026-08-19; source: https://talkbusiness.net/2026/08/poll-tight-race-for-us-senate-between-cotton-shoffner/)
- J.L. Partners, Aug 14–18, 803 LV: Cotton 51, Shoffner 35, Wadlin 4. (as_of: 2026-08-18; source: https://static1.squarespace.com/static/663253f0bc87b7070102f41f/t/6a84d771481c4817e92f0eee/1787090801301/AR+Aug+26+Polling+Memo.pdf)
- Silver Bulletin average through Aug 18: Cotton 47.6, Shoffner 41.0 (Cotton +6.6). (as_of: 2026-08-20; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Arkansas)
- Ratings: Cook Solid R (Oct 6), Inside Elections Solid R (Oct 1), Sabato Safe R (Oct 6), Silver Bulletin Solid R, DDHQ Safe R, The Economist Likely R, Split Ticket Safe R (Oct 7). (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Arkansas)
- Democrats have not won a Senate election in Arkansas since 2008. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Arkansas)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Arkansas 64.20% to 33.56%. (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Cotton 66.53% against Libertarian Ricky Dale Harrington Jr. (33.47%). No Democrat was on the ballot. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_Arkansas)
