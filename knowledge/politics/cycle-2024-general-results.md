# Cycle: 2024 General Election Results

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: 2024 presidential, Senate and House outcomes, national and swing-state polling error, and how the forecasts and prediction markets priced the presidential race just before Election Day.

## Claims

- The Block (Nov 2, 2024) reports Trump at 59.1% on Polymarket, down from a high of 71.5% on July 16, and at 55% on Kalshi, down from 65.2% on Oct 29. (as_of: 2024-11-02; source: https://www.theblock.co/post/324243/trumps-odds-fall-10-from-highs-on-polymarket-kalshi-while-whale-denies-political-agenda)
- The Block reports that a French bettor known as "Théo" had put about $30 million on Trump to win and said a Trump win would net him about $80 million. (as_of: 2024-11-02; source: https://www.theblock.co/post/324243/trumps-odds-fall-10-from-highs-on-polymarket-kalshi-while-whale-denies-political-agenda)
- On Monday afternoon, Nov 4, 2024, Polymarket had Trump at 58.1% and Harris at 41.9%. Kalshi had Trump at 55% and Harris at 45%. PredictIt was the exception, with Harris favored. (as_of: 2024-11-04; source: https://www.foxla.com/news/trump-dominating-over-harris-betting-markets-ahead-election-day)
- Fortune reports Polymarket had Trump at 62% early on Nov 5, 2024, rising to 98% that evening, and Kalshi had him at 57% at the start of the day, rising to 95%. (as_of: 2024-11-06; source: https://fortune.com/2024/11/06/who-will-win-the-election-prediction-markets-donald-trump-surge-kamala-harris/)
- FiveThirtyEight's final forecast had Harris at 50% and Trump at 49%. Nate Silver called his own final model "about as close as you can possibly get to 50/50." (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://salon.com/2024/11/05/nate-silvers-final-forecast-as-close-as-you-can-possibly-get-to-5050)
- FiveThirtyEight's final national polling average had Harris ahead 48.0% to 46.8%. (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://www.iheart.com/content/2024-11-05-predicted-winner-based-on-average-final-election-polls-revealed/)
- The final NYT/Siena swing-state polls (Oct 24 to Nov 2) had Harris ahead in NV (49-46), NC (48-46), WI (49-47) and GA (48-47), Trump ahead in AZ (49-45), and PA and MI tied. (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://www.iheart.com/content/2024-11-05-predicted-winner-based-on-average-final-election-polls-revealed/)
- AP called Wisconsin, and with it the presidency, for Trump at 5:34 a.m. ET on Nov 6, 2024. (as_of: 2024-11-06; source: https://pbswisconsin.org/news-item/why-ap-called-wisconsin-and-the-white-house-for-trump/)
- AP called House control for Republicans on Nov 13, 2024, when they reached 218 seats. (as_of: 2024-11-13; source: https://abcnews.go.com/US/wireStory/republicans-win-218-us-house-seats-claiming-majority-115846823)
- Bob Casey (D) conceded Pennsylvania's Senate race to Dave McCormick (R) on Nov 21, 2024, with McCormick ahead 48.8% to 48.6% (about 16,000 votes). The automatic recount was halted. (as_of: 2024-11-22; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2024-election/bob-casey-concedes-pennsylvania-senate-race-dave-mccormick-rcna181277)
- With McCormick's win, Republicans hold a 53-47 Senate majority for the next Congress. (as_of: 2024-11-22; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2024-election/bob-casey-concedes-pennsylvania-senate-race-dave-mccormick-rcna181277)
- The last House race was called for Adam Gray (D) over John Duarte (R) in CA-13 by 187 votes, nearly a month after Election Day. That left the final House at 220 Republicans and 215 Democrats. (as_of: 2024-12-04; source: https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/final-house-race-called-see-official-results)
- NBC News Decision Desk found that in the final two weeks, polls understated Trump's national support by 2.4 points on average, against 3.3 points in 2020. (as_of: 2024-12-03; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2024-election/-polls-missed-decisive-slice-trump-voters-2024-rcna182488)
- NBC found the polling miss in all seven swing states was "consistently between 2 and 3 percentage points," all in Trump's direction. (as_of: 2024-12-03; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2024-election/-polls-missed-decisive-slice-trump-voters-2024-rcna182488)
- NBC found polls also understated Trump outside the battlegrounds: New York by 4.6 points, Texas by 4.4 and Wyoming by 5.8. (as_of: 2024-12-03; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2024-election/-polls-missed-decisive-slice-trump-voters-2024-rcna182488)
- Official electoral vote: Trump 312, Harris 226. Maine split 1 Trump / 3 Harris and Nebraska split 4 Trump / 1 Harris. (as_of: 2025-01-16; source: https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/2024presgeresults.pdf)
- Official popular vote: Trump 77,302,580 (49.80%), Harris 75,017,613 (48.32%), out of 155,238,302 total, a Trump margin of about 1.47 points. (as_of: 2025-01-16; source: https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/2024presgeresults.pdf)
- Official swing-state margins (Trump minus Harris, share of total vote): AZ +5.53, NV +3.10, NC +3.21, GA +2.19, PA +1.70, MI +1.41, WI +0.86. (as_of: 2025-01-16; source: https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/2024presgeresults.pdf)
