# Race: 2026 Iowa Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open Republican-held seat (Joni Ernst retiring): Rep. Ashley Hinson (R) vs state Rep. Josh Turek (D), November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Incumbent Joni Ernst (R) is not seeking a third term. In the June 2 primaries, Trump-endorsed Hinson won about 74% over Jim Carlin and Turek won about 63% over state Sen. Zach Wahls. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Iowa)
- The general-election ballot also lists Libertarian Thomas Laehn. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Iowa)
- FEC totals through June 30, 2026: Hinson raised $10.22M with $6.75M on hand. Turek raised $6.28M with $2.36M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=IA&election_full=true)
- Inside Elections moved Iowa from Lean Republican to Tilt Republican. (as_of: 2026-08-06; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball moved Iowa's open Senate race from Leans Republican to Toss-up. (as_of: 2026-09-22; source: https://centerforpolitics.org/crystalball/rating-changes-toward-democrats-across-the-board-mi-sen-to-leans-d-other-key-statewide-races-to-toss-up/)
- Fox News (Beacon/Shaw), Sep 24–28: Turek 49, Hinson 47 among likely voters; Turek 50, Hinson 46 among registered voters. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/fox-news-poll-turek-hinson-close-iowa-senate-race)
- NYT/Siena, late September, likely voters: Hinson 48, Turek 47. (as_of: 2026-10-03; source: https://sri.siena.edu/2026/10/03/nytimes-siena-poll-of-likely-voters-senate-races/)
- Per CNN, the Senate Leadership Fund added $20M to its October advertising in Iowa. CNN puts Trump's Iowa approval at 42% in the Fox poll. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- Suffolk University, Oct 1–4, 500 LV, MoE ±4.4: Turek 48, Hinson 44. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.suffolk.edu/-/media/suffolk/documents/academics/research-at-suffolk/suprc/polls/other-states/2026/10_7_2026_iowa_statewide_marginals.pdf?la=en&hash=2D72AC4AC373F8C2FA39466A7E5CD59D2370B6F9)
- CNN/SSRS, Sep 29–Oct 5, 801 LV, MoE ±4.2: Hinson 45, Turek 43. Almost 3 in 10 had not firmly decided. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/08/politics/cnn-polls-ohio-iowa-senate-governor-midterms)
- Same CNN/SSRS poll: Iowa LV prefer Republican Senate control 53% to 46%, and Trump approval is 41%. Democrat Rob Sand leads the governor's race by 9. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/08/politics/cnn-polls-ohio-iowa-senate-governor-midterms)
- Cook Political Report lists Iowa (open) as a Toss Up. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Iowa 55.73% to 42.52% (R+13.21). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Trump won Iowa 53.09% to 44.89% (R+8.20). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Ernst (R) 51.74%, Theresa Greenfield (D) 45.15%. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_Iowa)
- Democrats have not won an Iowa Senate election since 2008. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Iowa)
- Kalshi SENATEIA-26 on Oct 9: R (Hinson) bid/ask 0.56/0.57; D (Turek) 0.43/0.44. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEIA-26?with_nested_markets=true)
