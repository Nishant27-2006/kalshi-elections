# Policy: Early Departures from Office (McConnell, Walz)

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: whether Sen. Mitch McConnell quits or announces an early exit before Election Day 2026, and whether Gov. Tim Walz leaves office before 2027. Both are lame ducks; the markets only pay on an early exit inside the window.

## Claims

- McConnell announced on Feb. 20, 2025 that he would not run for an eighth Senate term in 2026 and would retire from politics. (as_of: 2025-02-20; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_McConnell)
- McConnell fell at the U.S. Capitol on Oct. 16, 2025 while being doorstepped by an amateur reporter. (as_of: 2025-10-16; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_McConnell)
- McConnell was hospitalized for eight days in February 2026 for "flu-like symptoms." (as_of: 2026-02-28; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_McConnell)
- Kentucky's Senate primaries were held May 19, 2026. Trump-endorsed Rep. Andy Barr won the Republican nomination with 60.5% over Daniel Cameron, and Charles Booker won the Democratic nomination with 47%. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Kentucky)
- On June 14, 2026, McConnell was found unconscious at his home and rushed to a hospital. (as_of: 2026-06-14; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_McConnell)
- Emergency dispatch and scanner audio from the day of McConnell's hospitalization recorded first responders finding an unconscious person in cardiac arrest and performing CPR (NBC News, July 1). (as_of: 2026-07-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_McConnell)
- McConnell returned to the Senate on Sep. 14, 2026 after missing votes from mid-June. He said he was still in physical therapy and had not fully recovered. (as_of: 2026-09-14; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_McConnell)
- On Oct. 7, 2026, asked on MS NOW whether the 84-year-old McConnell should step down for the rest of his term, Sen. Thom Tillis compared the question to the one asked about Joe Biden. McConnell was still being moved around the Capitol in a wheelchair and was not answering questions. ## Resolution notes

- KXRETIREMM-26 rule text: "If Mitch McConnell has resigned, retired, or otherwise voluntarily stepped down from their Congressional office, or announced their intent to resign, retire, or otherwise voluntarily step down from their office prematurely, after Issuance and before Nov 3, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes." (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXRETIREMM-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXRETIREMM-26 opened Dec. 21, 2024 and closes Nov. 3, 2026 15:00 UTC. The Feb. 2025 decision not to seek re-election did not settle it, because the market requires a *premature* exit or announcement. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXRETIREMM-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXLEAVEWALZ rule text: "If Tim Walz leaves as Governor of Minnesota after Issuance and before [date], then the market resolves to Yes." Secondary: "Tim Walz must have an actual departure date by vacating the role within the time period. If the person leaves the role due to death (i.e., if the person dies while holding the role), all contracts on the person may resolve to the last fair price as determined in the sole discretion of the Exchange. Temporary leaves of absence, suspensions, or recusals do not constitute leaving, resigning from, or retiring from the role unless the individual formally and permanently ceases to hold the position. If the person vacates the role and then re-occupies the role, the contract may settle on the initial vacation of the role. For 'leave,' cessation of holding the role qualifies, including resignation, retirement, removal, expiration of term without renewal, recall, or other means by which the individual no longer occupies the role—this includes both voluntary early departure AND the natural expiration of a term." (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXLEAVEWALZ-27JAN01?with_nested_markets=true)
- The "Before 2027" Walz market closes Jan. 2, 2027 at 14:59 UTC. That is before the Jan. 4, 2027 end of term, so the natural expiration of his term falls outside the window. Only an early exit (resignation, removal or similar) settles it Yes. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXLEAVEWALZ-27JAN01?with_nested_markets=true)
