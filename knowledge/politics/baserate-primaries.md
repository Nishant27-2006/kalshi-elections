# Base Rate: Primaries

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: incumbent primary defeat rates (Congress and state legislatures), Trump-endorsed candidates' primary success rates, and how often the first-round leader wins a primary runoff.

## Claims

- In 2022, 16 U.S. House incumbents lost re-election in a primary, the most in the ten years Ballotpedia had tracked. (as_of: 2024-08-07; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2024/08/07/rep-cori-bush-d-mo-becomes-the-fourth-u-s-house-incumbent-to-lose-a-primary-election-this-year/)
- From 2014 through 2022, Ballotpedia tracked 41 U.S. House incumbents who lost re-election in a primary, an average of 8.2 per cycle. (as_of: 2024-08-07; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2024/08/07/rep-cori-bush-d-mo-becomes-the-fourth-u-s-house-incumbent-to-lose-a-primary-election-this-year/)
- As of Aug. 7, 2024, four U.S. House incumbents had lost primaries in 2024 (Carl, Bowman, Good, Bush), one of them in an incumbent-vs-incumbent race. (as_of: 2024-08-07; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2024/08/07/rep-cori-bush-d-mo-becomes-the-fourth-u-s-house-incumbent-to-lose-a-primary-election-this-year/)
- As of Aug. 20, 2026, ten U.S. House incumbents had lost 2026 primaries to non-incumbents, tying 2022 for the most since at least 2014; an eleventh lost an incumbent-vs-incumbent runoff (TX-18). Six of the ten were Democrats. (as_of: 2026-08-20; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/08/20/ten-u-s-house-incumbents-have-lost-primaries-to-non-incumbents-this-year-tying-2022-for-the-most-since-at-least-2014/)
- As of Sept. 2022, 216 state legislative incumbents (4.5% of those running) had lost 2022 primaries, which Ballotpedia called elevated relative to recent cycles. (as_of: 2022-09-15; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2022/09/15/heart-of-the-primaries-2022-republicans-issue-39)
- In 2024 state legislative primaries, 34 Democratic incumbents lost (1.5% of 2,214 running) and 134 Republican incumbents lost (5.1% of 2,624). (as_of: 2025-01-07; source: http://web.archive.org/web/20250107153137/https://ballotpedia.org/Incumbents_defeated_in_state_legislative_elections,_2024)
- In FairVote's dataset of 276 U.S. House and Senate primary runoffs from 1994 to 2022, turnout fell from the first round to the runoff in 266 (96%), with a median decline of 40%. (as_of: 2022-11-09; source: https://fairvote.org/report/primary-runoffs-report-2022/)
- In 2024 the median turnout drop from the initial primary to the federal runoff was 63%, the largest since FairVote began tracking in 1994. (as_of: 2024-12-17; source: https://fairvote.org/report/low-turnout-and-high-cost-in-primary-runoffs-1994-2024/)
