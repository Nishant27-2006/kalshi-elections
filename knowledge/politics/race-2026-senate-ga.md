# Race: 2026 Georgia Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Jon Ossoff (D) seeks a second term against Rep. Mike Collins (R), November 3, 2026, with a December 1 runoff if no one wins a majority.

## Claims

- If no candidate wins a majority on Nov 3, a runoff is scheduled for Dec 1. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Georgia)
- Ossoff ran unopposed in the May 19 Democratic primary. On the GOP side no one won a majority, which sent Collins and Derek Dooley to a June 16 runoff. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Georgia)
- Collins won the June 16 GOP runoff with 55.5% after Trump endorsed him. (as_of: 2026-06-16; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Georgia)
- FEC totals through June 30, 2026: Ossoff raised $97.99M with $42.59M on hand. Collins raised $7.41M with $2.08M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=GA&election_full=true)
- YouGov, Sep 15–18, 3,447 RV, MoE ±2.7: Ossoff 51, Collins 41. Trump approval among Georgia RV was 39/60. (as_of: 2026-09-23; source: https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2026/09/23/new-poll-ossoff-holds-double-digit-lead-over-collins-governors-race-dead-heat/)
- Cook Political Report shifted Georgia toward Democrats in a Sept 23 update that also moved Kansas and South Carolina. Analyst Jessica Taylor cited a "very blue environment forming." (as_of: 2026-09-23; source: https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/congressional/4738956/cook-political-report-senate-shift-democrats/)
- Big Data Poll (R), Sep 21–23, 678 LV: Ossoff 52, Collins 41. (as_of: 2026-09-24; source: https://www.bigdatapoll.com/blog/peach-state-poll-jon-ossoff-opens-up-lead-over-mike-collins-in-georgia/)
- InsiderAdvantage (R), Sep 22–23, 1,200 LV: Ossoff 50, Collins 42. (as_of: 2026-09-25; source: https://insideradvantage.com/insideradvantage-georgia-survey-jackson-leads-by-two-in-race-for-governor-ossoff-leads-by-eight-in-senate-contest/)
- Inside Elections moved Georgia from Tilt Democrat to Lean Democrat. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- Wedgewood Polls, Oct 2–3, 600 LV (forced choice): Ossoff 55, Collins 45. (as_of: 2026-10-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Georgia)
- Per CNN (AdImpact), Georgia is the only top Senate race where Democrats hold a big final-month ad-spending lead, $23M to $16M. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- Cook Political Report lists Georgia (Ossoff) as Likely Democrat. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rated Georgia Likely D as of Oct 6. Silver Bulletin, Split Ticket and FiftyPlusOne had Safe D as of Oct 7. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Georgia)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Georgia 50.72% to 48.53% (R+2.19). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Biden won Georgia 49.47% to 49.24% (D+0.23). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat: in the 2020 first round, David Perdue (R) took 49.73% and Ossoff 47.95%. Ossoff won the Jan 5, 2021 runoff 50.61% to 49.39%. (as_of: 2021-01-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_United_States_Senate_election_in_Georgia)
- Kalshi SENATEGA-26 on Oct 9: D (Ossoff) bid/ask 0.954/0.955; R (Collins) 0.040/0.047. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEGA-26?with_nested_markets=true)

## Resolution notes

- Rules text: "If a representative of the Republican [Democratic] party is sworn in as a Senator of Georgia for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." A Dec 1 runoff would only delay settlement; it would not change the criterion. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEGA-26?with_nested_markets=true)
