# Race: 2026 Mississippi Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith (R) seeks a second full term against Democrat Scott Colom, with independent Ty Pinkins also on the ballot, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Hyde-Smith won the March 10 Republican primary with 80.8% (127,852 votes) over physician Sarah Adlakha. (as_of: 2026-03-10; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Mississippi)
- Lowndes County DA Scott Colom won the Democratic primary with 72.9% (109,817 votes) over Priscilla Williams-Till (18.6%) and Albert Littell (8.5%). (as_of: 2026-03-10; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Mississippi)
- Ty Pinkins, the 2024 Democratic Senate nominee, left the Democratic Party and is running as an independent. (as_of: 2025-07-01; source: https://www.clarionledger.com/story/news/politics/2025/07/01/ty-pinkins-leaving-mississippi-democratic-party/84427842007/)
- Rep. Bennie Thompson endorsed Colom before the primary. (as_of: 2026-02-17; source: https://magnoliatribune.com/2026/02/17/thompson-endorses-colom-ahead-of-march-democratic-primary/)
- The U.S. Chamber of Commerce endorsed Hyde-Smith. (as_of: 2026-09-29; source: https://www.uschamber.com/improving-government/u-s-chamber-endorses-senator-cindy-hyde-smith-for-u-s-senate-in-mississippi)
- FEC through June 30, 2026: Hyde-Smith raised $5.46M with $2.54M cash on hand; Colom raised $2.38M with $0.85M on hand; Pinkins raised $98K. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Mississippi)
- Impact Research (D, for SPLC Action Fund, which opposes Hyde-Smith), Apr 8–12, 500 LV: Hyde-Smith 42, Colom 39, Pinkins 6; head-to-head Hyde-Smith 47, Colom 44. (as_of: 2026-04-20; source: https://www.splcactionfund.org/news/new-poll-of-likely-voters-shows-mississippi-senate-race-tightening-as-voters-continue-to-sour-on-sen-cindy-hyde-smith/)
- Same Impact Research poll: Hyde-Smith 33 vs a generic opponent 53. (as_of: 2026-04-20; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Mississippi)
- Data for Progress (D, for Way to Lead PAC), Aug 5–15, 1,018 LV: Hyde-Smith 44, Colom 38, Pinkins 7; head-to-head Hyde-Smith 47, Colom 41. (as_of: 2026-08-24; source: https://www.filesforprogress.org/datasets/2026/8/dfp_ms_ge26_aug2026_crosstabs.pdf)
- Ratings: Cook Solid R (Oct 6), Inside Elections Solid R (Oct 1), Sabato Safe R (Oct 6), DDHQ Likely R, The Economist Likely R, Silver Bulletin Solid R, Split Ticket Safe R (Oct 7). (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Mississippi)
- Democrats have not won a Senate election in Mississippi since 1982. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Mississippi)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Mississippi 60.89% to 38.00%. (as_of: 2024-12-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Hyde-Smith 54.11%, Mike Espy (D) 44.13%. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_Mississippi)

## Resolution notes

- SENATEMS-26-R: "If a representative of the Republican party is sworn in as a Senator of Mississippi for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." The D market uses the same wording for the Democratic party. There is no Pinkins contract, so a Pinkins win would settle both No. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEMS-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Both markets can be settled early once a consensus of media organizations projects the winner. Expected expiration is 2027-01-04. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEMS-26?with_nested_markets=true)
