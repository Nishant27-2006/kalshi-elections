# Nomination: 2028 Democratic

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: the 2028 Democratic presidential and vice-presidential nominations and who formally runs: candidates, national primary polling, the DNC calendar, statements of intent, and Kalshi prices.

## Claims

### Calendar
- The DNC adopted its 2028 early-state calendar by an overwhelming voice vote at its meeting in Austin, Texas, with only a few audible objections. (as_of: 2026-08-15; source: https://washingtonsun.com/democrats/dnc-new-presidential-primary-calendar-black-vote-south-carolina)
- Adopted early-window order and dates: South Carolina Jan 22, Nevada Feb 1, New Hampshire Feb 8, New Mexico Feb 15, Michigan Feb 22, Virginia Feb 29, 2028. (as_of: 2026-08-15; source: https://washingtonsun.com/democrats/dnc-new-presidential-primary-calendar-black-vote-south-carolina)
- Iowa was left out of the early window; Iowa Democratic chair Rita Hart objected that the calendar "has no representation of states with a majority of Central or Mountain time zones." Iowa had earlier threatened to hold a rogue caucus if excluded. (as_of: 2026-08-15; source: https://washingtonsun.com/democrats/dnc-new-presidential-primary-calendar-black-vote-south-carolina)
