# Chamber: 2026 Senate Control

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: Senate control and seat counts after the 2026 midterms. Covers the map, the path to a majority, nominees in the battleground races, ratings, forecast models and Kalshi prices.

## Claims

- Before the election the Senate has 53 Republicans, 45 Democrats and 2 independents (Sanders, King) who caucus with Democrats. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)
- 35 seats are up: 33 Class 2 seats plus special elections in Ohio (Vance's seat) and Florida (Rubio's seat). Republicans are defending 22 of them and Democrats 13. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)
- Control takes 51 seats, or 50 seats plus the vice presidency. With VP JD Vance a Republican, Democrats need a net gain of 4 to reach 51. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)
- Eleven senators (4 D, 7 R) are not seeking re-election, the most since 1996. Open R seats: AL, IA, KY, MT, NC, OK, WY. Open D seats: IL, MI, MN, NH. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)
- Sen. Bill Cassidy (R-LA) lost the Louisiana Senate GOP primary, and a Trump-backed challenger advanced to the runoff. (as_of: 2026-05-16; source: https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/5876242-cassidy-fails-louisiana-gop-primary-letlow-fleming/)
- Ken Paxton defeated Sen. John Cornyn in the May 26 Texas GOP runoff. State Rep. James Talarico is the Democratic nominee after beating Rep. Jasmine Crockett. (as_of: 2026-05-26; source: https://apnews.com/live/election-primary-texas-runoff-05-26-2026)
- North Carolina's open Tillis seat is Democrat Roy Cooper against Republican Michael Whatley, both nominated in the March 3 primary. (as_of: 2026-03-03; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/cooper-whatley-north-carolina-senate-primary-wins-election-race-rcna259989)
- Ohio special election: Sherrod Brown (D) faces appointed Sen. Jon Husted (R) after the May 5 primaries. (as_of: 2026-05-06; source: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/may/05/jon-husted-sherrod-brown-ohio-senate-primary)
- Maine Democratic nominee Graham Platner suspended his Senate bid on July 8, 2026 after sexual assault allegations, which he denied. (as_of: 2026-07-08; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/live-blog/graham-platner-maine-senate-election-trump-live-updates-rcna353556)
- Former state Senate President Troy Jackson replaced Platner as the Maine Democratic nominee against Sen. Susan Collins. He won the convention vote 566-5. (as_of: 2026-07-25; source: https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/07/25/troy-jackson-replaces-graham-platner-maine-senate-democrat-nominee/91020463007/)
- In New Hampshire's open seat, Rep. Chris Pappas (D) and former Sen. John E. Sununu (R) won their Sep 8 primaries, beating Karishma Manzur and Scott Brown. (as_of: 2026-09-08; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/chris-pappas-john-sununu-new-hampshire-senate-primary-winner-rcna596479)
- Iowa open seat: Rep. Ashley Hinson (R) won the June 2 primary, and state Rep. Josh Turek won the Democratic nomination over Zach Wahls. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)
- Michigan open seat: Abdul El-Sayed (D), who narrowly beat Haley Stevens in the primary, faces former Rep. Mike Rogers (R). (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)
- Alaska's Aug 18 top-four primary advanced Sen. Dan S. Sullivan (R), former Rep. Mary Peltola (D), Gerald Heikes (R) and a second "Dan J. Sullivan." The general election uses ranked-choice voting. (as_of: 2026-09-01; source: https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/09/01/election-2026-20-candidates-drop-out-alaska-races-withdrawal-deadline-hits/)
- Sen. Lindsey Graham (R-SC) died on July 11, 2026. His appointed sister, Darline Graham, won the Aug 25 GOP runoff against Rep. Ralph Norman 52-48 and faces Democrat Annie Andrews. (as_of: 2026-08-25; source: https://apnews.com/article/south-carolina-election-lindsey-graham-03899983c3702842039e99d62e16ddad)
- In Nebraska, independent Dan Osborn is the main challenger to Sen. Pete Ricketts. The Democratic nominee, Cindy Burbank, withdrew on July 17. (as_of: 2026-07-17; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)
- Cook Senate ratings as of Oct 6, 2026: Toss-ups are AK, IA, KS, ME, MI, OH and TX. NC (open R seat) is Likely D, a flip. GA (Ossoff) is Likely D, and NH and MN are Lean D and Likely D. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball Senate ratings as of Oct 6, 2026: Toss-ups are IA, ME, OH and TX. AK and NC are Lean D flips, MI is Lean D, and KS is Lean R. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)
- Inside Elections Senate ratings as of Oct 1, 2026: Toss-ups are AK, ME, NH and OH. IA and TX are Tilt R, MI is Tilt D, and NC is a Lean D flip. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections)

## Resolution notes

- CONTROLS-2026 can resolve early on a consensus of media calls. Otherwise it settles on the party of the President pro tempore on Feb 1, 2027. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/CONTROLS-2026?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXDSENATESEATS-27 counts Democratic seats on Feb 1, 2027, and independents who caucus with a party count with that party (for example, Sanders counts as D). An independent who caucuses with neither party, such as Osborn if he won, would not add to the D count. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXDSENATESEATS-27?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXCLOSESTSENATE-27JAN03 uses the absolute gap between first and second place. Ranked-choice races such as Alaska use the final round. Preliminary results count if 95% of the expected vote is in by Jan 3, 2027. Exact ties split the payout, and postponed races resolve No. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXCLOSESTSENATE-27JAN03?with_nested_markets=true)
