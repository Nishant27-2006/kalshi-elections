# Race: 2026 Michigan Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open-seat Michigan governor race (Whitmer term-limited): Democrat Jocelyn Benson vs Republican John James, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Gov. Gretchen Whitmer (D) is term-limited. (as_of: 2026-08-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Michigan_gubernatorial_election)
- Former Detroit Mayor Mike Duggan ended his independent campaign for governor on May 21, 2026. (as_of: 2026-05-21; source: https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2026/05/21/mike-duggan-ends-independent-michigan-governor-campaign/90194574007/)
- August 4 primaries: Secretary of State Jocelyn Benson won the Democratic nomination with 83.2% over Sheriff Chris Swanson; Trump-endorsed Rep. John James won the GOP nomination with 50.1% over Perry Johnson. (as_of: 2026-08-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Michigan_gubernatorial_election)
- In September 2026 the Libertarian Party of Michigan disavowed its nominee, Anthony Hudson, after the withdrawal deadline, so his name stays on the ballot. (as_of: 2026-09-18; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Michigan_gubernatorial_election)
- Republicans have not won a Michigan governor race since 2014. (as_of: 2026-08-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Michigan_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: Whitmer (D) 54.5%, Tudor Dixon (R) 43.9%, about D+10.6. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Michigan by 1.42 points (Harris 48.31%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook rates the race Lean D and Inside Elections Tilt D (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Lean D (Oct 6, 2026 table). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Marist, Sep 24–27, 1,235 RV, ±3.8: Benson 53, James 41. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://maristpoll.marist.edu/polls/midterm-elections-in-michigan-september-2026/)
- Fox News (Beacon/Shaw), Sep 24–28, 1,028 LV: Benson 54, James 45; the same poll had El-Sayed (D) 50, Rogers (R) 49 for Senate. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/fox-news-poll-michigan-voters-split-between-el-sayed-rogers)
