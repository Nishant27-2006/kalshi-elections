# Race: 2026 Texas Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open-seat Texas Senate race (Cornyn lost renomination): Republican Ken Paxton vs Democrat James Talarico, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- In the March 3 GOP primary, Cornyn led with 42.0% to Paxton's 40.5% and Wesley Hunt's 13.5%, forcing a runoff. (as_of: 2026-03-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Texas)
- State Rep. James Talarico won the Democratic nomination outright with 52.4% over Rep. Jasmine Crockett. (as_of: 2026-03-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Texas)
- AG Ken Paxton beat four-term Sen. John Cornyn in the May 26 GOP runoff. The AP called it shortly after 8 p.m. Trump had endorsed Paxton about a week before the vote. (as_of: 2026-05-26; source: https://www.texastribune.org/2026/05/26/texas-john-cornyn-ken-paxton-us-senate-republican-primary-runoff/)
- Paxton took 63.8% in the runoff. Cornyn became the first Texas senator to lose renomination since Ralph Yarborough in 1970. (as_of: 2026-05-27; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Texas)
- The general-election ballot also includes Libertarian Ted Brown. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Texas)
- FEC totals through June 30, 2026: Talarico raised $68.56M with $21.55M cash on hand. Paxton raised $9.25M with $1.76M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=TX&election_full=true)

- Rasmussen Reports, Sep 28–29, 1,128 LV: Talarico 46, Paxton 45. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://www.rasmussenreports.com/public_content/politics/public_surveys/toplines_texas_senate_september_28_29_2026)

## Resolution notes

- SENATETX-26 is a party market. "If a representative of the Republican [Democratic] party is sworn in as a Senator of Texas for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATETX-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Both markets can resolve early once a consensus of media organizations projects the winner. Expected expiration is 2027-01-04. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATETX-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- The market is about party, not person. A Libertarian win (Ted Brown) would resolve both listed sides No. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATETX-26?with_nested_markets=true)
