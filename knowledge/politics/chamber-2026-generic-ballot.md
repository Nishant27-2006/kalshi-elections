## Resolution notes

- The margin is the vote share of all Democratic House candidates minus the share of all Republican House candidates in the general election. Ranges include their lower bound and exclude their upper bound, with no rounding: 5.99999 falls in 5-6, and exactly 6 falls in 6-7. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXHOUSEPOPVOTEMARGIN-27NOV03?with_nested_markets=true)
- The market settles "at the release of the national House popular vote data." Its close and expiration are listed as Nov 3, 2027, so settlement may wait for official compiled totals. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXHOUSEPOPVOTEMARGIN-27NOV03?with_nested_markets=true)
