# Race: 2026 North Carolina Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open Republican-held seat (Thom Tillis retiring): former Gov. Roy Cooper (D) vs former RNC chair Michael Whatley (R), November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Incumbent Thom Tillis (R) is not running again. He withdrew after voting against the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_North_Carolina)
- In the March 3 primaries, Trump-endorsed Whatley won 64.6% over Don Brown and Cooper won 92%. (as_of: 2026-03-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_North_Carolina)
- The general-election ballot also lists Libertarian Shannon Bray and Green Michael Dublin. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_North_Carolina)
- FEC totals through June 30, 2026: Cooper raised $34.98M with $20.72M on hand. Whatley raised $11.27M with $3.46M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=NC&election_full=true)
- AARP (Fabrizio Ward/Impact Research), Sep 17–20, 1,806 LV: Cooper 53, Whatley 42. (as_of: 2026-09-28; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_North_Carolina)
- Big Data Poll, Sep 27–29, 642 LV: Cooper 47, Whatley 35; with leaners, Cooper 51, Whatley 39. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://www.bigdatapoll.com/blog/tar-heel-state-poll-roy-cooper-holds-solid-lead-over-michael-whatley-for-us-senate-in-north-carolina/)
- Inside Elections moved North Carolina from Tilt Democrat to Lean Democrat (a Democratic flip). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- Per CNN, an Elon University/YouGov poll released in September had Cooper up 11 among likely voters. CNN ranks NC the seat most likely to flip. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- Per CNN, the Senate Leadership Fund is pausing its NC ad campaign, and Republican outside groups cut more than $15M from their remaining bookings (AdImpact). (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- Cook Political Report moved North Carolina from Lean Democrat to Likely Democrat. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6132331-cook-political-report-democrats-shift/)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rated NC Lean D (flip) as of Oct 6. Silver Bulletin had Solid D and Split Ticket Safe D as of Oct 7. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_North_Carolina)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won NC 50.86% to 47.65% (R+3.21). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Trump won NC 49.93% to 48.59% (R+1.35). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Tillis (R) 48.69%, Cal Cunningham (D) 46.94%. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_North_Carolina)
- Democrats have not won a North Carolina Senate election since 2008. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_North_Carolina)
- Kalshi SENATENC-26 on Oct 9: D (Cooper) bid/ask 0.968/0.969; R (Whatley) 0.030/0.031. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATENC-26?with_nested_markets=true)
