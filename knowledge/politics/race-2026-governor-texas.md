# Race: 2026 Texas Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Republican incumbent Greg Abbott seeks a fourth term against Democratic state Rep. Gina Hinojosa, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Abbott (R) is seeking a fourth term; if re-elected he would become Texas's longest-serving governor. (as_of: 2025-11-09; source: https://www.texastribune.org/2025/11/09/texas-greg-abbott-reelection-campaign-governor/)
- March 3, 2026 primaries: Abbott won the GOP nomination with 82%; Hinojosa won the Democratic nomination with 59% over a field including ex-Rep. Chris Bell. (as_of: 2026-03-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Texas_gubernatorial_election)
- Democrats have not won a Texas governor race since 1990. (as_of: 2026-03-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Texas_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: Abbott (R) 54.8%, Beto O'Rourke (D) 43.9%, about R+10.9. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Texas by 13.68 points (Harris 42.46%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook rates the race Lean R and Inside Elections Likely R (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Lean R (Oct 6, 2026 table); RealClearPolitics lists it as a Tossup (Oct 5). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Big Data Poll, Sep 24–26, 698 LV: Abbott 49, Hinojosa 44. (as_of: 2026-09-27; source: https://www.bigdatapoll.com/blog/lone-star-state-poll-talarico-paxton-in-close-race-us-senate-in-texas/)
