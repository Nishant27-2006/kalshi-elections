# Race: 2026 Kansas Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Roger Marshall (R) seeks a second term against Democratic pastor Adam Hamilton, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- In the Aug 4 primaries, Marshall won the GOP nomination against minimal opposition. Hamilton won the Democratic nomination with 34.9% in a multi-candidate field. (as_of: 2026-08-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Kansas)
- Libertarian David Graham is also on the general-election ballot. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Kansas)
- FEC totals through July 15, 2026 (pre-primary): Marshall raised $6.21M with $5.39M on hand. Hamilton raised $4.17M with $1.73M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-23; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Kansas)
- Inside Elections moved Kansas from Solid Republican to Likely Republican. (as_of: 2026-09-03; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- Emerson College/Nexstar, Sep 15–16, 750 LV: Hamilton 45, Marshall 43, Graham (L) 4. Same poll: Marshall favorability 41/48, Trump approval 40/53. (as_of: 2026-09-18; source: https://emersoncollegepolling.com/kansas-2026/)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball moved Marshall from Likely Republican to Leans Republican. (as_of: 2026-09-22; source: https://centerforpolitics.org/crystalball/rating-changes-toward-democrats-across-the-board-mi-sen-to-leans-d-other-key-statewide-races-to-toss-up/)
- Cook Political Report shifted Kansas toward Democrats in its Sept 23 update. (as_of: 2026-09-23; source: https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/congressional/4738956/cook-political-report-senate-shift-democrats/)
- Kansas Reflector reported that Marshall alleged defamation over an ad about a former patient who was arrested while in medical debt. (as_of: 2026-09-28; source: https://kansasreflector.com/2026/09/28/roger-marshall-alleges-defamation-in-new-ad-about-former-kansas-patient-arrested-while-in-debt/)
- Trafalgar Group (R), Sep 29–Oct 1, 1,095 LV: Marshall 44, Hamilton 44, Graham 2. (as_of: 2026-10-02; source: https://www.thetrafalgargroup.org/news/ks-senate-general-1001/)
- NYT/Siena, late September, likely voters: Hamilton 45, Marshall 45, Graham (L) 3. (as_of: 2026-10-03; source: https://sri.siena.edu/2026/10/03/nytimes-siena-poll-of-likely-voters-senate-races/)
- Per CNN, Hamilton is a Republican-turned-Democrat megachurch pastor. Former Gov. Mike Hayden became the second former Republican Kansas governor to back him, and the Senate Leadership Fund will spend millions to defend Marshall. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- GBAO (D), Sep 30–Oct 4, 600 LV: Hamilton 46, Marshall 41, Graham 7. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://static.politico.com/37/ce/d434188d4d2b83a7e3c9bcf2427c/gbao-hamilton-polling-memo-100626.pdf)
- Cook Political Report moved Kansas from Lean Republican to Toss Up. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://www.mediaite.com/politics/new-cook-political-report-officially-declares-toss-up-senate-race-in-kansas-where-a-democrat-hasnt-won-in-94-years/)
- YouGov, Sep 24–Oct 4, 2,255 LV, MoE ±3.2: Hamilton 48, Marshall 45. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.newsweek.com/democrats-flip-senate-seats-midterms-poll-12535052)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Kansas 57.16% to 41.04% (R+16.12). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Trump won Kansas 56.14% to 41.51% (R+14.64). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Marshall (R) 53.22%, Barbara Bollier (D) 41.79%. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_Kansas)
- Democrats have not won a Kansas Senate election since 1932. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Kansas)
