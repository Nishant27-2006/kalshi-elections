# Nomination: 2028 Republican

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: the 2028 Republican presidential and vice-presidential nominations and who formally runs: Vance, Rubio, and others; national polls; Trump's statements on succession; and the 22nd Amendment.

## Claims

### Constitutional constraint
- 22nd Amendment, Section 1: "No person shall be elected to the office of the President more than twice." Ratified Feb 27, 1951. (as_of: 1951-02-27; source: https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/amendments-11-27)

### Trump statements on succession and a third term
- Trump on Vance as heir to the MAGA movement: "Well, I think most likely. In all fairness, he's the vice president, and I think Marco is also somebody that maybe would get together with JD in some form." He said Vance "would be probably favored at this point." (as_of: 2025-08-05; source: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-says-jd-vance-would-probably-favored-2028-republican-presidential-nomination)
- Trump privately teased Vance and Rubio: "Which one of you is going to be at the top of the ticket? I used to think it would be Vance-Rubio, but maybe it will be Rubio-Vance." A White House official called it a joke. (as_of: 2025-08-11; source: https://wlos.com/news/nation-world/trump-privately-teases-rubio-could-rival-vance-for-2028-gop-nomination)
- Asked about running for vice president in 2028, Trump said: "You'd be allowed to do that, but I wouldn't do that. I think it's too cute." He said a Vance–Rubio pairing "would be unstoppable." (as_of: 2025-10-27; source: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-answers-whether-he-would-run-vice-presidential-candidate-2028.amp)
- On Air Force One, Trump said of the 22nd Amendment: "If you read it, it's pretty clear — I'm not allowed to run. It's too bad." (as_of: 2025-10-29; source: https://www.axios.com/2025/10/29/trump-third-term-2028-ruled-out)
- On the Pod Force One podcast, Trump said of Vance and Rubio: "I don't know how you beat them if they're together. That would be a great team." He added that the decision rests with them. (as_of: 2026-06-03; source: https://www.mediaite.com/media/tv/trump-says-hes-been-studying-rubio-and-vance-to-see-if-they-like-each-other-ahead-of-2028/)
- Asia Business Daily reported that Trump said on July 24, 2026: "I am going to run for a fourth term as President of the United States." The paper listed this as one of his repeated third-term references. (as_of: 2026-07-27; source: https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026072709330592911)

### Vance and Rubio statements
- Rubio to Vanity Fair: "If J.D. Vance runs for president, he's going to be our nominee. And I'll be one of the first people to support him." (as_of: 2025-12-16; source: https://www.theamericanconservative.com/report-rubio-says-vance-will-be-2028-nominee-if-he-runs/)
- Vance to CBS on 2028: "Usha and I will absolutely sit down and talk about what comes next for our family." He also said: "I have no doubt that the president of the United States is going to be very supportive of anything that I ultimately decide to do." (as_of: 2026-06-14; source: https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/06/15/jd-vance-says-hell-discuss-2028-presidential-run-with-wife-after-midterms/)
