# Race: 2026 South Carolina Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Appointed Sen. Darline Graham (R), who replaced her late brother Lindsey Graham, faces Democrat Annie Andrews, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Pediatrician Annie Andrews won the Democratic nomination in the June 9 primary. (as_of: 2026-06-09; source: https://abcnews4.com/news/local/dr-annie-andrews-wins-democratic-nomination-for-us-senate)
- Lindsey Graham won the June 9 GOP primary with 56.8%. (as_of: 2026-06-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_South_Carolina)
- NBC reported that Sen. Lindsey Graham died of an aortic dissection Saturday night (July 11). Gov. Henry McMaster appoints a successor to serve until January 3. A Republican special primary to pick a new nominee was expected by Aug 11. (as_of: 2026-07-12; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/sen-lindsey-graham-dies-71-brief-sudden-illness-rcna552722)
- Aug 11 GOP special primary first round: Darline Graham 32.8%, Rep. Ralph Norman 24.6%, Rep. Russell Fry 19.7%, Mark Sanford 14.9%. (as_of: 2026-08-11; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_South_Carolina)
- Per PBS, Darline Graham was appointed to her brother's seat as interim senator. Trump endorsed her and campaigned for her before the runoff, and his super PAC put in nearly $830,000 in the final week. (as_of: 2026-08-25; source: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/darline-graham-ralph-norman-go-head-to-head-in-south-carolinas-gop-senate-runoff)
- Darline Graham beat Norman in the Aug 25 runoff, about 52% to 48%. (as_of: 2026-08-25; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_South_Carolina)
- Libertarian Kasie Whitener and Constitution Party nominee Mark Hackett are also on the general-election ballot. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_South_Carolina)
- Fundraising per FEC data compiled by Wikipedia, as of Aug 5, 2026: Andrews raised $10.80M with $4.12M on hand. D. Graham raised $0.35M with $0.25M on hand. (as_of: 2026-08-13; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_South_Carolina)
