# Base Rate: Incumbency

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: how often House, Senate and governor incumbents who run again win, how that rate drops in wave years, and how often parties hold open seats.

## Claims

- In 2018, 52 House incumbents did not run (18 D, 34 R); 13 of those 52 open districts changed party, 10 R-to-D and 3 D-to-R. (as_of: 2019-02-11; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2019/02/11/woodall-r-is-third-u-s-house-incumbent-who-wont-run-for-re-election-how-many-seats-without-incumbents-flipped-in-2020/)
- In 2022, 28 governors ran for reelection and 27 won; the only loser was Steve Sisolak (D-NV). (as_of: 2023-02-28; source: https://governors.rutgers.edu/?p=11545)
