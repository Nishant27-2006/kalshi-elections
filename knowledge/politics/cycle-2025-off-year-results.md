# Cycle: 2025 Off-Year Election Results

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: 2025 NJ and VA governor races, NYC mayor, CA Proposition 50, the WI Supreme Court race and 2025 U.S. House specials, with results set against the final polling averages.

## Claims

### New Jersey governor (Nov 4, 2025)
- The NJ Board of State Canvassers scheduled its meeting to officially certify the Nov 4, 2025 governor result for Wednesday, December 3, 2025. (as_of: 2025-11-26; source: https://nj.gov/state/elections/assets/pdf/meeting-notice/2025-1203-BOSC-Notice-Of-Public-Meeting.pdf)
- Certified result: Mikie Sherrill (D) 1,896,610 (56.88%) to Jack Ciattarelli (R) 1,417,705 (42.52%), a 14.36-point margin. (as_of: 2025-12-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_New_Jersey_gubernatorial_election)
- The final RCP Average had Sherrill up 3.3 points. Polls underestimated the Democratic margin by roughly 10 or more points. (as_of: 2025-11-06; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/stories/analysis/how-polls-undercounted-democratic-support-on-tuesday)
- RCP noted that no October poll showed Sherrill ahead by more than 7 points. (as_of: 2025-11-06; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/stories/analysis/how-polls-undercounted-democratic-support-on-tuesday)
- Decision Desk HQ: aggregators had Sherrill leading by only about 5 points before Election Day. NJ turnout was about 51% of the voting-eligible population, the highest for a non-presidential NJ election in more than 40 years. (as_of: 2025-11-10; source: https://decisiondeskhq.substack.com/p/virginia-and-new-jerseys-elections)
- Decision Desk HQ also recorded that NJ's polling error has flipped direction between cycles. In 2021, aggregators showed Phil Murphy well ahead and he won by about 3 points. (as_of: 2025-11-10; source: https://decisiondeskhq.substack.com/p/virginia-and-new-jerseys-elections)

### Virginia governor (Nov 4, 2025)
- The Virginia State Board of Elections certified the 2025 general election results on December 1, 2025. Democrats won governor (Spanberger), lieutenant governor (Hashmi) and attorney general (Jay Jones). (as_of: 2025-12-01; source: https://digital-release.wric.com/news/politics/local-election-hq/virginia-certifies-2025-general-election-results)
- Certified result: Abigail Spanberger (D) 1,976,857 (57.58%) to Winsome Earle-Sears (R) 1,449,586 (42.22%), a margin of about 15.4 points. (as_of: 2025-12-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Virginia_gubernatorial_election)
- The final RCP Average had Spanberger up 10.2 points, about 5 points short of her margin. (as_of: 2025-11-06; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/stories/analysis/how-polls-undercounted-democratic-support-on-tuesday)
- In the VA attorney general race, RCP said surveys showed Jay Jones ahead by at most 2 points, with most tied or showing Miyares ahead. (as_of: 2025-11-06; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/stories/analysis/how-polls-undercounted-democratic-support-on-tuesday)
- A December review found Democrats were underestimated in all three VA statewide races. Hashmi won LG by 11.6 and Jones won AG by 6.6. (as_of: 2025-12-31; source: https://thedemocraticstrategist.org/2025/12/how-did-political-polls-do-in-2025/)

### New York City mayor
- In the June 24, 2025 Democratic primary, the final ranked-choice round was Zohran Mamdani 573,169 (56.39%) to Andrew Cuomo 443,229 (43.61%). (as_of: 2025-07-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_New_York_City_mayoral_election)
- On election eve, the RCP average had Mamdani at 46.1%, Cuomo at 31.8% and Sliwa at 16.3%, a Mamdani lead of 14.3 points over Cuomo. (as_of: 2025-11-04; source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/11/3/what-the-latest-polls-are-showing-in-the-mamdani-vs-cuomo-nyc-mayoral-race)
- The NYC Board of Elections certified the general result on Tuesday, December 2, 2025. (as_of: 2025-12-02; source: https://vote.nyc/sites/default/files/pdf/election_results/2025/20251104General%20Election/00000100000Citywide%20Mayor%20Citywide%20Recap.pdf)
- Certified general result: Mamdani (D) 1,114,184 (50.78%), Cuomo (Ind.) 906,614 (41.32%), Sliwa (R) 153,749 (7.01%). Mamdani's 9.5-point margin over Cuomo was smaller than the 14.3-point RCP lead. (as_of: 2025-12-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_New_York_City_mayoral_election)
- Review of polling: three-way NYC polls overstated Mamdani's winning margin, and two-way polls better captured Cuomo's late consolidation. (as_of: 2025-12-31; source: https://thedemocraticstrategist.org/2025/12/how-did-political-polls-do-in-2025/)

### California Proposition 50 (Nov 4, 2025 special)
- The final PPIC survey, released Oct 28, 2025, had Prop 50 at 56% Yes and 43% No among likely voters. (as_of: 2025-10-28; source: https://gvwire.com/2025/10/28/majority-of-californians-back-prop-50-poll-shows-record-interest/)
- The Secretary of State certified the Nov 4, 2025 special election on December 12, 2025. Turnout was 11,584,393 ballots (50.16% of registered voters). (as_of: 2025-12-12; source: https://www.sos.ca.gov/administration/news-releases-and-advisories/2025-news-releases-and-advisories/secretary-state-shirley-n-weber-phd-has-certified-results-california-november-4-2025-statewide-special-election)
- Certified Prop 50 result: Yes 7,453,339 (64.42%), No 4,116,998 (35.58%). That is a 28.8-point margin, against 13 points in the final PPIC poll. (as_of: 2025-12-12; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_California_Proposition_50)

### Other 2025 contests and specials
- Wisconsin Supreme Court (Apr 1, 2025): liberal-backed Susan Crawford beat Brad Schimel by more than 8 points on unofficial results, in a race that cost nearly $99M. (as_of: 2025-04-02; source: https://pbswisconsin.org/news-item/crawford-wins-the-2025-wisconsin-supreme-court-election/)
- FL-1 and FL-6 specials (Apr 1, 2025): Republicans Patronis and Fine both won but trailed Trump's 2024 share. Trump took about 65% in FL-6 in 2024, and Fine ran about 10 points behind Waltz. (as_of: 2025-04-02; source: https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2025/04/02/republicans-win-florida-special-elections-in-trump-strongholds-but-by-significantly-narrower-margins-than-in-2024/)
- AZ-7 special (Sep 23, 2025): Adelita Grijalva (D) won 68.92% to 29.42% on 23.18% turnout. (as_of: 2025-09-23; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Arizona%27s_7th_congressional_district_special_election)
- TN-7 special (Dec 2, 2025): Matt Van Epps (R) won 53.8% to Aftyn Behn (D) 45.2%. (as_of: 2025-12-04; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2025/12/04/matt-van-epps-r-won-the-special-election-for-tennessees-7th-congressional-district/)
- In TN-7, Democrats ran 12.6 points ahead of their 2024 margin in a district rated R+10 by Cook PVI. (as_of: 2025-12-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Tennessee%27s_7th_congressional_district_special_election)
- TX-18 special runoff (Jan 31, 2026): Christian Menefee beat Amanda Edwards (both D) with 67% when AP called it. The win narrowed the House to 218-214. (as_of: 2026-01-31; source: https://www.texastribune.org/2026/01/31/christian-menefee-wins-special-election-runoff-texas-18th-congressional-district/)
