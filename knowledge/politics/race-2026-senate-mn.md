# Race: 2026 Minnesota Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open DFL-held seat (Tina Smith retiring): Lt. Gov. Peggy Flanagan (DFL) vs former broadcaster Michele Tafoya (R), November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Incumbent Tina Smith (DFL) is not seeking a second full term. It is Minnesota's first Senate race without an incumbent since 2006. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota)
- In the Aug 11 primaries, Flanagan beat Rep. Angie Craig with 59%. Tafoya won 52.1% over Adam Schwarze and Royce White. (as_of: 2026-08-11; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota)
- The general-election ballot also lists independent Marisa Simonetti and Libertarian Rebecca Whiting. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota)
- FEC totals through July 22, 2026 (pre-primary): Flanagan raised $6.52M with $1.06M on hand. Tafoya raised $5.34M with $1.37M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-30; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=MN&election_full=true)
- Quantus Insights (R), Sep 8–10, 720 LV: Flanagan 48, Tafoya 44. (as_of: 2026-09-11; source: https://quantusinsights.org/f/new-quantus-polls-moody-leads-in-florida-flanagan-in-minnesota)
- KSTP/SurveyUSA, Sep 9–14, 654 LV: Flanagan 42, Tafoya 42, 12% undecided. That was down from a 5-point Flanagan lead in the post-primary poll. (as_of: 2026-09-16; source: https://kstp.com/kstp-news/top-news/kstp-surveyusa-poll-governor-senate-races-tightening-days-before-early-voting-starts-in-minnesota/)
- InsiderAdvantage (R), Sep 28–29, 1,200 LV: Flanagan 46, Tafoya 45. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://insideradvantage.com/insideradvantage-survey-u-s-senate-minnesota-contest-a-one-point-race-flanagan-46-tafoya-45/)
- Inside Elections moved Minnesota from Likely Democrat to Lean Democrat. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- Big Data Poll (R), Sep 29–30, 622 LV: Flanagan 40, Tafoya 38; with leaners, Flanagan 46, Tafoya 44. (as_of: 2026-10-02; source: https://www.bigdatapoll.com/blog/north-star-state-poll-peggy-flanagan-holds-slight-lead-over-michele-tafoya-for-us-senate-in-minnesota/)
- CNN lists Minnesota among long-shot GOP targets and notes Harris carried the state by 4 points in 2024. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- Cook Political Report lists Minnesota (open) as Likely Democrat. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rated Minnesota Likely D as of Oct 6. RealClearPolitics had it as a Tossup as of Oct 5. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota)
- 2024 presidential: Harris won Minnesota 50.92% to 46.68% (D+4.24). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Biden won Minnesota 52.40% to 45.28% (D+7.11). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Smith (DFL) 48.74%, Jason Lewis (R) 43.50%, Kevin O'Connor (Legal Marijuana Now) 5.91%. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota)
- Republicans have not won a Minnesota Senate election since 2002. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota)
