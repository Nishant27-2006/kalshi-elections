# Race: 2026 Florida Senate Special

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Special election for the rest of Marco Rubio's term: appointed Sen. Ashley Moody (R) vs state Rep. Angie Nixon (D), November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Ashley Moody, appointed by Gov. Ron DeSantis, was sworn in as US senator on Jan 21, 2025, replacing Marco Rubio, who became secretary of state. (as_of: 2025-01-21; source: https://floridaphoenix.com/2025/01/21/ashley-moody-sworn-in-as-u-s-senator-while-marco-rubio-takes-over-at-state/)
- The special-election winner completes Rubio's term. A regular election for the full term follows in 2028. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Florida)
- On Aug 18, progressive state Rep. Angie Nixon upset DSCC-recruited Alex Vindman in the Democratic primary. Vindman had raised over $16M to Nixon's under $1M. Moody easily won her primary the same day. (as_of: 2026-08-18; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/angie-nixon-winner-florida-senate-primary-democrat-alex-vindman-moody-rcna593255)
- Nixon won the Democratic primary with 56.1%. (as_of: 2026-08-18; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Florida)
- Independent Neil Gillespie is also on the general-election ballot. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Florida)
- Fundraising per FEC data compiled by Wikipedia, as of July 29, 2026: Moody raised $11.26M with $8.48M on hand. Nixon raised $0.97M with $0.26M on hand. (as_of: 2026-08-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Florida)
- Quantus Insights (R), Sep 8–10, 733 LV: Moody 50, Nixon 43. (as_of: 2026-09-11; source: https://quantusinsights.org/f/new-quantus-polls-moody-leads-in-florida-flanagan-in-minnesota)
- St. Pete Polls, Sep 15–17, 913 LV: Moody 45, Nixon 45. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: http://stpetepolls.org/files/StPetePolls_2026_State_Gov_September17_G7V29XU6.pdf)
- InsiderAdvantage (R), Sep 20–21, 600 LV: Moody 49, Nixon 42. (as_of: 2026-09-22; source: https://insideradvantage.com/fl-u-s-senate-survey-top-line-tabs/)
- Stetson University, Sep 14–21, 830 LV: Moody 51, Nixon 40. (as_of: 2026-09-24; source: https://www.stetson.edu/artsci/cpor/media/cpor-sept.-2026-poll-final.pdf)
- YouGov, Sep 28–Oct 5, 3,813 LV, MoE ±2.7: Moody 49, Nixon 44. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.newsweek.com/democrats-flip-senate-seats-midterms-poll-12535052)
- St. Pete Polls, Oct 5–7, 836 LV: Moody 47, Nixon 45. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.stpetepolls.org/files/StPetePolls_2026_State_Gov_October7_K7ZU3GA.pdf)

- 2024 presidential: Trump won Florida 56.09% to 42.99% (R+13.10). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Trump won Florida 51.22% to 47.86% (R+3.36). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2022): Rubio (R) 57.68%, Val Demings (D) 41.27%. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_Senate_election_in_Florida)
