# International: France 2027 Presidential Election

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Covers the 2027 French presidential race: dates, Le Pen's eligibility after her appeal, the declared field, polls, and Kalshi pricing.

## Claims

- On April 19, 2026, The Republicans held a member vote in which more than 73% backed nominating party leader Bruno Retailleau outright. (as_of: 2026-04-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_French_presidential_election)
- The government set the first round for April 18, 2027 and the runoff for May 2, 2027. (as_of: 2026-07-01; source: https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/07/01/france-sets-presidential-election-dates)
- Emmanuel Macron cannot run again after two terms. (as_of: 2026-07-01; source: https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/07/01/france-sets-presidential-election-dates)
- On July 7, 2026, the Paris Court of Appeal upheld Marine Le Pen's embezzlement conviction. It cut her ineligibility from five years to 45 months, 30 of them suspended, and imposed three years' imprisonment: two suspended and one served under electronic monitoring. (as_of: 2026-07-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_French_presidential_election)
- Under the appeal judgment Le Pen will be eligible in April 2027, because she has already served the 15-month ban that had run since the first-instance ruling. (as_of: 2026-07-07; source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/07/french-court-opens-door-for-marine-le-pen-to-run-for-president-with-ankle-tag.html)
- After the ruling, Le Pen announced she would run for president and appeal the conviction again. (as_of: 2026-07-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_French_presidential_election)
- Cannes mayor David Lisnard left Les Républicains, called the party's nomination vote "rigged," and declared his own presidential candidacy. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://lcp.fr/actualites/je-suis-candidat-david-lisnard-quitte-lr-et-relance-le-debat-sur-une-primaire-a-droite)
- Listed contenders include Le Pen (RN), Gabriel Attal (Renaissance), Édouard Philippe (Horizons), Jean-Luc Mélenchon (LFI), Retailleau (LR), Éric Zemmour (Reconquête) and Raphaël Glucksmann, who is contesting a Socialist primary. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_French_presidential_election)
- Cluster17 (Sep 15-16, n=1,943) had Le Pen 31%, Mélenchon 19.5%, Philippe 19% and Retailleau 7.5% in the first round. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_for_the_2027_French_presidential_election)
- YouGov (Sep 17-21, n=1,103) had Le Pen 34%, Mélenchon 17%, Philippe 12% and Retailleau 8% in the first round. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_for_the_2027_French_presidential_election)
- Toluna Harris Interactive for RTL/M6 (Sep 22-24, n=1,974) had Le Pen at 35-36% in the first round, Mélenchon about 18%, Philippe about 13%, Attal 12-13% and Retailleau 8-10%, depending on scenario. (as_of: 2026-09-28; source: https://fr.euronews.com/2026/09/28/presidentielle-2027-vers-un-duel-le-pen-melenchon-selon-un-sondage)
- In the same Harris Interactive poll, Le Pen won the runoff 57% to 43% against Philippe and 69% to 31% against Mélenchon. (as_of: 2026-09-28; source: https://fr.euronews.com/2026/09/28/presidentielle-2027-vers-un-duel-le-pen-melenchon-selon-un-sondage)
