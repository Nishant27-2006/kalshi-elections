# Race: 2028 New Hampshire Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Kalshi lists GOVPARTYNH-28 under a 2028 ticker, but its rules and listed candidates refer to the November 3, 2026 race: Gov. Kelly Ayotte (R) against Democrat Cinde Warmington.

## Claims

- 2024 result: Ayotte 53.61%, Joyce Craig (D) 44.27%. (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_New_Hampshire_gubernatorial_election)
- New Hampshire has voted Democratic for president in every race since 2004 and has had an all-Democratic congressional delegation since 2017. Republicans hold the statewide trifecta and a supermajority in the state Senate. (as_of: 2024-11-07; source: https://www.seacoastonline.com/story/news/politics/elections/2024/11/07/nh-voters-republicans-state-government-democrats-washington/76085089007/)
- New Hampshire governors serve two-year terms. Ayotte is running for a second. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_Hampshire_gubernatorial_election)
- Ayotte won the Sep 8 Republican primary with 91.9% (100,920 votes). (as_of: 2026-09-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_Hampshire_gubernatorial_election)
- Former Executive Councilor Cinde Warmington won the Democratic primary with 126,223 votes, with only write-ins (4,788) against her. Jon Kiper had left the Democratic race on Apr 13 to run as an independent, then later withdrew. (as_of: 2026-09-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_Hampshire_gubernatorial_election)
- Libertarian Stephen Villee is also on the ballot. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_Hampshire_gubernatorial_election)
- UNH Survey Center, Aug 20–24, 1,878 LV: Ayotte 49, Warmington 39. (as_of: 2026-08-27; source: https://scholars.unh.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1990&context=survey_center_polls)
