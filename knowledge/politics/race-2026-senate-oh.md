# Race: 2026 Ohio Senate Special

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Special election for the rest of JD Vance's term: appointed Sen. Jon Husted (R) vs former Sen. Sherrod Brown (D), November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- The special election fills the rest of the term JD Vance won in 2022. Vance resigned in 2025 to become vice president. It is Ohio's first Senate special election since 1954. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Ohio)
- Husted, a former lieutenant governor, was appointed to the seat Vance vacated. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/08/politics/cnn-polls-ohio-iowa-senate-governor-midterms)
- In the May 5 primaries, Husted was unopposed for the GOP nomination and Brown won the Democratic nomination against minimal opposition. (as_of: 2026-05-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Ohio)
- The general-election ballot also lists Libertarian Bill Redpath and independent Greg Levy. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Ohio)
- FEC totals through June 30, 2026: Brown raised $38.57M with $16.23M on hand. Husted raised $14.36M with $9.42M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=OH&election_full=true)
- Big Data Poll, Sep 26–27, 680 LV: Brown 46, Husted 43. (as_of: 2026-09-29; source: https://www.bigdatapoll.com/blog/buckeye-state-poll-sherrod-brown-leads-jon-husted-for-us-senate-in-ohio/)
- InsiderAdvantage (R), Sep 28–29, 1,200 LV: Brown 44, Husted 43. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://insideradvantage.com/insideradvantage-ohio-senate-race-narrows-to-brown-up-by-one/)
- NYT/Siena, late September, likely voters: Brown 49, Husted 46. (as_of: 2026-10-03; source: https://sri.siena.edu/2026/10/03/nytimes-siena-poll-of-likely-voters-senate-races/)
- CNN says a September Marist poll of Ohio registered voters had Brown up 8. In the NYT/Siena poll, favorability was Brown 46% and Husted 37%. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- YouGov, Sep 18–30, 3,780 LV, MoE ±2.5: Brown 49, Husted 45. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.newsweek.com/democrats-flip-senate-seats-midterms-poll-12535052)
- CNN/SSRS, Sep 29–Oct 5, 760 LV, MoE ±4.5: Brown leads Husted by 6 points. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/08/politics/cnn-polls-ohio-iowa-senate-governor-midterms)
- Same CNN/SSRS poll: Ohio LV split 52% to 48% for Democratic vs Republican Senate control. Trump approval in Ohio was 36%. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/08/politics/cnn-polls-ohio-iowa-senate-governor-midterms)
- Inside Elections moved Ohio from Tilt Republican to Toss-up. (as_of: 2026-09-03; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- Cook Political Report lists Ohio (Husted) as a Toss Up. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rated Ohio a Toss-up as of Oct 6. The Economist, Silver Bulletin, Split Ticket and FiftyPlusOne had Lean D (flip) as of Oct 7. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Ohio)
- Per CNN, Republican groups plan to spend more than $80M from the start of October through Election Day, against more than $50M from Democrats. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Ohio 55.14% to 43.93% (R+11.21). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Trump won Ohio 53.27% to 45.24% (R+8.03). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2022): JD Vance (R) 53.03%, Tim Ryan (D) 46.92%. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_Senate_election_in_Ohio)
- In the 2024 race for Ohio's other seat, Bernie Moreno (R) beat incumbent Brown 50.09% to 46.47%. (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Senate_election_in_Ohio)
