# Mechanics: Vote-Count Timing and Red-Mirage / Blue-Shift Patterns

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: how fast six swing states count, what state law drives the speed, which direction late-counted votes have moved, and when the AP called recent races.

## Claims

### General pattern
- In 2020, several states including Arizona, Georgia, Michigan, Nevada and Pennsylvania looked red early in the count and then shifted blue. Urban Democratic counties with large mail-ballot batches report later than small rural counties. (as_of: 2024-10-07; source: https://www.opb.org/article/2024/10/07/why-the-electoral-college-maps-colors-can-change)
- Pennsylvania and Wisconsin do not allow mail ballots to be processed before Election Day. Arizona allows processing as ballots arrive, but many voters drop off mail ballots on Election Day itself. (as_of: 2024-10-07; source: https://www.opb.org/article/2024/10/07/why-the-electoral-college-maps-colors-can-change)

### Pennsylvania
- Pennsylvania law bars counties from starting to process ("pre-canvass") mail ballots until 7 a.m. on Election Day, one of seven states with that restriction. Mail ballots must arrive by 8 p.m. on Election Day. (as_of: 2024-10-21; source: https://www.spotlightpa.org/news/2024/10/pennsylvania-election-mail-ballots-precanvassing-101/)
- A House-passed bill to allow earlier pre-canvassing had not been acted on by the Republican-controlled state Senate as of October 2024. (as_of: 2024-10-21; source: https://www.spotlightpa.org/news/2024/10/pennsylvania-election-mail-ballots-precanvassing-101/)
- The AP called Pennsylvania for Trump at 2:24 a.m. EST on November 6, 2024, the morning after Election Day. (as_of: 2024-11-06; source: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/ap-race-call-donald-trump-wins-pennsylvania)

### Wisconsin
- Wisconsin is one of seven states, and one of two swing states, that do not begin processing absentee ballots before Election Day. (as_of: 2024-11-14; source: https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2024/nov/14/instagram-posts/no-an-early-morning-influx-of-milwaukee-votes-does/)
- Milwaukee's central count reports absentee results in one batch, usually between midnight and 4 a.m. Around 4 a.m. on November 6, 2024 it reported about 108,000 absentee ballots, and the Democratic Senate candidate won nearly 90% of them. (as_of: 2024-11-14; source: https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2024/nov/14/instagram-posts/no-an-early-morning-influx-of-milwaukee-votes-does/)
- An error in Milwaukee's initial tabulation in 2024 forced a retabulation of more than 30,000 ballots, which delayed reporting further. (as_of: 2024-11-14; source: https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2024/nov/14/instagram-posts/no-an-early-morning-influx-of-milwaukee-votes-does/)
- Trump passed 270 electoral votes when the AP called Wisconsin early on Wednesday, November 6, 2024. (as_of: 2024-11-10; source: https://www.gpb.org/news/2024/11/10/donald-trump-wins-arizona)

### Michigan
- In 2024, for the first time in a presidential election, Michigan clerks could pre-process absentee ballots up to eight days before Election Day (starting Monday, October 28, 2024): opening envelopes, checking ballots and scanning them through tabulators. Participation was voluntary, and totals could not be released until polls closed. (as_of: 2024-10-25; source: https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2024/10/26/michigan-clerks-to-begin-preprocessing-absentee-ballots-before-election-day)

### Georgia
- Georgia processes and scans early in-person and absentee ballots in advance and requires counties to report results by 8 p.m. on election night. Before the 2024 election, Secretary of State Raffensperger projected that 70-75% of all votes would be reported by 8 p.m. (as_of: 2024-11-01; source: https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2024/11/01/georgia-record-early-voting-could-mean-earlier-results-election-night/)

### Arizona
- In 2024, more than 225,000 early ballots were dropped off on Election Day in Maricopa County. These "late early" ballots need signature verification before they can be tabulated, so they cannot be counted on election night. (as_of: 2024-11-07; source: https://san.com/cc/why-arizona-and-nevada-are-taking-days-to-count-ballots/)
- Arizona law gives voters a five-day cure period for signature problems. Maricopa officials estimated the 2024 count would take 10-13 days. (as_of: 2024-11-07; source: https://san.com/cc/why-arizona-and-nevada-are-taking-days-to-count-ballots/)
- The AP called the 2022 Arizona Senate race for Mark Kelly on the night of Friday, November 11, 2022, three days after Election Day, when he led Masters 52% to 46%. (as_of: 2022-11-11; source: https://www.deseret.com/2022/11/11/23454503/arizona-senate-race-won-by-mark-kelly/)
- Arizona was the last state the AP called in the 2024 presidential race. It was called for Trump on Saturday, November 9, 2024, four days after Election Day. (as_of: 2024-11-10; source: https://www.gpb.org/news/2024/11/10/donald-trump-wins-arizona)

### Nevada
- In 2024, Nevada counted mail ballots postmarked by Election Day if they arrived through Saturday, November 9, and ballots without a clear postmark if they arrived by 5 p.m. Friday, November 8. Signature curing ran through November 12. (as_of: 2024-11-07; source: https://san.com/cc/why-arizona-and-nevada-are-taking-days-to-count-ballots/)
- In 2022, Laxalt's early Nevada Senate lead shrank as late ballots came in from Las Vegas and Reno. The AP called the race for Cortez Masto on Saturday, November 12, 2022, four days after Election Day. (as_of: 2022-11-12; source: https://www.fox5vegas.com/2022/11/12/senate-control-may-come-down-nevada-count-nears-end/)
- When the AP called Arizona's Senate race, Laxalt led Cortez Masto by about 800 votes on Friday night, November 11, 2022. (as_of: 2022-11-11; source: https://www.deseret.com/2022/11/11/23454503/arizona-senate-race-won-by-mark-kelly/)
- The AP called Nevada for Trump early Saturday, November 9, 2024, with a lead of about 46,000 votes. Harris would have needed about 85% of the remaining ballots, a share she had not reached in any update since polls closed. (as_of: 2024-11-09; source: https://www.kolotv.com/2024/11/09/why-ap-called-nevadas-presidential-race-trump/)

### Mail-ballot grace periods (national)
- On June 29, 2026, in Watson v. Republican National Committee, the Supreme Court held that federal Election Day statutes do not stop states from counting mail ballots postmarked by Election Day but received afterward. That leaves state grace periods in place for 2026. (as_of: 2026-07-14; source: https://www.swlaw.com/publication/supreme-court-holds-federal-election-day-statutes-do-not-preempt-state-receipt-deadlines-for-timely-cast-mail-ballots/)

## Resolution notes
- SENATEGA-26 and SENATEMI-26 resolve on who is sworn in for the term beginning in 2027 but are "eligible for accelerated determination after a consensus of media organizations project the winner". So the speed of the count matters through the media call, not certification. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEMI-26?with_nested_markets=true)
