# Race: 2026 Arizona Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Democratic incumbent Katie Hobbs seeks a second term against Republican Rep. Andy Biggs, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Hobbs (D) is seeking a second term and was unopposed in the July 21, 2026 primary; Biggs won the GOP nomination over Rep. David Schweikert with 73.6%. (as_of: 2026-07-21; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-primary-elections/arizona-governor-results)
- It is Arizona's first governor election with running mates, for the lieutenant governor office created by Proposition 131 (2022). (as_of: 2023-09-21; source: https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2023/09/21/arizona-will-elect-its-first-lieutenant-governor-in-2026-what-to-know/70848159007/)
- 2022 result: Hobbs (D) 50.3%, Kari Lake (R) 49.7%, about D+0.7. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Arizona by 5.53 points (Harris 46.69%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook rates the race Lean D and Inside Elections Tilt D (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Lean D (Oct 6, 2026 table). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
