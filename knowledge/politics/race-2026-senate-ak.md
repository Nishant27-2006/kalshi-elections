# Race: 2026 Alaska Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Dan Sullivan (R) seeks a third term against former Rep. Mary Peltola (D), November 3, 2026, under Alaska's top-four primary and ranked-choice general election.

## Claims

- PBS reported that Peltola, a former congresswoman, is challenging Sullivan. (as_of: 2026-01-12; source: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/democrat-mary-peltola-barrier-breaking-ex-alaska-congresswoman-is-challenging-republican-dan-sullivan-in-senate-race)
- In the Aug 18 nonpartisan top-four primary, Peltola took 49.5% and Sullivan 41.4%. Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. (R) and Gerald Heikes (R) also advanced to the RCV general. (as_of: 2026-08-18; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Alaska)
- Per CNN, the November contest is decided by ranked-choice voting. A second Republican named Dan Sullivan, a former schoolteacher, is on the ballot, and Republicans worry voters may confuse the two. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- FEC totals through July 29, 2026 (pre-primary): Peltola raised $18.55M with $4.64M on hand. Sullivan raised $11.74M with $7.30M on hand. (as_of: 2026-08-06; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=AK&election_full=true)
- Data for Progress (D), Jul 28–Aug 4, 605 LV, first choice: Peltola 45, Sullivan 41. (as_of: 2026-08-17; source: https://www.dataforprogress.org/blog/2026/8/17/as-alaskas-affordability-crisis-deepens-peltola-leads-in-senate-race)
- AARP (Fabrizio Ward/Impact Research), Sep 8–11, 800 LV: Peltola 45, Sullivan 41 in the first round. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: https://www.aarp.org/pri/topics/voter-research/politics/2026-midterm-election-poll-alaska/?CMP=RDRCT-PRI-POLT)
- Inside Elections moved Alaska from Tilt Republican to Toss-up. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- NYT/Siena, late September, likely voters: Peltola 50, Sullivan 43. (as_of: 2026-10-03; source: https://sri.siena.edu/2026/10/03/nytimes-siena-poll-of-likely-voters-senate-races/)
- Per CNN, Sullivan appeared with Trump in the Oval Office to tout a $54B South Korean investment in the Alaska LNG project. A NYT report detailed allegations of hostile behavior by Peltola toward former aides, which her campaign denied as a pattern. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball moved Alaska from Toss-up to Leans Democratic, which would be a pickup. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://centerforpolitics.org/crystalball/four-rating-changes-headlined-by-alaska-senate-moving-to-leans-democratic/)
- Cook Political Report lists Alaska (Sullivan) as a Toss Up. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Alaska 54.54% to 41.41% (R+13.13). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Trump won Alaska 52.83% to 42.77% (R+10.06). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020, before RCV): Sullivan (R) 53.90%, Al Gross (I, Democratic nominee) 41.19%. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_Alaska)
- Democrats have not won an Alaska Senate election since 2008. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Alaska)
- Kalshi SENATEAK-26 on Oct 9: D (Peltola) bid/ask 0.73/0.74; R 0.25/0.26. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEAK-26?with_nested_markets=true)

The R side would also pay if either of the other Republicans on the ballot won. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEAK-26?with_nested_markets=true)
