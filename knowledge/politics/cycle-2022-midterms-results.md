# Cycle: 2022 Midterm Results

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: 2022 House, Senate and governor outcomes, final seat counts, call dates, and how the polls and forecasts did. Used as a reference cycle for midterm priors.

## Claims

- AP projected Catherine Cortez Masto (D) the winner in Nevada late on Saturday, Nov 12, 2022, which gave Democrats the 50 seats they needed to keep the Senate (with the VP tiebreak). (as_of: 2022-11-12; source: https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2022/11/12/democrats-keep-control-of-us-senate-as-republican-push-falters-in-nevada/)
- Cortez Masto beat Republican Adam Laxalt, who had Trump's endorsement. (as_of: 2022-11-12; source: https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2022/11/12/democrats-keep-control-of-us-senate-as-republican-push-falters-in-nevada/)
- AP called Pennsylvania's Senate race for John Fetterman (D) over Mehmet Oz (R) at 12:51 a.m. ET on Nov 9, 2022, flipping the seat held by retiring Republican Pat Toomey. (as_of: 2022-11-09; source: https://www.axios.com/2022/11/09/john-fetterman-pennsylvania-election-results-senate)
- Official PA Senate count: Fetterman 2,751,012 to Oz 2,487,260, about a 4.9-point margin. (as_of: 2023-02-28; source: https://clerk.house.gov/member_info/electionInfo/2022/statistics2022.pdf)
- In the Nov 8, 2022 Georgia Senate general, Raphael Warnock (D) got 49.4%, Herschel Walker (R) 48.5% and Chase Oliver (L) 2.1%. Nobody passed 50%, so the race went to a runoff. (as_of: 2022-12-07; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2022/12/07/warnock-d-defeats-walker-r-in-the-runoff-election-for-u-s-senate-in-georgia/)
- Warnock beat Walker in the Dec 6, 2022 runoff, which took Democrats to 51 Senate seats against 49 for Republicans. (as_of: 2022-12-07; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2022/12/07/warnock-d-defeats-walker-r-in-the-runoff-election-for-u-s-senate-in-georgia/)
- Republicans clinched the 218th House seat on the night of Wednesday, Nov 16, 2022, eight days after Election Day, when AP projected Mike Garcia (R) re-elected over Christy Smith in California. (as_of: 2022-11-16; source: https://floridaphoenix.com/2022/11/16/republicans-win-control-of-the-u-s-house-with-218th-seat/)
- The Clerk of the House's official statistics list the 118th Congress at 222 Republicans and 213 Democrats in the House. (as_of: 2023-02-28; source: https://clerk.house.gov/member_info/electionInfo/2022/statistics2022.pdf)
- Official national House vote: Republican candidates 54,227,992, Democratic candidates 51,280,463, out of 108,443,387 total, a Republican margin of about 2.7 points. (as_of: 2023-02-28; source: https://clerk.house.gov/member_info/electionInfo/2022/statistics2022.pdf)
- Ron DeSantis (R) was re-elected Florida governor by nearly 19 points over Charlie Crist, the largest Florida governor margin since 1982. (as_of: 2022-11-09; source: https://www.wuft.org/2022-11-09/desantis-wins-2022-florida-governors-race-by-largest-margin-in-40-years)
- The Eagleton Center (Rutgers) puts the 2022 governor result at a net Democratic gain of two seats. Democrats flipped Arizona, Maryland and Massachusetts, and Republicans flipped Nevada by defeating an incumbent Democrat. (as_of: 2023-02-28; source: https://governors.rutgers.edu/2022-gubernatorial-election-wrap-up/)
- FiveThirtyEight's final 2022 forecasts gave Democrats a 41% to 50% chance of keeping the Senate, depending on model version. Prediction markets had them at 32%. (as_of: 2023-02-02; source: https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/23368444-how-our-2022-midterm-forecasts-performed)
- FiveThirtyEight's post-mortem says the actual seat changes (Democrats +1 in the Senate, Republicans +9 in the House) fell inside the 80% confidence intervals of every version of its model. (as_of: 2023-02-02; source: https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/23368444-how-our-2022-midterm-forecasts-performed)
- FiveThirtyEight reports Republicans won the House popular vote by 2.8 points, close to its forecast range of 2.4 to 4.0 points. (as_of: 2023-02-02; source: https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/23368444-how-our-2022-midterm-forecasts-performed)
- FiveThirtyEight's published Deluxe forecast called 488 of 506 races correctly (96%). (as_of: 2023-02-02; source: https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/23368444-how-our-2022-midterm-forecasts-performed)
- FiveThirtyEight names Marie Gluesenkamp Perez (D) winning Washington's 3rd district as its biggest upset; it had given her about a 2% chance. (as_of: 2023-02-02; source: https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/23368444-how-our-2022-midterm-forecasts-performed)
- FiveThirtyEight found the 2021-22 polls more accurate than in any cycle since at least 1998, with almost no bias toward either party. (as_of: 2023-03-10; source: https://fivethirtyeight.com/features/2022-election-polling-accuracy/)

## Resolution notes

- Call-to-control lag in 2022: Senate control was called 4 days after Election Day (Nov 12) and House control 8 days after (Nov 16), and the final Senate seat was decided by the Dec 6 Georgia runoff. A market that settles on a media call stays open through that whole stretch. (as_of: 2022-12-07; source: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2022/12/07/warnock-d-defeats-walker-r-in-the-runoff-election-for-u-s-senate-in-georgia/)
