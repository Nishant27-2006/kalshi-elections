# Chamber: 2026 Mid-Decade Redistricting

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: the 2025-2026 mid-decade congressional redistricting fights, covering what was enacted, blocked or struck, the dates, seat effects as named sources stated them, and how the Kalshi redistricting market settles.

## Claims

- Texas Gov. Greg Abbott signed a new congressional map drawing 5 new Republican-leaning districts. (as_of: 2025-08-29; source: https://www.cnn.com/2025/08/29/politics/texas-redistricting-greg-abbott-house)
- A federal panel in El Paso barred Texas from using the new map in the 2026 midterms, ruling it an illegal racial gerrymander. (as_of: 2025-11-18; source: https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/18/politics/texas-redistricting-trump-court-ruling)
- The U.S. Supreme Court voted 6-3 to stay the El Paso ruling, letting Texas use the new map in 2026. (as_of: 2025-12-04; source: https://apnews.com/article/supreme-court-redistricting-texas-trump-02b07b477b153f23ed5c387f2f9ae0c4)
- Missouri Gov. Mike Kehoe signed a map aimed at netting Republicans one seat by splitting Kansas City (Rep. Emanuel Cleaver's district). (as_of: 2025-09-28; source: https://thehill.com/homenews/state-watch/5500453-missouri-redistricting-gop-gains/amp/)
- North Carolina's legislature passed a map designed to give the GOP one extra seat by making Rep. Don Davis's NC-1 more Republican. The governor cannot veto it. (as_of: 2025-10-22; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/elections/north-carolina-lawmakers-pass-new-map-designed-give-gop-extra-house-se-rcna238702)
- The Ohio Redistricting Commission unanimously passed a map that makes Landsman's OH-1 and Kaptur's OH-9 more competitive for Republicans. It was described as moving the delegation from 10-5 R toward 12-3 R. (as_of: 2025-10-31; source: https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2025/10/31/ohio-redistricting-commission-unanimously-passes-congressional-map-further-gop-advantage/)
- California voters approved Proposition 50 65%-35%, allowing a new map that makes five seats more favorable to Democrats for 2026. (as_of: 2025-11-05; source: https://ballotpedia.org/Redistricting_ahead_of_the_2026_elections)
- The U.S. Supreme Court denied the California GOP's application, so the Prop 50 map stays in use. (as_of: 2026-02-04; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/supreme-court-allows-california-use-new-congressional-map-democrats-rcna257036)
- A Utah judge rejected the legislature's map and adopted plaintiffs' Map 1, which creates a safe Democratic district in Salt Lake County for 2026. (as_of: 2025-11-11; source: https://www.abc4.com/news/politics/inside-utah-politics/redistricting-ruling-judge-rules-against-utah-legislature-and-picks-plaintiffs-map-1-for-utahs-2026-midterms/)
- The Indiana Senate rejected the House-passed redistricting plan 31-19, breaking with Trump. (as_of: 2025-12-11; source: https://apnews.com/article/indiana-lawmakers-redistricting-final-vote-80e3e546fc7acec4a7bd7cd110787375)
- Virginia voters approved a redistricting amendment on Apr 21, 2026, putting in place a map that gives Democrats the advantage in 10 of 11 districts (they held 6). CNN counted Democrats at 10 seats redrawn in their favor nationally against Republicans' 9 at that point. (as_of: 2026-04-22; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/21/politics/virginia-redistricting-referendum-passes)
- The Virginia Supreme Court voided the amendment 4-3, holding that its first passage missed the intervening-election requirement. (as_of: 2026-05-08; source: https://thehill.com/regulation/court-battles/5869616-virginia-supreme-court-tosses-redistricting-map-2026-midterms/)
- The U.S. Supreme Court rejected Virginia Democrats' bid to restore the map. (as_of: 2026-05-15; source: https://apnews.com/article/supreme-court-virginia-redistricting-democrats-republicans-fdd33b89ac29cf2806f790f9b22ee2e9)
- The Supreme Court's Louisiana v. Callais decision struck down Louisiana's map and narrowed what Section 2 of the Voting Rights Act requires. (as_of: 2026-04-29; source: https://www.npr.org/2026/04/29/nx-s1-5754657/supreme-court-louisiana-redistricting)
- Florida's legislature passed DeSantis's map, which is expected to create four new Republican-leaning districts. It abolishes Soto's and Castor's seats and makes Moskowitz's and Wasserman Schultz's seats GOP-leaning. (as_of: 2026-04-29; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/florida-legislature-passes-redistricting-plan-creating-four-additional-rcna342656)
- Tennessee's legislature passed, and Gov. Bill Lee signed, a map targeting Rep. Steve Cohen's Memphis-based TN-9, the state's only Democratic seat. (as_of: 2026-05-07; source: https://www.wkrn.com/news/tennessee-politics/tn-special-session-redistricting-day-3/)
- Louisiana Gov. Jeff Landry signed a map that eliminates one of the state's two majority-Black, Democratic-held districts. (as_of: 2026-05-29; source: https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/louisiana-lawmakers-send-new-congressional-map-erasing-majority-black-district-to-governor-to-sign/)
- The U.S. Supreme Court voted 6-3 to let Alabama use its 2023 legislative map, which eliminates one of two majority-Black districts. Rep. Shomari Figures (D) is likely to lose out. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/supreme-court/supreme-court-allows-alabama-congress-map-eliminate-black-district-rcna346920)
- The U.S. Supreme Court declined for the third time to let Missouri use its 2025 map, so Missouri will use the 2022 map in 2026. (as_of: 2026-09-25; source: https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/09/for-the-third-time-supreme-court-declines-to-allow-missouri-to-use-congressional-map-expected-to/)
- Ballotpedia: as of September 2026, nine states had new congressional maps (AL, CA, FL, LA, NC, OH, TN, TX, UT). Before 2025, only two states had done voluntary mid-decade redistricting since 1970. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://ballotpedia.org/Redistricting_ahead_of_the_2026_elections)
- Wikipedia's tally of enacted plans in effect for 2026: Republican advantage of TX 5, NC 1, OH 2, FL 4, TN 1, LA 1 and AL 1; Democratic advantage of CA 5 and UT 1. Net Republican advantage: 9 seats. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_United_States_redistricting)
- NPR estimated that about 1 in 10 registered voters have new district lines, and noted Trump's job approval is below average in newly drawn South Florida and Texas border districts such as TX-28 and FL-25. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.npr.org/2026/10/08/nx-s1-5993809/2026-house-map-democrats-flip-republicans-tossup-triage)

## Resolution notes

- Each state market in KXREDISTRICTING-26 resolves Yes if that state's legislature adopts new, qualifying congressional maps after Issuance and before Nov 1, 2026. The maps must be enacted into law, not enjoined, vacated, stayed or struck down in full as of expiration, and in effect for the first federal election after adoption. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXREDISTRICTING-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Maps fully redrawn by a court or special master resolve No, and that applies to Utah's court-adopted Map 1. Maps withdrawn by the legislature resolve No. Maps stayed pending appeal but later upheld resolve Yes. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXREDISTRICTING-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Kalshi clarification of Sep 3, 2026: Alabama's reinstated 2023 map, and its use in the August 2026 special primary, do not count because that map was adopted before Issuance. Alabama can still resolve Yes only if it adopts a new qualifying map before Nov 1, 2026. (as_of: 2026-09-03; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXREDISTRICTING-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Issuance (market open) dates vary by state: OH, CA, LA, NY, MD, GA opened 2025-07-24; MO 2025-07-29; TX 2025-08-04; IN 2025-08-29; VA 2025-09-10; UT and FL 2025-09-14; NC 2025-10-14; AL 2026-04-29; TN 2026-05-01. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXREDISTRICTING-26?with_nested_markets=true)
