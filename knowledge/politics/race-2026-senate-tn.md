# Race: 2026 Tennessee Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Bill Hagerty (R) seeks a second term against Democrat Marquita Bradshaw in a 2020 rematch, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Hagerty was unopposed in the Aug 6 Republican primary and received 629,194 votes. (as_of: 2026-08-06; source: https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/08/06/us/elections/results-tennessee-us-senate-primary.html)
- Marquita Bradshaw, the 2020 nominee, won the Democratic primary with 54.8% (186,230 votes) over Maria Brewer (18.0%) and three others. (as_of: 2026-08-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Tennessee)
- Several independent and minor-party candidates are also running, including Federalist Party candidate Andrew Woodruff Mixon. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Tennessee)
- FEC reports as of July 17, 2026: Hagerty raised $15.97M with $5.23M cash on hand. Bradshaw reported $2,257 raised. (as_of: 2026-07-17; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Tennessee)
- Targoz Market Research (for the Beacon Center of Tennessee), Aug 15–26, 1,157 RV: Hagerty 51, Bradshaw 31, "an independent candidate" 6. (as_of: 2026-08-28; source: https://tennsight.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Beacon-Poll-of-TN-August-2026-Crosstabs-Party-Ideology-and-Region-1.pdf)
- Ratings: Cook Solid R (Oct 6), Inside Elections Solid R (Oct 1), Sabato Safe R (Oct 6), Silver Bulletin Solid R, DDHQ, The Economist, FiftyPlusOne and Split Ticket all Safe R (Oct 7). (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Tennessee)
- Democrats have not won a Senate election in Tennessee since 1990. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Tennessee)
- 2024 presidential: Trump won Tennessee 64.19% to 34.47%. (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Hagerty 62.20%, Bradshaw 35.16%. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_Tennessee)
- Kalshi SENATETN-26 on Oct 9: R (Hagerty) bid/ask 0.93/0.95; D (Bradshaw) 0.050/0.063. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATETN-26?with_nested_markets=true)
