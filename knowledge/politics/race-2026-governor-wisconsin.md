# Race: 2026 Wisconsin Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open-seat Wisconsin governor race (Evers retiring): Democrat David Crowley vs Republican Tom Tiffany, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Gov. Tony Evers (D) declined to seek a third term, making this the first open Wisconsin governor race since 2010. (as_of: 2025-07-24; source: https://www.jsonline.com/story/news/politics/2025/07/24/wisconsin-democratic-gov-tony-evers-wont-seek-re-election-in-2026/84246291007/)
- At the August 11 primary, Milwaukee County Executive David Crowley won the Democratic nomination in an upset over state Rep. Francesca Hong with 39.8%. (as_of: 2026-08-12; source: https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6023019-crowley-wins-wisconsin-governor-primary/)
- Pre-primary polling had Hong ahead; State Navigate, Aug 3–6, 1,473 LV, showed Hong 44, Crowley 22. (as_of: 2026-08-07; source: https://statenavigate.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/WIDEMPoll2.pdf)
- U.S. Rep. Tom Tiffany won the GOP nomination without serious challenge. (as_of: 2026-08-11; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Wisconsin_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: Evers (D) 51.2%, Tim Michels (R) 47.8%, about D+3.4. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Wisconsin by 0.86 points (Harris 48.74%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook and Inside Elections both rate the race a Tossup (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Lean D (Oct 6, 2026 table); Decision Desk HQ and Silver Bulletin also have it at Lean D. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Platform Communications (R), Aug 12–13, 500 LV: Crowley 48, Tiffany 44. (as_of: 2026-08-16; source: https://www.wpr.org/news/crowley-leading-tiffany-four-points-new-poll)
- TIPP Insights (R), Aug 14–18: Crowley 47, Tiffany 43 among 1,199 LV; 43–41 among 1,434 RV. (as_of: 2026-08-21; source: https://tippinsights.com/league-of-american-workers-survey-wisconsin-4/)
- Marquette Law School, Aug 12–20: Crowley 49, Tiffany 44 among 738 LV; tied 44–44 among 891 RV. (as_of: 2026-08-26; source: https://law.marquette.edu/poll/2026/08/26/marquette-law-poll-finds-crowley-leading-tiffany-among-likely-voters-tied-among-all-registered-voters/)
- Marquette Law School, Sep 16–23: Crowley 49, Tiffany 46 among 692 LV (±4.8); Crowley 46, Tiffany 45 among 843 RV (±4.3). (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://law.marquette.edu/poll/2026/09/30/mlsp91-press-release/)
- RealClearPolitics average (Jul 20–Aug 20 polls, updated Sep 13): Crowley 47.5, Tiffany 44.0 (Crowley +3.5). (as_of: 2026-09-13; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/polls/governor/general/2026/wisconsin/crowley-vs-tiffany)
