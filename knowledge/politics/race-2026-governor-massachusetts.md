# Race: 2026 Massachusetts Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Democratic incumbent Maura Healey seeks a second term against Republican Mike Minogue, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- At the September 1, 2026 primaries, Healey was unopposed for the Democratic nomination and Mike Minogue beat Brian Shortsleeve for the GOP nomination. (as_of: 2026-09-02; source: https://commonwealthbeacon.org/politics/minogue-wins-gop-primary-for-governor-setting-stage-for-matchup-with-healey/)
- Minogue spent $6.1 million in September 2026, which the Boston Globe called the most expensive Massachusetts governor bid on record, topping Kerry Healey's $4.4M in 2006. (as_of: 2026-10-02; source: https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/10/02/metro/minogue-61-million-spending-september-governors-race/)
- 2022 result: Healey (D) 63.7%, Geoff Diehl (R) 34.6%, about D+29.1. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Harris won Massachusetts by 25.20 points (61.22% to 36.02%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook and Inside Elections both rate the race Solid D (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it Safe D (Oct 6, 2026 table). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Suffolk/Boston Globe, Jun 8–12, 500 LV: Healey 56, Shortsleeve 29 (pre-primary hypothetical). (as_of: 2026-06-16; source: https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/06/16/metro/full-results-poll-of-massachusetts-voters/)
- University of New Hampshire, Feb 12–16, 620 LV: Healey 58, Shortsleeve 28 (pre-primary hypothetical). (as_of: 2026-02-24; source: https://scholars.unh.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1930&context=survey_center_polls)
- University of New Hampshire, Sep 17–21, 564 LV: Healey 54, Minogue 38. (as_of: 2026-09-24; source: https://scholars.unh.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=2007&context=survey_center_polls)
- MassINC for CommonWealth Beacon, Sep 14–22, 800 LV: Healey 54, Minogue 32 with leaners; Healey favorability 52/33. (as_of: 2026-09-29; source: https://commonwealthbeacon.org/politics/poll-healey-cruising-in-reelection-bid-with-trump-high-on-voters-mind/)
- MassINC: 27% of respondents had not formed an opinion of Minogue and 16% had never heard of him. (as_of: 2026-09-29; source: https://commonwealthbeacon.org/politics/poll-healey-cruising-in-reelection-bid-with-trump-high-on-voters-mind/)
- RealClearPolitics average (May 26–Sep 21 polls): Healey 54.3, Minogue 34.0 (Healey +20.3). (as_of: 2026-09-24; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/polls/governor/general/2026/massachusetts/minogue-vs-healey)
