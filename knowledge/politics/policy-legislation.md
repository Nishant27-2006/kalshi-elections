# Policy: Trump-Era Legislation Markets

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: dated floor history and pricing for the SAVE Act and SAVE America Act, the CLARITY Act (crypto market structure), tariff "dividend" checks and the KXBILLS basket, how the Senate filibuster constrains them, and GovTrack base rates for bills that reach a floor vote becoming law.

## Claims

- The House passed H.R. 22, the SAVE Act, 220-208 on Apr. 10, 2025; all 216 Republicans voting and 4 Democrats voted yea. (as_of: 2025-04-10; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2025/h102)
- The House passed H.R. 3633, the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act, 294-134 on July 17, 2025, with 78 Democrats voting yea. (as_of: 2025-07-17; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2025/h199)
- Trump posted on Truth Social on Nov. 9, 2025 that everyone except high earners would get at least $2,000 in "dividends" from tariff revenue. (as_of: 2025-11-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_the_second_Trump_presidency_(2025_Q4))
- The House passed the SAVE America Act, as a House amendment to S. 1383, 218-213 on Feb. 11, 2026. (as_of: 2026-02-11; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2026/h69)
- In Learning Resources v. Trump, decided Feb. 20, 2026, the Supreme Court held 6-3 (Roberts writing) that IEEPA does not authorize the President to impose tariffs. (as_of: 2026-02-20; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning_Resources,_Inc._v._Trump)
- The government stopped collecting IEEPA tariffs on Feb. 24, 2026, and replacement Section 122 tariffs at 10% took effect that day. (as_of: 2026-02-24; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning_Resources,_Inc._v._Trump)
- Senate Majority Leader John Thune said on Mar. 10, 2026 that the SAVE bill was unlikely to pass the Senate because there were not enough votes to override the filibuster or to support a talking filibuster. (as_of: 2026-03-10; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safeguard_American_Voter_Eligibility_Act)
- The Senate agreed 51-48 on Mar. 17, 2026 to proceed to S. 1383 (SAVE America Act). (as_of: 2026-03-17; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2026/s57)
- On Mar. 21, 2026, cloture on the Tuberville amendment to the motion to concur in the House amendment to S. 1383 failed 49-41, short of the 60 needed. (as_of: 2026-03-21; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2026/s60)
- On Mar. 24, 2026, the Senate voted 53-47 on a motion to table S. 1383 (motion agreed to). (as_of: 2026-03-24; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2026/s67)
- The House voted 217-209 on July 15, 2026 to attach the SAVE Act provisions to the FY2027 NDAA. As of September 2026 the Senate had not passed the NDAA. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safeguard_American_Voter_Eligibility_Act)
- The House passed the FY2027 NDAA (H.R. 8800) 216-212 on July 22, 2026. (as_of: 2026-07-22; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2026/h278)
- Senate Banking took up H.R. 3633 on Jan. 15 and May 14, 2026, and a Senate-reported text was published June 1, 2026. (as_of: 2026-06-01; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/bills/119/hr3633)
- Cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633 (CLARITY Act) failed 49-50 on Sep. 15, 2026. All 49 yeas were Republicans; 4 Republicans and 46 Democrats voted nay. (as_of: 2026-09-15; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2026/s234)
- At the GOP midterm convention, Trump offered a $5,000 "dividend" to all Americans if Republicans keep Congress; 63% disapproved in a Reuters/Ipsos poll. (as_of: 2026-09-14; source: https://gvwire.com/2026/09/14/trump-approval-up-from-record-low-but-outlook-sours-for-republicans-reuters-ipsos-poll-finds/)
- GovTrack counted 122 enacted bills (about 1%) of 19,596 bills and resolutions introduced in the 119th Congress so far. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/bills/statistics)

## Resolution notes

- GovTrack's "Got A Vote" bucket counts bills with a significant vote in either chamber that were not enacted, so the floor-vote base rates above are a proxy for "passed the House, became law in the same Congress," not an exact match. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.govtrack.us/congress/bills/statistics)
- KXSAVEACT-27 names H.R. 22 specifically. KXELECTIONBILL resolves on any law requiring proof of U.S. citizenship to register for federal elections, which would include the SAVE America/NDAA route. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXSAVEACT-27?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXCRYPTOSTRUCTURE needs a law that sets a comprehensive digital-asset framework, divides SEC/CFTC authority, and defines when assets are securities or commodities. Stablecoin-only bills do not count. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXCRYPTOSTRUCTURE-26JAN?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXTARIFFCHECKS needs a Source Agency to report as fact (not as a claim by the President) that at least 1 million Americans got $1,000+ payments funded by tariff revenue (clarified 12/18/25). (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXTARIFFCHECKS-26?with_nested_markets=true)
