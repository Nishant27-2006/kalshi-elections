# Race: 2026 New Hampshire Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open Democratic-held seat (Jeanne Shaheen retiring): Rep. Chris Pappas (D) vs former Sen. John E. Sununu (R), November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Incumbent Jeanne Shaheen (D) is not seeking a fourth term. It is New Hampshire's first open Senate race since 2010. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_New_Hampshire)
- In the Sept 8 primaries, Pappas won 65% over Karishma Manzur. Sununu won 68.5% over former Massachusetts Sen. Scott Brown. (as_of: 2026-09-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_New_Hampshire)
- Constitution Party nominee Edmond LaPlante Jr. is also on the ballot. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_New_Hampshire)
- FEC totals through Aug 19, 2026 (pre-primary): Pappas raised $15.38M with $4.25M on hand. Sununu raised $4.18M with $3.07M on hand. (as_of: 2026-08-27; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=NH&election_full=true)
- Inside Elections moved New Hampshire from Tilt Democrat to Toss-up. (as_of: 2026-09-03; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- InsiderAdvantage (R), Sep 16–17, 1,200 LV: Pappas 48, Sununu 40. (as_of: 2026-09-18; source: https://insideradvantage.com/nh-top-line-tabs/)
- Per CNN, a September University of New Hampshire poll had Pappas up 8 among likely voters, while a NYT/Siena poll over about the same period showed no clear leader. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- Trafalgar Group (R), Sep 26–27, 1,082 LV: Pappas 47, Sununu 41. (as_of: 2026-09-27; source: https://www.thetrafalgargroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/NH-Midterms-Survey-Report-0927.pdf)
- Cook Political Report moved New Hampshire from Toss Up to Lean Democrat. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6132331-cook-political-report-democrats-shift/)
- YouGov, Sep 28–Oct 5, 1,520 LV, MoE ±3.9: Pappas 51, Sununu 43. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://www.newsweek.com/democrats-flip-senate-seats-midterms-poll-12535052)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rated NH Lean D as of Oct 6. Silver Bulletin, Split Ticket, The Economist and DDHQ had Likely D as of Oct 7. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_New_Hampshire)
- Per CNN, Democrats are attacking Sununu's lobbying background. Republicans hope Gov. Kelly Ayotte, who leads in her reelection polling, will help Sununu. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- 2024 presidential: Harris won NH 50.65% to 47.87% (D+2.78). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Biden won NH 52.71% to 45.36% (D+7.35). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Shaheen (D) 56.63%, Corky Messner (R) 40.99%. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_New_Hampshire)
