# Policy: Trump Executive Actions and Fiscal One-Offs

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: a set of single-event executive and legislative markets: the Insurrection Act, an election national emergency, whether the midterms happen on time, a Trump $250 bill, marijuana rescheduling, a federal gas-tax suspension, and repeal of the 90% gambling-loss cap.

## Claims

- On Jan. 15, 2026, Trump threatened to invoke the Insurrection Act over the Minneapolis protests against ICE operations. He backed off the next day, saying there was not a "reason right now," and added, "if I needed it, I'd use it." (as_of: 2026-01-16; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_military_deployments_by_the_second_Trump_administration)
- In a TIME interview, asked whether he would rule out declaring a state of emergency before the midterms, Trump said "I don't rule anything out or in. We'll see." On the Insurrection Act he said: "No, I've never used it. I could use it. A lot of people think I should use it sometimes." (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/trump-makes-sinister-midterm-threat-161334839.html)

(as_of: 2026-04-03; source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/03/2026-06601/ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections)

## Resolution notes

- KXINSURRECTION rule text: "If the President of the United States has invoked the Insurrection Act to deploy the United States military and/or the federalized National Guard within the United States before Jan 1, 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." The 2028 and Jan 20, 2029 strikes use the same wording. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXINSURRECTION-29?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXELECTIONEMERGENCY rule text: "If Donald Trump has taken any executive action declaring a national emergency regarding the 2026 United States midterm election before [date], then the market resolves to Yes." Secondary: "The qualifying action must be of the specific type designated (executive order, presidential memorandum, proclamation, directive, determination, or finding), be signed by the President personally during the specified time period, and explicitly address the topic in the document's operative provisions, title, or official White House summary. Actions must have legal or policy effect - ceremonial proclamations without policy impact do not qualify unless specifically included. Actions that only incidentally mention the topic, statements without formal action, actions by cabinet members, legislative proposals without executive action, and signing statements do not qualify. The action must be publicly announced or documented by a Source Agency before expiration." (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events?series_ticker=KXELECTIONEMERGENCY&with_nested_markets=true)
- Kalshi settled KXELECTIONEMERGENCY-26SEP01 No on Sep. 1, 2026, a day after the Aug. 31 continuation of the foreign-election-interference emergency was signed. So the routine continuation did not settle the market Yes. (as_of: 2026-09-01; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events?series_ticker=KXELECTIONEMERGENCY&with_nested_markets=true)
- KXMIDTERMHAPPEN rule text: "If the regularly scheduled biennial federal general election for the U.S. House of Representatives is conducted on time in all 50 U.S. states on the federally prescribed Election Day for 2026, then the market resolves to Yes." A state counts as on time if officials "lawfully accept ballots" in every House district on Election Day. Localized disruptions do not disqualify a district, but a district where no ballots are accepted anywhere fails the state. A date change "by federal legislation, executive order, presidential proclamation, federal agency directive, state law or proclamation, court order, or any other instrument" fails regardless of lawfulness. Early votes alone do not satisfy it. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMIDTERMHAPPEN-2026?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXMIDTERMHAPPEN clarification (10/06/26): "a district will not fail the 'conducted on time' requirement solely because, under state law in effect at Issuance, an unopposed U.S. House candidate is omitted from the ballot and deemed elected. This does not apply to a contest cancelled, postponed, or removed from the ballot pursuant to a law or order adopted after Issuance." (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMIDTERMHAPPEN-2026?with_nested_markets=true)
- Federal law fixes House Election Day: "The Tuesday next after the 1st Monday in November, in every even numbered year, is established as the day for the election ... of Representatives and Delegates to the Congress." In 2026 that is Nov. 3. (as_of: 1934-06-05; source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/2/7)
- KX250DOLLARBILL rule text: "If bill that mandates the creation and issuance of a $250 U.S. bill featuring Donald J. Trump's likeness becomes law before Jan 1, 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." A Treasury design alone does not count. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KX250DOLLARBILL-27?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXMJSCHEDULE rule text: "If marijuana is rescheduled from Schedule I to a lower drug schedule under the Controlled Substances Act before January 1, 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." Secondary: "If the DEA were to reschedule only certain cannabis derivatives or specific cannabinoids (e.g., move THC or a particular cannabis-derived pharmaceutical to a different schedule) without reclassifying marijuana/cannabis as a whole, that would not qualify as rescheduling marijuana for this contract. Ending the schedule altogether would resolve the market to Yes." Clarification (12/12/25): "The Payout Criterion requires that marijuana is legally removed from Schedule I. The mere announcement or publication of a Final Rule in the Federal Register does not satisfy the Payout Criterion if the rule has not taken legal effect." The market stayed open after the Apr. 28, 2026 medical-only rule. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events?series_ticker=KXMJSCHEDULE&with_nested_markets=true)
- KXSUSPENDGASTAX rule text: "If legislation that would temporarily suspend or reduce the federal excise tax on gasoline has become law after Issuance and before Jan 1, 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." Secondary: "For 'become law' markets, the bill must be signed by the President or become law through veto override. Presidential pocket vetoes that expire resolve to No." An executive order alone cannot qualify. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXSUSPENDGASTAX-26MAY?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXGAMBLINGREPEAL rule text: "If a bill that removes/changes the gambling tax provision in the One Big Beautiful Bill Act ('OBBB') has become law after Issuance and before [date], then the market resolves to Yes." Any enacted change to the provision qualifies, not only a full repeal. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXGAMBLINGREPEAL-26JAN?with_nested_markets=true)
