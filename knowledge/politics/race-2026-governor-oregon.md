# Race: 2026 Oregon Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Gov. Tina Kotek (D) seeks a second term in a rematch with 2022 Republican nominee Christine Drazan, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- 2022 result: Kotek 46.96%, Drazan 43.54%, independent Betsy Johnson 8.62%. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Oregon_gubernatorial_election)
- Kotek won the May 19 Democratic primary with 385,999 of 461,695 votes (about 84%). (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Oregon_gubernatorial_election)
- Drazan won the May 19 Republican primary with 40.5%, ahead of Ed Diehl (33.0%) and Chris Dudley (16.9%). (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Oregon_gubernatorial_election)
- This is Oregon's first gubernatorial rematch since 1978. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Oregon_gubernatorial_election)
- Pacific Green nominee Brett Smith is also on the ballot. The Oregon Working Families Party endorsed Kotek. (as_of: 2026-08-21; source: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/08/21/kotek-drazan-smith-minor-parties-three-way-race-oregon-governor/)
- Public Opinion Strategies (R, Drazan campaign), Jun 22–24, 600 RV: Drazan 48, Kotek 44. (as_of: 2026-07-09; source: https://www.oregonlive.com/politics/2026/07/new-poll-shows-oregon-governors-race-is-far-from-settled.html)
- DHM Research (for Grow Oregon), Sep 3–9, 600 LV: Drazan 45, Kotek 43, Smith 4. (as_of: 2026-09-15; source: https://www.oregonjournalismproject.org/new-poll-shows-drazan-with-slight-lead-in-governors-race)
- DHM Research for OPB, Sep 17–27, 915 LV: Drazan 46, Kotek 44, Smith 3. With leaners: Drazan 47, Kotek 46. (as_of: 2026-09-30; source: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/09/30/opb-poll-shows-oregon-governors-race-virtually-deadlocked/)
- Kotek and Drazan debated at the University of Oregon on Oct 1. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VUR0fsIADZU)
- DHM Research for The Oregonian, Sep 24–Oct 1, 549 LV: Kotek 46, Drazan 44, Smith 3, undecided 7. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/10/08/new-polls-kotek-may-lead/)
- GBAO (Kotek campaign internal), Oct 1–4, 800 LV: Kotek 46, Drazan 42, Smith 6, undecided 6. The same poll put Trump's approval in Oregon at 29%. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/10/08/new-polls-kotek-may-lead/)
- In OPB's September poll, Kotek's favorability among likely voters was 38%, against 37% for Trump. More than 20% of Kotek's own voters viewed her unfavorably. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/10/08/new-polls-kotek-may-lead/)
- Silver Bulletin average through Oct 1: Kotek 45.9, Drazan 43.9 (Kotek +2.0). (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Oregon_gubernatorial_election)
- Ratings: Cook Lean D (Sep 10), Inside Elections Lean D (Sep 3), DDHQ Likely D (Sep 23), Silver Bulletin Likely D (Aug 25), RealClearPolitics Lean D (Oct 5), Fox News Lean D (Oct 6). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Oregon_gubernatorial_election)
- 2024 presidential: Harris won Oregon 55.27% to 40.97%. (as_of: 2024-12-13; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Kalshi GOVPARTYOR-26 on Oct 9: D (Kotek) bid/ask 0.76/0.77; R (Drazan) 0.23/0.24. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/GOVPARTYOR-26?with_nested_markets=true)

## Resolution notes

- GOVPARTYOR-26-D: "If a representative of the Democratic party is inaugurated as the governor of Oregon pursuant to the 2026 election, then the market resolves to Yes." The R market uses the same wording for the Republican party. Both can be settled early once a consensus of media organizations projects the winner. Expected expiration is 2027-01-09.
