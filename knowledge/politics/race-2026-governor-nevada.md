# Race: 2026 Nevada Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Republican incumbent Joe Lombardo seeks a second term against Democratic Attorney General Aaron Ford, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- At the June 9, 2026 primaries, Lombardo won the GOP nomination with 91.0% and Ford won the Democratic nomination with 63.8% over Washoe County Commission chair Alexis Hill. (as_of: 2026-06-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nevada_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: Lombardo (R) 48.8%, Steve Sisolak (D) 47.3%, about R+1.5. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Nevada by 3.10 points (Harris 47.49%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook rates the race a Tossup and Inside Elections Tilt R (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it a Tossup (Oct 6, 2026 table). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Public Policy Polling (D), Jul 15–16, 558 LV: Lombardo 44, Ford 44. (as_of: 2026-07-22; source: https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/lombardo-ford-tied-in-nevada-governors-race-dem-poll-shows)
- Tarrance Group (R), Jul 25–29: Lombardo 50, Ford 43; the Nevada Independent reported three GOP polls showing Lombardo with a sizable lead. (as_of: 2026-08-05; source: https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/three-gop-polls-show-lombardo-with-sizable-lead-in-nevada-governors-race)
- Emerson, Sep 5–8, 680 LV, ±3.7: Ford 44, Lombardo 42, 11% undecided; Lombardo job approval 40% approve, 39% disapprove. (as_of: 2026-09-10; source: https://emersoncollegepolling.com/nevada-2026-poll-ford-and-lombardo/)
- Noble Predictive Insights, Sep 22–26, 708 LV (±3.7): Lombardo leads Ford by 2, with 7% for independent Danielle Ford and 9% undecided. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://www.noblepredictiveinsights.com/post/lombardo-tries-to-escape-trump-s-orbit-up-2-over-ford)
- Noble: voters rate Lombardo on the economy at 45% approve, 47% disapprove, against Trump at 38/61; pollster Mike Noble credits that gap with keeping the race close. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://www.noblepredictiveinsights.com/post/lombardo-tries-to-escape-trump-s-orbit-up-2-over-ford)
- RealClearPolitics average (Sep 5–26 polls): Lombardo 42.0, Ford 42.0 (tie). (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://www.realclearpolling.com/polls/governor/general/2026/nevada/lombardo-vs-ford)
- Kalshi GOVPARTYNV-26: Democratic (Ford) contract 51¢ bid / 52¢ ask; Republican (Lombardo) 48¢ / 49¢. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/GOVPARTYNV-26?with_nested_markets=true)

## Resolution notes

- GOVPARTYNV-26-D resolves Yes "If a representative of the Democratic party is inaugurated as the governor of Nevada pursuant to the 2026 election"; -R mirrors it. An independent winner (e.g. Danielle Ford) would resolve both No. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/GOVPARTYNV-26?with_nested_markets=true)
