# Race: 2026 Maine Senate

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Sen. Susan Collins (R) seeks a sixth term against replacement Democratic nominee Troy Jackson, November 3, 2026, under ranked-choice voting.

## Claims

- The election uses ranked-choice voting. Collins was the only candidate to qualify for the June 9 Republican primary. (as_of: 2026-06-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Maine)
- Graham Platner won the June 9 Democratic primary over Gov. Janet Mills. (as_of: 2026-06-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Maine)
- After sexual-assault allegations surfaced on July 6, Platner said on July 8 that he would withdraw from the Senate race. (as_of: 2026-07-08; source: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/graham-platner-says-hell-withdraw-from-senate-race-in-maine)
- After county caucuses, Troy Jackson (former state Senate president) and Saundra Pelletier were the only two candidates left for the July 25 nominating convention. Shah, Bellows and Wood had dropped out. (as_of: 2026-07-23; source: https://www.wbur.org/news/2026/07/23/maine-primary-democrats)
- Jackson won the convention vote over Pelletier, 566 to 5. (as_of: 2026-07-25; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Maine)
- FEC totals through June 30, 2026: Collins raised $18.93M with $11.04M on hand. Jackson was not yet a candidate. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/elections/?cycle=2026&office=senate&state=ME&election_full=true)
- Inside Elections moved Maine from Tilt Republican to Toss-up. It said Jackson led most polling and that it was "analytically dangerous" to assume Collins would again outperform her polls. (as_of: 2026-09-22; source: https://rollcall.com/2026/09/22/rating-changes-a-dozen-more-races-move-toward-democrats/)
- The Inside Elections ratings feed dates that Maine change to Sept 17. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year=2026_district=all_clean.json)
- AARP (Fabrizio Ward/Impact Research), Sep 20–22, 982 LV: Jackson 50, Collins 47. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://www.aarp.org/content/dam/aarp/research/topics/voter-opinion-research/politics/2026-midterm-election-poll-maine.doi.10.26419-2fres.01065.013.pdf)
- CBS News/YouGov, Sep 23–Oct 1, 1,136 LV: Collins 50, Jackson 50. About 10% of voters said they could still change their minds. (as_of: 2026-10-02; source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/maine-senate-poll-2026-10-02/)
- Trafalgar Group (R), Sep 29–Oct 1, 1,091 LV: Collins 46, Jackson 46. (as_of: 2026-10-02; source: https://www.thetrafalgargroup.org/news/me-senate-gen-1002/)
- Per CNN, a September University of New Hampshire poll had Jackson ahead of Collins 51% to 47% among Maine likely voters, the first public poll with the Democrat ahead. CNN/SSRS and NYT/Siena polls showed no clear leader. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- Marist, Oct 1–4, 1,505 RV: Jackson 51, Collins 47. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://maristpoll.marist.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Marist-Poll_Maine-NOS-and-Tables_202610051032xtc92617B.pdf)
- Per CNN, Collins and Jackson were scheduled for four debates over ten days starting Tuesday, Oct 6. Democrats are pressing a ProPublica report alleging pay-to-play in Collins's office during her 2020 campaign, which Collins called a "political hit job." (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/05/politics/10-senate-seats-most-likely-to-flip-2026)
- Cook Political Report lists Maine (Collins) as a Toss Up. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings)
- Sabato's Crystal Ball rated Maine a Toss-up as of Oct 6. The Economist, Silver Bulletin, Split Ticket and FiftyPlusOne had Lean D (flip) as of Oct 7. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Maine)
- 2024 presidential: Harris won Maine statewide 52.40% to 45.46% (D+6.94). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- 2020 presidential: Biden won Maine statewide 53.09% to 44.02% (D+9.07). (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election)
- Last election for this seat (2020): Collins (R) 50.98%, Sara Gideon (D) 42.39%, a win above 50% in the first round. (as_of: 2020-11-03; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Senate_election_in_Maine)
- Collins is the only Republican senator from a state Trump never won in any of his three campaigns. Democrats have not won a Maine Senate election since 1988. (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Maine)

## Resolution notes

- SENATEME-26 rules: "If a representative of the Republican [Democratic] party is sworn in as a Senator of Maine for the term beginning in 2027, then the market resolves to Yes." It can resolve early on a media consensus projection. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/SENATEME-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXMESENATEPERSON-26 rules: "If Troy Jackson wins the 2026 Maine Senate election, then the market resolves to Yes." It can resolve early on a media consensus projection. Expected expiration is 2026-11-03 and close is 2027-07-07. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMESENATEPERSON-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- The person event still lists Graham Platner, Janet Mills and other non-nominees at about 0.001. The race is decided under RCV, so a projection may wait on later tabulation rounds if no one passes 50% in the first round. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMESENATEPERSON-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXMESENOUTCOME-27JAN rules name Platner: GPD needs "Dem Nominee: Graham Platner, General Election Winner: Democrat" and GPR needs Platner plus a Republican winner. Legs settle under the SENATEPARTYNOM / SENATEPARTY rulesets, and if any leg fails the contract resolves No. Even so, on Oct 9 GPD traded at 0.54/0.58 and GPR at 0.39/0.44, close to the party market, so check the full rules for how the nominee leg is being treated. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMESENOUTCOME-27JAN?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXMETXCOMBO-26NOV is a two-race person combo. For example, TAL-COL needs "Texas Senate: James Talarico wins, Maine Senate: Susan Collins wins." The original series lists Platner legs, and the KXMETXCOMBO-26NOVB markets carry Troy Jackson legs. Settlement uses the TX and ME Secretaries of State. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMETXCOMBO-26NOV?with_nested_markets=true)
