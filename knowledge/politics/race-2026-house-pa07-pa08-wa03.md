# Race: 2026 House PA-07, PA-08 and WA-03

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Three competitive House seats on November 3, 2026: two Pennsylvania seats Republicans flipped in 2024 (PA-07 Mackenzie, PA-08 Bresnahan) and Marie Gluesenkamp Perez's Trump-won Washington seat (WA-03).

## Claims

### PA-07 (Lehigh Valley): Ryan Mackenzie (R, inc.) vs Bob Brooks (D)

- The district covers all of Lehigh, Northampton and Carbon counties and a sliver of Monroe. Mackenzie flipped it in 2024 with 50.5%. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)
- Pennsylvania held its primaries on May 19, 2026. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)
- Bob Brooks won the Democratic primary with 28,232 of 68,865 votes, ahead of Ryan Crosswell (14,660), Lamont McClure (13,736) and Carol Obando-Derstine (12,237). Mackenzie was unopposed. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)
- A Commonwealth Court judge ruled that Andrew Tupone could not appear on the ballot as the Green Party candidate, because the party had not selected him. Democrats had accused him of being a GOP-backed spoiler. (as_of: 2026-09-04; source: https://www.inquirer.com/politics/pennsylvania/green-party-seventh-district-congress-lehigh-valley-ballot-election-20260904.html)
- The Pennsylvania Supreme Court upheld Tupone's removal from the ballot. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: https://politicspa.com/pennsylvania-supreme-court-says-no-to-andrew-tupone-in-pa-07/153407/)
- Tupone said he intends to run a write-in campaign. (as_of: 2026-09-04; source: https://penncapital-star.com/briefs/judge-rules-green-party-candidate-accused-of-being-gop-plant-can-be-kicked-off-ballot-in-pa-07/)
- FEC through June 30, 2026: Mackenzie raised $4.36M with $2.94M cash on hand; Brooks raised $2.39M with $1.02M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)
- GBAO (D, for House Majority PAC), Jun 29–Jul 2, 550 LV: Brooks 47, Mackenzie 43. (as_of: 2026-07-09; source: https://static.notus.org/4f/15/f7a7796a4707bdcacd0c633f3fd0/hmp-poll-bob-brooks-leads-ryan-mackenzie-in-pa-07.pdf)
- Franklin & Marshall, Sep 9–20, 357 RV, MoE ±5.8: Brooks 45, Mackenzie 40. (as_of: 2026-09-24; source: https://storage.ghost.io/c/6f/65/6f6547c9-4c4e-45a8-b12e-56fdcab002c9/content/files/2026/09/KEYSEPCD26_Summary.pdf)
- Ratings: Cook Lean D flip (listed as of Oct 9), Sabato Lean D flip (Sep 29), Silver Bulletin Lean D flip (Aug 20), DDHQ Lean D flip (Jul 14). Inside Elections' Toss-up dates from Sep 2025. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)
- A WFMZ-TV debate is scheduled for Oct 21. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)

### PA-08 (Scranton/Wilkes-Barre): Rob Bresnahan (R, inc.) vs Paige Cognetti (D)

- The district covers Lackawanna, Wayne and Pike counties and most of Luzerne and Monroe. Bresnahan flipped it in 2024 with 50.8%. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)
- Scranton Mayor Paige Cognetti launched her campaign. (as_of: 2025-09-02; source: https://www.thetimes-tribune.com/2025/09/02/cognetti-announces-campaign-for-congress/)
- Gov. Josh Shapiro endorsed Cognetti. (as_of: 2026-02-05; source: https://www.poconorecord.com/story/news/politics/elections/congressional/2026/02/05/josh-shapiro-endorses-scranton-mayor-paige-cognetti-8th-district-pa/88515292007/)
- Both candidates were unopposed in the May 19 primaries: Bresnahan 38,075 votes, Cognetti 58,726. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)
- The Pennsylvania Conference of Teamsters switched its endorsement from Cognetti to Bresnahan. (as_of: 2026-06-23; source: https://keystonenewsroom.com/news/elections/teamsters-endorsement-bresnahan-cognetti/)
- FEC through June 30, 2026: Bresnahan raised $5.23M with $2.61M cash on hand; Cognetti raised $4.61M with $3.41M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)
- Lake Research Partners (D, for House Majority PAC), Jun 18–23, 400 V: Cognetti 47, Bresnahan 45. (as_of: 2026-06-26; source: https://www.thehousemajoritypac.com/news/hmp-poll-paige-cognetti-leads-rob-bresnahan-in-pa-08)
- Impact Research (D, Cognetti campaign), Jun 8–11, 500 LV: Cognetti 46, Bresnahan 45. (as_of: 2026-06-29; source: https://paigeforpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/IMPACT-PRESS-MEMO-PA-CD-08-POLL-FINDINGS-JUNE-2026_REV-C.pdf)
- Ratings: Cook Toss-up (Apr 7), Inside Elections Toss-up (Jun 12), DDHQ Toss-up (Jul 14), Sabato Toss-up (Jul 30), Silver Bulletin Lean D flip (Aug 20). (as_of: 2026-08-20; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania)

### WA-03 (Southwest Washington): Marie Gluesenkamp Perez (D, inc.) vs John Braun (R)

- WA-03 has a Cook PVI of R+2. Gluesenkamp Perez won re-election in 2024 with 51.7%, one of 13 Democrats to win a district Trump also carried. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Washington)
- In the Aug 4 top-two primary, Braun (R) took 39.7% and Gluesenkamp Perez 36.4%. Democrat Brent Hennrich, running against her from the left, took 16.7%. (as_of: 2026-08-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Washington)
- Emerson College (for Northwest Progressive Institute), Jul 8–10, 500 LV, primary ballot: Braun 26, Gluesenkamp Perez 25, Hennrich 11. Same poll, general: Braun 43, Gluesenkamp Perez 42. (as_of: 2026-07-13; source: https://www.nwprogressive.org/weblog/2026/07/wa-03-is-still-washingtons-tossup-district-marie-gluesenkamp-perez-and-john-braun-are-tied-ahead-of-2026-top-two-election.html)
- Campaign finance as of July 15, 2026: Gluesenkamp Perez raised $6.29M with $2.06M cash on hand; Braun raised $2.05M with $1.12M on hand. (as_of: 2026-07-15; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Washington)
- DHM Research for OPB, Sep 17–27, 543 LV, MoE ±4.2: Braun 46, Gluesenkamp Perez 44, 11% unsure. With leaners: Braun 48, Gluesenkamp Perez 47. Trump has endorsed Braun. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/10/01/gluesenkamp-perez-braun-southwest-washington-3rd-district/)
- OPB reported on Oct 1 that Cook rated WA-03 a Toss-up. (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/10/01/gluesenkamp-perez-braun-southwest-washington-3rd-district/)
- Wikipedia lists Cook at Lean D as of Oct 9. Other ratings: Split Ticket Likely D (Jul 28), DDHQ Likely D (Jul 14). Inside Elections Toss-up and Sabato Lean D date from 2025. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Washington)

## Resolution notes

- All three are party markets. HOUSEPA7-26-D: "If the House member sworn in for PA-07 for the term beginning in 2027 is a member of the Democratic party, then the market resolves to Yes." PA-08 and WA-03 use the same wording, and the R side is worded the same way for Republicans. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/HOUSEPA7-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- All three can be settled early once a consensus of media organizations projects the winner. Expected expiration is 2027-01-04, and the settlement source is congress.gov. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/HOUSEWA3-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Under Washington's top-two system, only Gluesenkamp Perez and Braun advanced to the November ballot. (as_of: 2026-08-04; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Washington)
