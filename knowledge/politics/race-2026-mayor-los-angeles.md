# Race: 2026 Los Angeles Mayor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Nonpartisan Los Angeles mayoral runoff on November 3, 2026: incumbent Karen Bass vs City Councilmember Nithya Raman.

## Claims

- The June 2, 2026 nonpartisan primary sends the top two to a November 3 runoff when no candidate tops 50%. (as_of: 2026-06-08; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/nithya-raman-advances-spencer-pratt-face-l-mayor-karen-bass-runoff-rcna347483)
- Certified primary: Bass 34.3% (292,593), Raman 29.0% (247,781), Spencer Pratt 25.5% (217,977), Adam Miller 3.5%; 853,892 total votes. (as_of: 2026-06-26; source: https://results.lavote.gov/#year=2026&election=4338)
- Pratt, a former reality TV star and registered Republican who ran on Bass's wildfire response, was second on election night, but Raman passed him as late ballots were counted. (as_of: 2026-06-08; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/nithya-raman-advances-spencer-pratt-face-l-mayor-karen-bass-runoff-rcna347483)
- Raman is a member of the Democratic Socialists of America and is challenging Bass from the left; both are Democrats. (as_of: 2026-06-08; source: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/nithya-raman-advances-spencer-pratt-face-l-mayor-karen-bass-runoff-rcna347483)
- Berkeley IGS pre-primary, May 19–24, 1,913 RV: Raman 32, Bass 28, Pratt 25. Hypothetical runoffs were Bass 47–22 over Pratt and Raman 45–28 over Pratt. (as_of: 2026-06-02; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Los_Angeles_mayoral_election)
- A purported Median Strategies poll showing Bass up 12 was withdrawn as fake; the firm said on Aug 17 it was a "short term social experiment" and shut down. (as_of: 2026-08-17; source: https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-08-17/poll-showing-bass-leading-raman-by-double-digits-was-bogus-company-says)
- Around the fake poll, Kalshi's Bass contract rose from 64.3¢ before release to a 67.2¢ peak, and Polymarket's from about 61¢ to 70¢ on Aug 17. (as_of: 2026-08-19; source: https://prospect.org/2026/08/19/fake-poll-scandal-pump-and-dump-scheme-prediction-markets-los-angeles-mayor/)
- Tulchin Research (D), August, 1,200 LV: Bass 41, Raman 35, 24% undecided. (as_of: 2026-09-19; source: https://www.latimes.com/california/newsletter/2026-09-19/los-angeles-mayoral-ad-blitz-hollywood-ice-homelessness)
- Tavern Research (D), Aug 20–25, 2,258 LV: Raman 38, Bass 32, 30% undecided (53–47 Raman when pushed). (as_of: 2026-09-21; source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QVDxRxC0v8ZuoJuC3g_Z6xfI6J19PopY/view)
- Berkeley IGS/LA Times, Sep 15–20, 2,150 LV: Raman 39, Bass 28, 33% undecided. (as_of: 2026-09-23; source: https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-09-23/raman-takes-big-lead-over-bass-in-latest-poll-for-la-mayors-race)
- Late-September filings: hedge fund manager Aaron Sosnick gave $4M ($3M to Raman's main committee) and the Western States Regional Council of Carpenters $3M for Raman. Airbnb has put over $4M behind Bass and other candidates. (as_of: 2026-09-25; source: https://laist.com/news/politics/la-mayor-race-bass-raman-donors-campaign-finance-sosnick-airbnb)
- The LAPD officers' union has spent nearly $3M, over $2M of it against Raman; ballots begin arriving the week of Oct 5. (as_of: 2026-09-25; source: https://laist.com/news/politics/la-mayor-race-bass-raman-donors-campaign-finance-sosnick-airbnb)
- Kalshi KXMAYORLA-26: Raman 56¢ bid / 57¢ ask; Bass 42¢ / 43¢ (open interest on the Bass contract about 7.1M). (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXMAYORLA-26?with_nested_markets=true)
