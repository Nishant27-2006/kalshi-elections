# Policy: Trump Pardons and Political Prosecutions

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Scope: dated second-term clemency grants (from the DOJ Pardon Attorney list) and dated federal charges against Trump adversaries, plus pricing for the pardon, charge and arrest markets.

## Claims

- About 1,500 January 6 defendants received clemency on Jan. 20, 2025. (as_of: 2025-01-20; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_people_granted_executive_clemency_in_the_second_Trump_presidency)
- Ross Ulbricht was pardoned on Jan. 21, 2025. (as_of: 2025-01-21; source: https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-grants-president-donald-j-trump-2025-present)
- Rod Blagojevich was pardoned on Feb. 10, 2025. (as_of: 2025-02-10; source: https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-grants-president-donald-j-trump-2025-present)
- Six pardons went to BitMEX-linked defendants, including Benjamin Delo, on Mar. 27, 2025. (as_of: 2025-03-27; source: https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-grants-president-donald-j-trump-2025-present)
- A Justice Department criminal investigation into former CIA Director John Brennan was reported (Axios) to be ongoing in September 2025. (as_of: 2025-09-30; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_O._Brennan)
- James Comey was indicted in September 2025 on one false-statements count and one obstruction count. A federal judge dismissed the case on Nov. 24, 2025. (as_of: 2025-11-24; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Comey)
- New York AG Letitia James was indicted on Oct. 9, 2025 on one count of bank fraud and one count of false statements to a financial institution. (as_of: 2025-10-09; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letitia_James)
- George Santos's sentence was commuted on Oct. 17, 2025. (as_of: 2025-10-17; source: https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-grants-president-donald-j-trump-2025-present)
- Binance founder Changpeng Zhao was pardoned on Oct. 21, 2025. (as_of: 2025-10-21; source: https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-grants-president-donald-j-trump-2025-present)
- Judge Cameron McGowan Currie dismissed the Letitia James indictment on Nov. 24, 2025, ruling that its sole signatory, Lindsey Halligan, had been unlawfully appointed. Grand juries declined to re-indict on Dec. 4 and Dec. 11, 2025. (as_of: 2025-12-11; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letitia_James)
- Former Honduran President Juan Orlando Hernández was pardoned on Dec. 1, 2025. (as_of: 2025-12-01; source: https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-grants-president-donald-j-trump-2025-present)
- Rep. Henry (Enrique Roberto) Cuellar was among three people pardoned on Dec. 2, 2025. (as_of: 2025-12-02; source: https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-grants-president-donald-j-trump-2025-present)
- Comey was indicted again and surrendered in April 2026, over a briefly posted Instagram post alleged to be a veiled call for Trump's assassination. (as_of: 2026-04-30; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Comey)
- The DOJ Pardon Attorney list shows no grants dated between Jan. 20, 2026 and Sep. 3, 2026. The Sep. 3, 2026 batch had 23 pardons and 6 commutations, including ex-union boss John Dougherty, and a single pardon followed on Sep. 8, 2026. (as_of: 2026-09-08; source: https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-grants-president-donald-j-trump-2025-present)

## Resolution notes

- Pardon markets count a presidential pardon, commutation or reprieve during Trump's second term before the deadline. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXTRUMPPARDONS-29JAN21?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXFEDERALCHARGE needs a new complaint, information or indictment filed after issuance. Reinstated pre-issuance charges and charges still sealed at the deadline do not count. Charges later dismissed still count. Military-court charges count. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXFEDERALCHARGE-27JAN01?with_nested_markets=true)
- KXARREST counts voluntary surrender to law enforcement under an indictment, even without an arrest warrant (clarified 9/25/25). (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXARREST-27JAN?with_nested_markets=true)
