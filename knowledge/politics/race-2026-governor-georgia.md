# Race: 2026 Georgia Governor

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Open-seat Georgia governor race (Kemp term-limited): Republican Rick Jackson vs Democrat Keisha Lance Bottoms, November 3, 2026.

## Claims

- Incumbent Brian Kemp (R) is term-limited; primaries were held May 19, 2026 with a June 16 primary runoff. (as_of: 2026-06-16; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Georgia_gubernatorial_election)
- Businessman Rick Jackson won the GOP runoff over Lt. Gov. Burt Jones, whom Trump and Kemp had endorsed; Jackson's campaign was mostly self-funded. (as_of: 2026-06-16; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Georgia_gubernatorial_election)
- Former Atlanta Mayor Keisha Lance Bottoms won the Democratic nomination without a runoff against six opponents. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Georgia_gubernatorial_election)
- Democratic primary turnout exceeded Republican turnout for the first time since 2006. (as_of: 2026-05-19; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Georgia_gubernatorial_election)
- 2022 result: Kemp (R) 53.4%, Stacey Abrams (D) 45.9%, about R+7.5. (as_of: 2022-11-08; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- 2024 presidential: Trump carried Georgia by 2.19 points (Harris 48.53%). (as_of: 2024-11-05; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election)
- Cook and Inside Elections both rate the race a Tossup (Oct 1, 2026 updates). (as_of: 2026-10-01; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- Sabato rates it a Tossup (Oct 6, 2026 table); Silver Bulletin had Lean D (flip) as of Oct 3. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections)
- YouGov, Sep 15–18, 3,447 RV, ±2.7: Jackson 45, Bottoms 44, 9% undecided; the same poll had Ossoff 51, Collins 41 for Senate. (as_of: 2026-09-23; source: https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2026/09/23/new-poll-ossoff-holds-double-digit-lead-over-collins-governors-race-dead-heat/)
