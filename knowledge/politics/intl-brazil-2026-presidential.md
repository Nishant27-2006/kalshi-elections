# International: Brazil 2026 Presidential Election

Back to [[Politics Knowledge Index]]

Covers the Lula vs. Flávio Bolsonaro race: first-round result, runoff date, polls before and after round one, endorsements, and Kalshi pricing.

## Claims

- In Datafolha's July 22-24 runoff scenario (2,004 interviews, ±2 pts), Lula led Flávio Bolsonaro 48% to 43%. (as_of: 2026-07-24; source: https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/politica/eleicoes/2026/07/24/pesquisa-datafolha-de-presidente-com-lula-e-flavio-bolsonaro.html)
- AtlasIntel's mid-September runoff scenario had Flávio Bolsonaro at 47.2% and Lula at 46.8%, a statistical tie. (as_of: 2026-09-17; source: https://gauchazh.clicrbs.com.br/politica/eleicoes/noticia/2026/09/pesquisa-atlasintel-mostra-flavio-bolsonaro-com-472-e-lula-com-468-no-segundo-turno-cmu5e5gw201aa014x2v4ff12s.html)
- Datafolha's last first-round poll (4,006 interviews on Oct 2-3, ±2 pts) put Lula at 45% of valid votes and Flávio at 42%, with Caiado 4%, Cury 3%, Renan Santos 3% and Zema 1%. (as_of: 2026-10-03; source: https://sbtnews.sbt.com.br/noticia/eleicoes/datafolha-no-1-turno-lula-tem-45-de-validos-e-flavio-42)
- Quaest's last first-round poll, released Oct 3 (fieldwork Oct 2-3, 3,702 voters), had Lula at 46% of valid votes and Flávio at 45%. (as_of: 2026-10-04; source: https://veja.abril.com.br/brasil/lula-x-flavio-o-que-cinco-institutos-disseram-em-suas-ultimas-pesquisas/)
- The first round was held Oct 4, 2026. With 98.62% of ballot boxes counted, Flávio Bolsonaro (PL) had 47.05% of valid votes and Lula (PT) 45.13%, followed by Augusto Cury 2.92%, Renan Santos 2.28% and Ronaldo Caiado 2.22%. (as_of: 2026-10-04; source: https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/122884/flavio-e-lula-vao-ao-segundo-turno-da-eleicao-presidencial)
- No candidate won a majority of valid votes, so the top two meet in a runoff on Oct 25, 2026. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://en.mercopress.com/2026/10/05/flavio-bolsonaro-edges-lula-in-the-first-round-both-head-to-an-october-25-runoff)
- Reports described Flávio Bolsonaro as beating most private polls, with 12 candidates on the first-round ballot. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://www.emirates247.com/world/flavio-bolsonaro-outperforms-polls-advances-to-brazil-presidential-runoff-against-lula/6341)
- Flávio Bolsonaro led the count from start to finish and thanked "some 56 million voters." Lula said he had been "convinced" he would win. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://en.mercopress.com/2026/10/05/flavio-bolsonaro-edges-lula-in-the-first-round-both-head-to-an-october-25-runoff)
- Ronaldo Caiado (PSD) and Romeu Zema (Novo) endorsed Flávio for the runoff. Augusto Cury (Avante) and Renan Santos (Missão) initially stayed neutral. (as_of: 2026-10-05; source: https://noticias.r7.com/eleicoes/2026/caiado-e-zema-declaram-apoio-a-flavio-no-2-turno-cury-e-renan-evitam-escolher-lado-05102026/)
- PP and União Brasil announced support for Flávio Bolsonaro in the runoff. (as_of: 2026-10-06; source: https://g1.globo.com/politica/eleicoes/2026/noticia/2026/10/06/pp-e-uniao-brasil-anunciam-apoio-a-flavio-bolsonaro-no-segundo-turno.ghtml)
- Third-placed Augusto Cury was reported set to endorse Flávio Bolsonaro for the runoff. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/10/08/augusto-cury-deve-declarar-apoio-a-flavio-bolsonaro-no-segundo-turno.ghtml)
- In PoderData's first runoff poll (3,000 phone interviews on Oct 5-7, ±1.8 pts), Flávio led Lula 49% to 44% on total votes and 53% to 47% on valid votes. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://revistaforum.com.br/politica/primeira-pesquisa-2-turno-lula-flavio-bolsonaro/)
- In Datafolha's first runoff poll (2,520 in-person interviews on Oct 6-7, ±2 pts), Flávio led Lula 49% to 45% on total votes and 52% to 48% on valid votes. (as_of: 2026-10-08; source: https://www.diariodepernambuco.com.br/politica/2026/10/11726197-datafolha-flavio-bolsonaro-tem-52-dos-votos-validos-e-lula-48.html)
- Quaest's first runoff poll was scheduled for release on Monday, Oct 12 (TSE registration BR-06799/2026). (as_of: 2026-10-07; source: https://exame.com/brasil/quando-sai-a-1a-pesquisa-quaest-do-segundo-turno/)
- On Kalshi, KXBRPRES-26-FBOL (Flávio Bolsonaro) was bid 0.87 / ask 0.88 (last 0.88, volume about 2.54M). KXBRPRES-26-LULA was bid 0.12 / ask 0.13 (last 0.13, volume about 2.69M). (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXBRPRES-26?with_nested_markets=true)
- Kalshi finalized every other KXBRPRES-26 strike as No on Oct 5, 2026 (UTC), including Tarcísio de Freitas, Michelle Bolsonaro, Caiado, Zema, Cury and Renan Santos. (as_of: 2026-10-09; source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXBRPRES-26?with_nested_markets=true)
